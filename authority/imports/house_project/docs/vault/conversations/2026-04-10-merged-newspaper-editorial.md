# Newspaper Editorial System — Full-Stack Upgrade

## Summary

Four sessions on April 10 brought O Grafo Diario from a broken scaffold to a fully operational editorial platform. The work upgraded the editor agent pipeline (Gemini 2.5 Pro, publications archive, dynamic date selector), rewrote the editorial voice using the Business Philosopher persona with a simplified single-body schema replacing the 3-tier content structure, migrated all 23 gen templates to the new schema with a formalized payload injection contract (v1.3.0), and connected the thesis layer by placing "Reasoning is navigation" in the phrasing backlog and creating a PhD viability discovery document.
