# Axiom-to-Code Derivation Chains -- Complete Mapping

## Summary

A multi-agent robot-talk investigation mapped all 14 project axioms (7 ontology, 2 business, 5 system) from abstract definitions through full derivation chains (axiom to premise to constitution to skill to code enforcement). It revealed an enforcement gradient: system axioms are 100% enforced, business 50%, and ontology only 29% (mostly skill-guided). The investigation identified 6 implementation gaps totaling ~15 hours of work and produced permanent reference artifacts in `/specs/ontology/docs/abstract-concrete/`.
