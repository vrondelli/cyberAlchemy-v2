## Shared evaluation frame

The three attacks converge on a six-part test that must not be collapsed:

1. **Referent:** does a repository object, practice, distinction, producer, or consumer exist?
2. **Need for rule:** does that referent require a shared normative meaning, a local rule, or only ordinary prose?
3. **Definition quality:** does the current wording discriminate the concept without circularity, contradiction, or ornamental notation?
4. **Adoption:** has an owner adopted the rule, or is it candidate/documentary?
5. **Enforcement:** is it unenforced, review-enforced, hybrid, or deterministically checked?
6. **Conformance:** do current instances actually satisfy it?

A missing validator does not erase a legitimate normative rule. Conversely, a real referent or an implemented field does not prove that CAV2 owns its generic definition or that current instances conform.

## Synthesis discipline

The classification in `findings.md` uses `KEEP` only where the existing text is substantially sound. Every other card is `FIX`, even when its referent is real. A `FIX` action states where the concept goes; it is not a claim that the referent is nonexistent.
