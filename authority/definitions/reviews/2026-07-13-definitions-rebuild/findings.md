## Decision rule

`KEEP` requires both a discriminating definition and concrete repository dependence. `REWRITE` requires a concretely used distinction whose present definition is unsound, overbroad, or out of sync. `RETIRE` means the spine should not define it now: common English use, citations, a candidate/template, or a future validator are insufficient. Retirement does not prohibit local prose or a local schema field.

## Exact minimal wording target for surviving rewrites

These are change requests, not direct mutations of `DEFINITIONS.md`.

| ID | Minimal normative wording |
|---|---|
| D18 | **Residue:** a recorded unresolved output from a named operation that states what remains, the evidence for it, its current owner, and its next treatment or route. A residue record does not itself prove failure, authority, or promotion. |
| D34 | **Authority:** repository-recognized permission for a named owner to perform a named operation on a named object within a stated scope and to produce a stated effect, based on a cited governing rule. No authority is implied outside those stated bounds. |
| D35 | **Provenance:** a resolvable chain of source, transformation, and decision records sufficient to explain where a governed claim or artifact came from. Provenance records origin and derivation; it does not establish truth or authority. |
| D40 | **Challenge contract:** a structured protocol stating admissible challenge modes, the deciding owner, the resolution gate, the effect while unresolved, and where challenge residue is recorded. `challenge_route` must reference or project this contract, not replace it with free text. |
| D49 | **Closed authority-kind enumeration:** in review mode, any authority kind not listed in `AUTHORITY-MODEL.md` is denied governing effect until the owner gate admits it. This rule does not claim deterministic enforcement or imply a promotion lifecycle. |
