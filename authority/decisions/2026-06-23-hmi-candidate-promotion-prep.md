## Consequential Work

This decision resolves the blockers that prevented
`synergetic cybernetic human-machine integration` from being prepared for a
future CyberAlchemy v2 definition promotion route.

It does not promote the term into
[DEFINITIONS.md](../definitions/DEFINITIONS.md). Canonical promotion still
requires an explicit definitions-governance update, index sync, and drift audit.

## Decision 1: Scope

Selected option: CAV2 product/philosophy proposition candidate.

Rejected options:

- Method specialization of [CAV2-D32 method](../definitions/DEFINITIONS.md#cav2-d32-method).
- T-1 primordial primitive.
- Public marketing phrase only.

Rationale: the candidate currently explains CyberAlchemy's value proposition
and product direction. It constrains future methods and proof surfaces, but it
does not yet define a repeatable method record with declared inputs, actions,
roles, gates, outputs, validation, and recomposition. It should stay in T5
Research And Product Towers until a later owner route decides whether to
promote it as a definition, specialize it as a method, or keep it as product
language.

Downstream effect: any canonical draft should define a proposition or
integration posture, not a runtime method.

## Decision 2: Canonical Candidate Spelling

Selected option: preserve `synergetic cybernetic human-machine integration` as
the internal candidate term.

Aliases:

- synergistic cybernetic human-machine integration
- governed human-machine integration
- CyberAlchemy human-machine integration
- governed human-machine integration for complex knowledge work

Rationale: `synergetic` preserves the user's source phrasing and better fits
the cybernetic/system-level thesis than the more generic marketing adjective
`synergistic`. `Synergistic` remains a useful alias for readers and search.

Downstream effect: canonical promotion should keep `synergetic` as the primary
candidate spelling unless public-boundary review intentionally selects a
different outward headline.

## Decision 3: Proof Surface

Selected option: Connective + IDE Ergonomics fixture package.

Minimum proof for canonical definition promotion:

1. The candidate source cluster and value proposition remain traceable.
2. IDE Ergonomics shows the route-card and integration-surface pattern.
3. Connective supplies the flagship proof surface.
4. At least one static or dry-run fixture demonstrates:
   - Craft state export;
   - opt-in human indicator snapshot;
   - user-executed test evidence;
   - domain priority lens;
   - ranked lane recommendation;
   - explicit user choice;
   - receipt or handoff target.
5. The proof keeps human indicators opt-in and reviewable.

Public product proof requires more than the definition proof: a boundary-scrubbed
demo, fixture, or product brief that does not rely on private parent paths or
private implementation evidence.

Downstream effect: the term can be prepared for definition drafting now, but
the stronger product claim should wait for the Connective fixture package.

## Decision 4: Public-Safe Wording

Selected public-safe short form:

> CyberAlchemy creates governed human-machine integration for complex knowledge
> work.

Selected public-safe long form:

> CyberAlchemy brings agent workflows, knowledge systems, software tools, and
> interface surfaces into reviewable loops where people stay in control of
> decisions, evidence, tests, and handoffs.

Internal thesis phrase:

> Synergetic cybernetic human-machine integration.

Avoid in public default copy:

- claims that machines replace human judgment;
- claims of autonomous decision closure;
- medical, psychological, or productivity-surveillance framing;
- private repository names, private parent paths, or private implementation
  proof;
- "AI does everything" language that hides owner routes, evidence, or consent.

Downstream effect: local CAV2 artifacts may preserve the technical thesis
phrase; public-facing material should lead with governed human-machine
integration unless the audience expects cybernetic vocabulary.

- public-safe wording.

Remaining promotion blockers:

Next step: either draft a canonical CAV2 definition under definitions-governance
or create the Connective fixture package that proves the term before promotion.
