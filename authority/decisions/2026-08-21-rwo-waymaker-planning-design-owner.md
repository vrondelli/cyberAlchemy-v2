## Required Owner Return Contract

The source response must include a manifest row for every individual contract,
profile, schema, vector, and fixture proposed as part of the authoritative
Waymaker corpus. Each row must state:

- artifact identifier and kind;
- an immutable locator in the form `repo@<full-commit>:path`;
- the artifact's SHA-256;
- its role in the corpus;
- its relationship to `WAY-MAKER.md`;
- its owner-adopted status, version, and decision reference;
- any supersession relation; and
- the exact downstream reference surface permitted for that artifact.

For this return, the owner-adopted status vocabulary is:

- `candidate`: proposed but not owner-reviewed or accepted;
- `reviewed`: examined by the owner without acceptance;
- `accepted`: normative content accepted by the owner, but not necessarily
  locked as an immutable downstream baseline;
- `frozen`: an accepted exact version locked as the downstream baseline; and
- `superseded`: replaced by an identified successor.

`accepted` and `frozen` are distinct states. A return that accepts content does
not freeze it unless the owner explicitly records `frozen`, the exact version,
and the governing decision reference.
