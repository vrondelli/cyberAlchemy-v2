## Decision

`/home/vrondelli/projects/resonantos-economy-research` as the working research

The initial CyberAlchemy v2 tower

## Rationale

The economy question needs failed-DAO precedent, science-of-science,
formal-mechanism research, behavioral economics, authority design, and validation
witnesses. Keeping that as a standalone repository prevents CAV2-local product
authority from being confused with ResonantDAO economy policy.

## Authority Granted

CyberAlchemy v2 grants only method authority:

- source posture and claim-kind discipline;
- promotion gates;
- non-collapse between evidence, ontology, authority, and implementation;
- public/private boundary caution;
- research-before-specification posture.

CyberAlchemy v2 does not grant:

- token policy authority;
- bounty policy authority;
- governance weights;
- payout or treasury rules;
- Phase 2 launch readiness;
- proof that any proposed mechanism is safe.

## Result

Decision gate result: PASS.

Downstream effect: CAV2 may cite the standalone repository as a governed research
surface. Any finding that wants to become CAV2 authority must still pass the
