Follow repository routing first; these instructions do not select a route or
expand its authorization. For new or revised ARE research plans, hypotheses,
experiments and handoffs in this tree, read [CONTINUITY.md](CONTINUITY.md), the
[project lighthouse](../LIGHTHOUSE.md) and the
[current plan](tool-state-audit-v1/PLAN.md) before proposing the next round.

Carry the required objective/method/decision bindings into each successor.
Complete the continuity review before finalizing a plan or freezing a run.
Bind [continuity-policy-v1.json](continuity-policy-v1.json) by exact digest and
pass `verifyResearchContinuity` from the evolution runtime in `freeze` phase.
Every selected and alternative hypothesis must cite the round continuity binding.
Do not replace the initial ARE/GLM objective with benchmark completion, or restore
another benchmark without a new user-directed scope decision. No document check
alone certifies scientific validity or authorizes execution.
