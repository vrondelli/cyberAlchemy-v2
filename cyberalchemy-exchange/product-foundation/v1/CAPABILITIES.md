- The host lifecycle report records local tests; it does not prove public deployment.

- The purchase preparation records eight SBF tests, eight native tests, seven client tests and seven deterministic artifacts on September 8. No successor deployment is recorded there.
- Older README plans may say authentication, real-engine verification or exact deployment IDs are still future work. The later source records narrow or supersede those statements; they do not erase other limits.
- Generic durable-storage definitions and their concrete consumer implementations have different maturity. A folder-level status is not the status of every capability within it.
