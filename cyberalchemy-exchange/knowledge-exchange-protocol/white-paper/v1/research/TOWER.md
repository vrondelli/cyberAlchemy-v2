# Research Tower

Target: an evidence-bounded technical white paper for the CyberAlchemy
Knowledge Expansion Protocol

## One-sentence model

KEP coordinates a public, replay-safe expansion from `{A}` to `{A,B}` for one
immutable knowledge-artifact version while keeping plaintext and secret keys
client-held and treating recipient access as an encrypted, separately verified
delivery obligation.

## Compositional spine

```text
meaning -> identity -> encrypted material -> durable preparation
  -> public co-owner transition -> finalized reconciliation
  -> recipient decryption -> reissue without ownership change
```

## Borrow carefully

- Problem-first and verification-limits exposition from Bitcoin.
- State-transition notation from Ethereum.
- Component and assumption separation from Solana.
- Content-addressed immutable identity from IPFS.
- Chain/data/provider/client layer separation from Ocean.
- Knowledge-asset provenance framing from OriginTrail.

## Block

- Exclusive-transfer language for copyable knowledge.
- Equating on-chain membership with plaintext possession or legal title.
- Treating test PASS as security, production, market, or governance proof.
- Treating logical SQL layers as independent backup failure domains.

## Promotion boundary

This tower informs paper prose only. It does not promote external terminology
into KEP definitions or change any admitted artifact.
