# L1 — Residue Map

- Novelty: related peer-reviewed cryptographic and decentralized-identity work
  is not yet surveyed.
- Security: no independent audit, fuzzing report, side-channel analysis,
  dependency review, or production key-custody assessment.
- Durability: no physically independent off-host restore evidence.
- External trial: no fresh hosted third-party Devnet transaction joins browser,
  API, PostgreSQL, program, and recipient decryption in one receipt.
- Governance: definitions, Product Capability, business ontology, DomainSpec,
  system ontology, and the exact implementation binding completed their owner
  decisions, but post-acceptance conformance is blocked because current bytes
  no longer match the accepted binding.
- Product: no user research, usability study, willingness-to-pay evidence, or
  market sizing.
- Legal: protocol co-ownership has no accepted mapping to copyright, licenses,
  database rights, or jurisdictional rules.
