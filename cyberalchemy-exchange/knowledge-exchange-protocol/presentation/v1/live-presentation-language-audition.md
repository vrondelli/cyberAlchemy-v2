## Opening

### Projected Copy

If I teach you something, I still know it.

### Spoken Copy

That sounds obvious. But it changes the whole model. If I send you a coin, I
do not have that coin anymore. If I show you how something works, now we both
know it. Knowledge does not move the way money moves.

## Tension

### Projected Copy

How do you share a trade secret without making it public?

### Spoken Copy

Imagine a company needs to share a confidential formula with one trusted
partner. It needs a record of which formula was shared, who approved it, and
who received it. The partner must be able to open the exact file. But the
formula itself cannot appear in the public record.

## Reveal

### Projected Copy

The origin stays. Access grows.

### Spoken Copy

KEP handles the exchange this way. The company keeps the original formula, and
the partner receives a protected copy it can open. Solana records that the
sharing was authorized, but the formula itself never goes on-chain. It stays
encrypted, and only the people with the right keys can read it.
