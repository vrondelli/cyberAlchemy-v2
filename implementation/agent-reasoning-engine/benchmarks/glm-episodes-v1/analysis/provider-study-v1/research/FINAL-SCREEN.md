# Final fixed-study screen

The [frozen protocol](../frozen/protocol.json) remains authoritative for this experiment: 24 complete matched blocks, at least three net initial-success wins, and zero semantic losses for metadata/host-envelope treatments. Boundary guidance is compared with the calibrated neutral control under its original rule. This is exploratory prioritization across four known variants, not held-out product qualification.

The existing `analyze.mjs` emits partial-cohort observations and conservatively counts unobserved semantics. Keep those historical snapshots. Final screening uses `final-screen.mjs` and the separate `raw-source-audit.mjs`: every attributable raw replacement source is tested even if its original output contract was rejected. Host-envelope's raw full-schema rejection is expected; native contract validity and normalized validity remain separate measurements.

This closes a measurement gap, not a threshold change. In retained block 3, baseline source passed 6/6 despite a rejected schema; host-envelope source passed 4/6 despite a valid native contract. Treating the rejected baseline as having no semantic result would hide that regression. The final zero-loss guard must count it. Original initial outcomes are never changed by supplemental tests, and the hidden oracle never feeds model correction.

A complete comparison missing its net-win threshold or exceeding its allowed semantic losses is counterevidence. Enough wins with unresolved potential semantic losses remain inconclusive. Incomplete comparisons cannot select a candidate. Reports retain all unknowns, per-pair results, physical receipts and source hashes. No controller, frozen candidate, fixtures or policy thresholds are edited.
