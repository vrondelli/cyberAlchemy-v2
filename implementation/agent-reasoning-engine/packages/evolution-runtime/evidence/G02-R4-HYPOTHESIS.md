# G02 large-window quality recovery — development hypothesis

Status: research candidate, **not** a frozen r4 campaign or a superiority claim. G02 r3 remains immutable; its 12 attempts and nine valid paired scores are recorded in [G02-R3-RESULT-NOTE.md](G02-R3-RESULT-NOTE.md). The conditional Python AST span diagnostic below was also rejected after a separate exposed-cohort check.

The r3 ARE candidate used fewer known model tokens on all four scored repository strata, but its mean native Span-F1 difference was negative in openlibrary (two valid pairs, −0.14897), navidrome (one valid pair, −0.23060), and nearly flat in element-web (three valid pairs, −0.00439). It was positive in qutebrowser (three valid pairs, +0.07093). These are tiny diagnostic strata, not independent effect estimates. Three DSH controls hit the frozen 1M-token cap and remain failures, not silently removed observations.

On the exposed openlibrary `f6521a2a` task, ARE and DSH predicted the **same three files**, but ARE's native span coverage was only about 0.224 (precision 0.852) across a five-file gold set. The private receipts are `/opt/are-benchmark-runs/g02-cross-layer-pr3-open-f6521a2a/evidence/run/{prediction.json,native-score.json}` and `/opt/are-benchmark-runs/g02-cross-layer-pr3-open-f6521a2a/dsh-export/{prediction.json,native-score.json}`. This points to a plausible recall/localization problem rather than simple file misidentification on that task. It does not prove the cause of the aggregate G02 loss. The prior small positive NodeBB/Flipt holdout does not override the newer negative cross-repository r3 result.

The pinned r3 `cross-layer-source-hints.mjs` in `/opt/are/evolution-runtime/g02-product-replication-tools-r2/` only admits JavaScript/TypeScript or Go source extensions; it has no Python relationship expansion. That is a concrete mechanism gap for openlibrary and qutebrowser, though the data do not yet show that filling it improves scores.

Hypothesis H-G02-R4: a bounded second-pass *source-only* expansion from initially localized files to nearby callers, implementations, tests, and line ranges—including a language-general or Python-specific path—will improve Span-F1 and File-F1 across repositories while retaining a material token advantage over DSH. A candidate must be based on general source relationships and task text, never native gold labels or per-task answer edits. Develop it on exposed r3 tasks with repository-held-out diagnostics, preserve negative cases, then freeze a new unexposed multi-repository cohort with matched arm budgets and complete failure accounting. A higher DSH cap is a separate protocol dimension, not an r3 repair.

Proposed falsification check before a new holdout: if the source-only expansion cannot increase recall on exposed openlibrary/navidrome cases without large precision loss or costs approaching DSH, reject this mechanism. Do not advance merely because one exposed task passes. A subsequent product claim still needs a prospective paired quality interval entirely above zero and a clean denominator under the frozen rule.
