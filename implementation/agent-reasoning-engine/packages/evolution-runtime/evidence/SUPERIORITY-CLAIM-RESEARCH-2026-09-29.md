R8's prospective evaluator-only positional screen remains necessary for its differently selected five-language cohort.

The claim discipline follows [FTC substantiation guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/ftc-policy-statement-regarding-advertising-substantiation) on possessing objective support *before* publishing a claim, [FTC comparative-advertising guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/statement-policy-regarding-comparative-advertising) on truthful, nondeceptive comparison, and [NIST AI RMF Measure guidance](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/) on documented test sets, conditions, uncertainty, limitations, and independent assessment. Statistical methods must fit the paired task design; [Dror et al.](https://aclanthology.org/P18-1128/) explain why NLP significance tests depend on task and measure. These are methodological inputs, not external certification of ARE. Public-benchmark exposure remains a limitation even with our own sequestered holdout; a fresh internal holdout avoids *our* adaptive reuse, not any unknown provider pretraining contamination.

The newer [NIST automated-evaluation practices, initial public draft](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.800-2.ipd.pdf) explicitly separate observations from inferences, call for advance statistical procedures and uncertainty, disclose evaluation versions/conditions/costs, and warn against generalizing beyond the measured setting. The [NIST statistical-models report](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.800-3.pdf) notes that a simple average plus standard error can produce invalid uncertainty and that higher benchmark scores do not necessarily predict performance on related tasks. For ARE, a paired, stratified interval addresses *within-cohort sampling uncertainty* under its assumptions; it does not by itself establish deployment-wide or cross-branch superiority.

## Claim ladder and decision rule

1. **Development observation:** Exposed reruns may identify a mechanism, regression, or efficiency tradeoff. They cannot increase the independent holdout denominator or support a prospective superiority claim.
2. **Audited cohort result:** A frozen, complete paired campaign may report its observed quality, cost, uncertainty, failures, and limits even when a gate fails. It supports only a cohort-specific descriptive statement.
3. **Metric-specific comparative claim:** If the frozen validity/integrity rule passes and that metric's prespecified paired interval clears its threshold, say only that metric, on that cohort, against that comparator. Do not imply other metrics passed.
4. **Branch quality-and-efficiency superiority:** Require every predeclared branch quality interval above zero, token and summed-arm-time ratio intervals below one, full attempt accounting, no unresolved score/label defect, and an independent receipt audit. The exact G01/G02/G03 protocol remains authoritative for each branch; this ladder does not revise a frozen gate.
5. **Cross-branch or product-wide superiority:** Three separate branch wins would still not prove all-workflow superiority. That broader language needs a separately defined target population, construct-validity argument, deployment-like external replication, and its own prospectively fixed aggregate decision rule. No current ARE campaign has that scope.

Vlad and Dave review the exact sanitized evidence card before any product-page publication. Passing a statistical gate is necessary for the corresponding headline under our protocol, not automatic approval to publish it.
