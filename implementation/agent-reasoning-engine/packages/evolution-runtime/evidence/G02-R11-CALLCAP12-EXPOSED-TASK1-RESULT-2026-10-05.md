The fixed 12-call cap must not advance to a fresh G02 cohort or a product claim. Future development should either instrument an explicit, receipt-bound stop-and-finalize transition that cannot be bypassed by voluntary selection, or prioritize source-observable retrieval and terminal evidence selection quality before another cap trial. Any superiority claim still requires a new valid matched cohort after candidate freeze.
