## Next gate

Stop repeating GLM attempts on these same exposed questions. First construct a source-only, role-aware retrieval and extraction contract that can (a) identify the exact entity and target page, (b) preserve complete UI groups such as menus and table rows, (c) separate observed source facts from answer inference, and (d) enforce exact-set and exact-destination decisions. Evaluate source-witness reach across **all 26 exposed questions** with a predeclared criterion, not a handful of cherry-picked corrections. Only if that screen passes should one frozen reader/scorer candidate be evaluated on a *new*, metadata-frozen reserve-development cohort with a prospective absolute and paired gate. The 140 confirmation IDs remain closed until a credible independent transfer succeeds. Native official V2 claims additionally require the fixed reader, judge, and latency protocol.
