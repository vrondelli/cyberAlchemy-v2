## External supply check

The [official LongMemEval repository](https://github.com/xiaowu0162/LongMemEval/blob/main/README.md) describes S, M, and oracle variants as 500-question files sharing the same question-ID schema. Its M/oracle variants therefore cannot simply be called an independent fresh question cohort after S exposure; exact ID overlap would need verification for any proposed subset. [LongMemEval-V2](https://github.com/xiaowu0162/LongMemEval-V2) is a distinct agent-trajectory memory task with an official backend interface and accuracy-latency evaluation, not a replication of the r6 conversational-memory result. Its [official dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/xiaowu0162/longmemeval-v2/blob/main/README.md) states Apache-2.0. [LoCoMo](https://github.com/snap-research/locomo) is another independent conversational-memory source, but its [official license](https://github.com/snap-research/locomo/blob/main/LICENSE.txt) is CC BY-NC 4.0; use in a commercial product-page evidence workflow needs separate rights review before relying on it publicly.

For a bounded V2 metadata check, the exact dataset revision `f152293e235517d504809563c833d7190b8c713b` supplied only `questions.jsonl`, `haystacks/lme_v2_small.json`, `SCHEMA.md`, and `checksums.sha256` to private WSL storage at `/home/vlad_/are-benchmark-archive/g03-lme-v2-feasibility-20261005/`. No question text, reference answer, evaluation function, or trajectory content was printed or used to select a cohort. The downloaded question and small-tier haystack SHA-256 values match the published checksum file: `0a3ae5ebea938c24d7800e1e0b0828e08ae1646f939a53853b2b8cdc08e292b7` and `9b5301defb23a088a5f06e45ff8d5f35e569d78305a66d492046a9fff9b46593`.

The official dataset tree reports `trajectories.jsonl` at **1,195,604,539 bytes** and the two screenshot archives at **3,354,163,660** and **2,562,302,847 bytes** (about 7.1 GB together). The approved VPS had about **1.05 GB** free on its benchmark filesystem during this check, while private WSL had about **845 GB** available. Thus a full V2 dataset cannot be staged in the VPS as-is. A future candidate should keep source preparation and cold evidence on WSL, send only bounded selected packets to the VPS GLM runner, and separately validate any image-capable model, multimodal parity, and provider cost before attempting a five-ability claim.

The pinned `trajectories.jsonl` was subsequently downloaded to that private WSL archive and verified against the published SHA-256 `363cec9a8e87aa8d9101ce4e600aadbf7031d674056ebe4f969e8424abc5f3c6`. This establishes image transport on the current endpoint, **not** benchmark visual comprehension, reliable image costs, or multimodal matched-arm parity.

The subsequent [two-pair development r1](G03-LME-V2-DEVELOPMENT-R1-2026-10-05.md) implemented paired, same-field 12K-character source packets and made four valid text-only GLM answer calls after four tokenizer admissions. Both control and treatment scored **0/2** under the pinned official deterministic rules. The exposed diagnosis separates clipped answer-bearing accessibility-tree lines from missing cross-item procedural counts; neither the r1 treatment nor its state-level comparator merits confirmation. The official harness was pinned for interface/scoring inspection, but this r1 pilot was **not** a native harness run or leaderboard result.

The later [r2–r6 development campaign](G03-LME-V2-DEVELOPMENT-R2-R6-2026-10-05.md) indexed source windows, made 56 paired answer attempts, and audited 64 arm-blind GLM-adapted judge attempts. Across all 28 exposed development questions, treatment was **6–8/28** versus control **3–4/28** because three judge arms split their two votes. Among 25 fully resolved pairs, three favored treatment and none favored control (exact two-sided McNemar p=0.25). These are custom GLM development observations, not a native official V2 result, confirmation, or product superiority.

The subsequent [r7–r8 site-scope study](G03-LME-V2-SITE-SCOPE-R7-R8-2026-10-05.md) removed 35 cross-site treatment windows, reran only ten changed development answers, and audited eight new blind judgments. Treatment rose to **7–9/28** against unchanged control **3–4/28**; 25 resolved pairs yielded exact two-sided McNemar p=0.125. The later [r9–r10 state/transition study](G03-LME-V2-STATE-TRANSITION-R9-R10-2026-10-05.md) indexed observed page states and action paths and scored **10/28** against the same inherited **3–4/28** control; 27 resolved pairs yielded exact two-sided McNemar p=0.0390625. A broad field-ranked r11 packet failed source-only evidence screening before provider calls. The narrowed [r12 form hybrid](G03-LME-V2-FORM-HYBRID-R11-R12-2026-10-05.md) preserved r9 elsewhere and recovered two deterministic cases, reaching **12/28** on the same exposed set; 27 resolved pairs yielded descriptive exact p=0.00390625. Absolute accuracy remains low, so none of these custom-reader development scores is a superiority result or confirmation gate.
