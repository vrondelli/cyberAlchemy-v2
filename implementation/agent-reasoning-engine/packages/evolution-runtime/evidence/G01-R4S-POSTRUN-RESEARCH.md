# G01 r4s post-run research and next claim boundary

Status: private research, 2026-09-29. [The frozen r4s protocol and terminal result](G01-R4S-PROTOCOL-CANDIDATE.md) remain unchanged. This is not a new cohort freeze or a product claim.

## What r4s established

The 24-pair attempt completed with 23 native-scored pairs and one candidate `frontier.source-digest` failure. On the valid pairs, the ARE-minus-control Span-F1 mean was +0.0718, but its repository-stratified 95% bootstrap interval was −0.0583 to +0.1923. The File-F1 difference and known-token/arm-time ratios favored ARE. The predeclared headline gate failed both completeness and Span-F1 uncertainty; an integrity-passing audit does not reverse those failures.

The failed pair's leading UTF-8 BOM was dropped by the frozen source tool's decoder while the recorded digest covered original bytes. Local prospective source-tool code now retains the BOM and has a regression test. Never patch the frozen VPS source snapshot, rescore the failed pair, or top up the r4s denominator.

## Development signal, not a holdout claim

The 23 scored rows show mean Span-F1 deltas by repository: Clap +0.0587 (4), cli −0.0772 (7), jq +0.1910 (3), fd +0.2019 (3), and Svelte +0.1296 (6). Two cli tasks contributed large negative deltas (−1.0000 and −0.6113). These are *exposed diagnostic cases* suitable for error analysis and hypothesis development, not for selecting a favorable future test cohort or claiming a repository-specific win. A quality successor needs a prospective hypothesis that improves the missed spans while preserving efficiency; the BOM repair alone is a reliability fix.

The first cli regression (`go-cli-2651398f-04`, Span-F1 1.0 control versus 0.0 candidate) is a concrete terminal-shortlist omission. A read-only scan of all 24 r4s candidate event logs found exactly one qualifying `go.mod` read of at least eight lines, this case, and its read ID was absent from the eligible shortlist. A prospective local predicate now admits exact `go.mod` basenames while retaining the test/fixture and minimum-width exclusions. That makes the right source selectable; whether it is selected and scored must be measured separately.

## Evidence needed for a defensible superiority statement

Keep a claim tied to one task population, comparator, model, window, metric, date, and protocol. Freeze a fresh nonadaptive matched-arm holdout after an exposed-case development phase. Preserve all attempts, pre-score seals, native scores, receipt hashes, failures, and independent audit. Use the declared 24/24 completeness and repository-stratified quality/efficiency interval gate unless a separately approved protocol changes it *before dispatch*. A claim such as “ARE is superior overall” does not follow from a single branch or a post-hoc favorable subset. An exact bounded G03 accuracy card has a different claim ceiling and must not be generalized to G01 or G02.

This boundary also aligns with external substantiation guidance: the [FTC's advertising-substantiation policy](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/ftc-policy-statement-regarding-advertising-substantiation) expects objective claims to have a reasonable basis before publication; [NIST's blind-data evaluation practice](https://ai-challenges.nist.gov/aite) explains the contamination risk that a sequestered holdout addresses; and [CONAR's code](https://www.conar.org.br/pdf/Codigo-CONAR-2024.pdf) calls for identifiable research/statistical sources without partial-data distortion. These sources inform claim discipline; they do not certify ARE or determine the campaign's statistical gate. Vlad and Dave must review the exact sanitized card before any publication.
