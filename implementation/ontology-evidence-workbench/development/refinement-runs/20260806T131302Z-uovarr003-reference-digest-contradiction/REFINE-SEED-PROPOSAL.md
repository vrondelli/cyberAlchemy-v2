## Candidate models

1. **Validated embedded back-reference** — hash relation IDs and portal/path semantics, exclude only nested reference digest values, bind them to the final digest, and validate every back-reference on admission.

3. **Two-level identity** — references bind a stable revision/content digest while an envelope digest covers the complete serialized scene.

A relation-ID-only constituent with ambient scene scope is retained as a negative control unless it proves detached serialization and stale-scene rejection.
