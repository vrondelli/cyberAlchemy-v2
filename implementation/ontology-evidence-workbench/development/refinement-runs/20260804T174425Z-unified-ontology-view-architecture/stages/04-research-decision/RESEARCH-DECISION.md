- Decision: research required and completed within the confirmed bounded lane.
- Why required: graph-library capabilities, versions, accessibility hooks, and scale guidance are temporally unstable; local prototypes cannot establish the current tool seam.
- Scope: current primary documentation for React Flow, Cytoscape.js, Sigma/Graphology, ELK, and D3, compared only against locally derived OEW invariants.

- Result: provisional React Flow + ELK primary; Cytoscape + ELK retained as non-dominated fallback; Sigma/Graphology deferred as optional high-scale adapter; D3 rejected as complete workbench owner.
- Selection gate: no dependency is finally admitted until the source-owned Body War adapter spike passes exact 276-node/301-relation semantic, geometry, accessibility, restoration, and performance checks.
- Authority: research evidence only; local source and owner contracts remain decisive.
