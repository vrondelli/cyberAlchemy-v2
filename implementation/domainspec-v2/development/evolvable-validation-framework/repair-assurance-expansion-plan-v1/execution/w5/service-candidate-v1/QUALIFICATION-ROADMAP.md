# Qualification and accreditation roadmap

Status checked: 2026-09-12. This is a roadmap, not a claim that CyberAlchemy is
certified or accredited.

## Recommended order

1. **Build an auditable management baseline.** Establish evidence retention,
   competence records, impartiality, complaints/appeals, subcontractor control,
   corrective action and internal audit. Pursue ISO/IEC 27001 for the information
   security management system when customer data handling justifies it. ISO says
   ISO/IEC 27001:2022 defines ISMS requirements for managing information-security
   risks: <https://www.iso.org/standard/27001>.
2. **Choose the conformity-assessment identity after pilots.** The strongest
   initial fit appears to be inspection under ISO/IEC 17020:2026 because the
   service examines a software implementation or process against explicit
   requirements and reports conformity within bounds. ISO describes the standard
   as requirements for competent, impartial and consistent inspection bodies:
   <https://www.iso.org/standard/86292.html>.
3. **Evaluate laboratory accreditation if repeatable testing becomes the core.**
   ISO/IEC 17025:2017 applies to organizations performing testing and is used by
   accreditation bodies to assess laboratory competence:
   <https://www.iso.org/standard/66912.html>. This path requires validated test
   methods, uncertainty or validity controls where applicable, equipment/tool
   control, proficiency evidence and a precise accreditation scope.
4. **Evaluate ISO/IEC 17029 only for a defined claim-verification programme.**
   It covers competent, impartial and consistent validation/verification of
   declared claims and requires a sector-specific programme in addition to the
   generic standard: <https://www.iso.org/standard/29352.html>. It does not
   automatically turn software testing into accredited verification.
5. **Do not issue CyberAlchemy product certificates yet.** Product, process or
   service certification requires a certification scheme and a body operating
   to ISO/IEC 17065. ISO/IEC 17065:2012 remains published but is being replaced;
   the second edition was at final-draft stage when checked:
   <https://www.iso.org/standard/46568.html> and
   <https://www.iso.org/standard/94443.html>. Scheme ownership, impartial
   certification decisions, surveillance, complaints and mark control are a
   separate business from an assurance engagement.
6. **Use ISO/IEC 42001 only for the organization's AI management system.** It
   addresses governance of organizations developing, providing or using AI and
   does not certify that one Daybreak Blue-assisted assessment is correct:
   <https://www.iso.org/standard/42001>.

## What can be offered before accreditation

Offer a bounded assurance report, evidence manifest, repair-verification report
and separate technical replay. Describe methods, bounds and reviewer
independence precisely. Do not use “certified,” an accreditation mark, or a
certificate of conformity.

## Accreditation discovery questions

- Will CyberAlchemy act as an inspection body, testing laboratory, or claim
  verification body?
- Which national accreditation body covers the legal entity and desired scope?
- Can repair consulting and independent conformity decisions be separated to
  meet impartiality requirements?
- What method-validation, proficiency-testing and inter-reviewer evidence is
  required for software correctness assessments?
- Is there a recognized scheme owner and specified requirements for any future
  product/service certificate?

## Open Questions

Select the legal jurisdiction, accreditation body and desired scope only after
one or more measured pilots demonstrate a repeatable method and viable demand.
The ISO/IEC 17065 revision must be rechecked before any certification programme
is designed.
