# Execution and independence protocol

## Required sequence

1. Record the owner-approved property and claim ceiling.
2. Freeze implementation, tools, commands, cases, bounds and expected evidence.
3. Validate the evidence pipeline with denominator, oracle, raw-evidence and
   execution-failure controls.
4. Run the unmodified subject and preserve raw observations.
5. Assign one of the four result types from raw evidence.
6. If a confirmed defect exists, bind its cause and authorized repair scope.
7. Preserve the pre-repair counterexample before changing the isolated subject.
8. Apply the repair and rerun the original counterexample, full frozen
   denominator, neutral checks and selected superficial-repair controls.
9. Seal the author manifest before independent review.
10. A separate reviewer verifies integrity, reconstructs the result and runs a
    fresh replay or records why replay is impossible.
11. Report author/reviewer agreement, disagreement, corrections and residual
    limits without erasing earlier evidence.

## Evidence rules

- Raw observations, normalized results and decisions remain separate.
- Missing, duplicated, cross-run or stale rows cannot count as pass.
- Tool, source and command identity drift creates a new epoch.
- Mutation survival is reported; expected failure is never manufactured.
- Seeded mutants are labeled calibration evidence.
- Natural defects are preserved before repair.
- A reviewer correction is additive and does not rewrite sealed author output.
- A technical replay is independent review only within its disclosed personnel,
  host, evidence-access and authorship boundaries.

## Repair roles

The property owner controls meaning. The assessment lead controls the frozen
search contract. The repair author may change only the authorized isolated
surface. The reviewer does not inherit the author's conclusion as an oracle.
The implementation owner decides whether any repair enters the product. The
deployment owner controls every runtime effect.

## Open Questions

For a customer pilot, reviewer qualifications, independence category and any
independent-host requirement must be agreed during intake.
