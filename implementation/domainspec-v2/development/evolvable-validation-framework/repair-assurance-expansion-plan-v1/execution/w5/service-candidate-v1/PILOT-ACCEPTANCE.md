# Bounded pilot acceptance criteria

## Entry criteria

- one named property owner approves the property and meaning source;
- one implementation revision and reproducible environment are available;
- scope fits one primary property and one implementation boundary;
- data/access terms are complete;
- no production effects are required;
- the customer accepts all four typed result classes;
- author, reviewer and customer decision roles are assigned; and
- maximum time, compute and retest limits are agreed before execution.

## Delivery acceptance

The pilot is delivered when:

- property, source, tools, commands, cases and bounds are digest-bound;
- the declared denominator has no missing or duplicate observations;
- selected controls challenge subject, oracle, denominator, evidence and
  execution paths;
- raw observations support exactly one typed result or disclose conflict;
- any natural counterexample is preserved before repair;
- any repair is separately identified and retested against the original
  counterexample and frozen regression set;
- a separate reviewer records AGREE, DISAGREE or INCONCLUSIVE;
- residual scope and evidence limitations are explicit; and
- the evidence manifest and data-disposition record are delivered.

Acceptance concerns completion and evidence integrity. It does not require the
software to pass, a defect to be found, a repair to be possible, or the reviewer
to agree with the author.

## Stop and re-scope triggers

Stop or re-scope on property-owner disagreement, source/tool drift, missing
denominator rows, surviving evidence-pipeline controls, unbounded resource use,
unauthorized data, production effect, or repair beyond the approved files.

## Open Questions

Customer-specific acceptance dates, dispute handling and evidence-retention
terms remain contract fields.
