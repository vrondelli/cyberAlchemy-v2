# Review - Precedent Gate

Reviewer: Lakatos, Imre.

## Status

**ACCEPT-WITH-REPAIRS.**

This is a valid progressive promotion candidate, not a block. It is strongest
when framed as a governed facet lifecycle for promotion candidates, not as a
new authority layer that overrides existing owners.

## Strongest Precedent Evidence

Schema is already owned by Arcanum: `DEF-ARC-SCHEMA` defines schema as
structural validity and explicitly denies promotion/authority transfer by shape
alone. Evidence: `arcanum/definitions/DEFINITIONS.md:77`,
`research.md:30`.

Taxonomy is already owned as DomainSpec typed vocabulary: DS-D1, DS-D2, and
DS-D8 distinguish category vocabulary, relation vocabulary, and edge-signature
validity. Evidence: `arcanum/definitions/DEFINITIONS.md:293`,
`research.md:94`.

Human and machine views have strong build-from-owned precedent: Craft separates
source ledger, derived index, and linked human view; framework-unification has
paired views and a proposed node envelope, but admits the validator /
typed-residue gate is not fully built. Evidence:
`cyberAlchemy-v2/authority/definitions/DEFINITIONS.md:463`,
`projects/framework-unification/CONCEPT-MAP.md:91`,
`projects/framework-unification/CONCEPT-MAP.md:174`.

Typed residue and owner routes are directly precedented by CAV2-D16: if a
coupling affects authority, validation, promotion, or implementation, it must
be explicit; otherwise it remains residue/blocker/candidate note. Evidence:
`cyberAlchemy-v2/authority/definitions/DEFINITIONS.md:626`.

## Weakest Assumption

The synthesis assumes one universal four-facet rule can bind definitions,
constitutions, axioms, disciplines, tasks, systems, and workflows at the same
maturity level. That is overbroad today. The repo has evidence for the pattern,
but not yet a single validator, owner matrix, or canonical envelope that
applies uniformly across all those artifact classes.

## Required Repairs

Define `governed facets` as a promotion-readiness lifecycle, not a new source of
authority.

Use `DEF-ARC-SCHEMA` by reference; only add CAV2 schema subtypes where authority
behavior differs.

Define `classification taxonomy` with owner, scope, authority posture,
membership rule, and promotion state. Do not let it collapse into ontology.

Make `human-continuation view` and `machine/checkable view` declare source of
truth, derivation/projection status, freshness, validation boundary, and
non-authority posture.

Soften the lifecycle rule to: every load-bearing promotion candidate must
declare the four facets or record typed residue with owner route, rationale,
and whether promotion may proceed with accepted residue.

Keep the axioms. They are exactly the anti-collapse laws this program needs.

## Final Recommendation

Accept with repairs. This is a progressive research-program move: it unifies
existing schema, taxonomy, Craft, paired-view, residue, and promotion-gate
precedents while producing stricter future tests. The repair is to stage it as
a governed promotion discipline before claiming universal primitive authority.
It has good bones, but needs narrower jurisdiction and sharper owner routing
before promotion.
