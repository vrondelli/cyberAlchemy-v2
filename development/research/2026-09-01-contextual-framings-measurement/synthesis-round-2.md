## `SYN2-01` Claim and intervention boundary

The tested intervention is the **analogical contextual-framing treatment** (`ACFT`): an evidence-equivalent representation that explicitly declares source-to-target correspondences, non-correspondences, limits, and a return path to the concrete case.

This design cannot support an inference about “framing” generally. Any result would apply only to the frozen `ACFT` bytes, tasks, cases, population, and conditions studied.

Current claim ceiling:

> We define candidate measures and a falsifiable round-trip protocol for an analogical contextual-framing treatment. Its effectiveness and psychological interpretation remain empirical questions.

The strategy packet’s statement that analogy quality “can improve explanation” is unsupported by the selected evidence and is excluded from this synthesis without mutating the packet.

## `SYN2-06` Non-operational primary hierarchy

The candidate hierarchy is:

1. `SYN2-P1a`: comparative claim-ceiling/authority-boundary error safety.
2. `SYN2-P1b`: treatment-only analogy-harm sentinel.
3. `SYN2-P2`: semantic fidelity, return fidelity, counterfactual discrimination, and unfamiliar transfer design family.
4. Secondary operational-cost and confidence-pairing outcomes.

`SYN2-P2` is explicitly non-operational. It cannot be scored as a composite until a single preregistered decision object freezes:

- component units and normalization;
- weights or lexicographic ordering;
- denominators;
- missing-data handling;
- literal comparative safety margin;
- analogy-harm trigger threshold;
- literal minimum benefit threshold;
- uncertainty rule and inferential method.

Until then, no primary effect verdict is computable.

Prospective decision logic:

- If P1a violates its frozen margin, treatment benefit is unsupported regardless of P2 or speed.
- If P1b crosses its frozen sentinel threshold, treatment benefit is unsupported and harm residue is routed.
- Only after P1a and P1b survive may P2 be evaluated.
- A favorable aggregate cannot conceal a harmed component.
- Accuracy and critical-error safety are lexically prior to completion time, actions, help, preference, or perceived effort.
- Failure to exceed the frozen P2 threshold falsifies or leaves unsupported the treatment-benefit direction; it does not falsify semantic fidelity or transfer as constructs.

## `SYN2-07` Build-from-owned candidate protocol inputs

The following are candidate inputs for `research-evidence-harness`, not inherited protocol facts:

- Baseline: one prose representation containing the same evidence atoms.

### Seven required materialized witness classes

1. Positive/helpful control witness: a valid analogy with an independently keyed boundary.
2. Neutral analogy.
3. Misleading analogy.
4. Harmful analogy.
5. Leakage witness.
6. Required-return witness.
7. Unfamiliar-transfer witness.

Each must have paired frozen baseline/`ACFT` bytes, evidence-atom inventory, answer key, allowed and prohibited inferences, and expected residue.

### Scoring and adjudication

- Machine-score exact categorical and key-presence fields where possible.
- Freeze a semantic scoring codebook before execution.
- Use condition-blinded independent scorers for semantic judgments.
- Freeze disagreement, escalation, third-adjudicator, and unresolved-result rules.
- Preserve original scores and adjudication trace.

This is a candidate scorer design, not proof that scoring reliability is adequate.

## `SYN2-08` Illustrative human pilot shape

This is not “the smallest adequate experiment.”

An illustrative candidate pilot could use a counterbalanced first-exposure design in which each participant receives alternate cases across baseline and `ACFT`, never the same case in both forms. The primary comparison must be either:

- first exposure only; or
- a preregistered first-period estimate.

Later periods may diagnose carryover but cannot silently replace the primary comparison. Task order, prior analogy exposure, familiarity, demand cues, fluency, help, fatigue, and interface conditions must be controlled or recorded.

Population, recruitment, participant count, power, sensitivity, burden ceiling, retention interval, stopping thresholds, and effect margins remain open residue. No participant contact or execution is authorized.

## `SYN2-10` Prospective invalidation and stop rules

These are build-from-owned candidate inputs whose literal thresholds remain unfrozen:

- Invalidate affected trials for digest mismatch, failed evidence equivalence, learner-visible keys or rubrics, missing primary observations, arm deviation, or compromised scoring blindness.
- Stop the instrument run for systemic leakage, invalid source identity, broken equivalence validation, or unusable telemetry.
- Stop a participant block on request or a preregistered burden/stall limit; retain the stop as an outcome.
- A treatment-only analogy-harm stopping threshold must be literal in the decision object; this synthesis does not invent its value.
- Do not stop, extend, or reweight because interim results appear favorable.
- Preserve invalid, failed, mixed, and harmful outcomes as typed residue.
