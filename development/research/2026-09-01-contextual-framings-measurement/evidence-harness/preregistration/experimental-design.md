# ACFT Experimental Design Candidate

Status: pending owner decisions. No live authority.

## Research question

For one frozen ACFT, do first-exposure responses preserve the bounded source
meaning and task-specific correctness at least as safely as an
evidence-equivalent prose baseline, without crossing a separately frozen
analogy-harm threshold?

This question concerns only the frozen arms, task family, cases, population,
and conditions. It does not ask whether framing generally works, whether ACFT
improves a person's general comprehension, or whether CyberAlchemy improves
usability, productivity, cognitive workload, or ergonomics.

## Fixed design constraints

- Compare baseline prose with ACFT using identical evidence atoms.
- Never expose one participant to both forms of the same case.
- Treat first exposure as primary; later periods may diagnose carryover only.
- Preserve all seven witness classes: helpful control, neutral, misleading,
  harmful, leakage, required return, and unfamiliar transfer.
- Freeze stimuli, evidence atoms, prompts, task order policy, help/time policy,
  codebook, scoring rubric, manifests, and digests before execution.
- Score safety before correctness; process cost and self-report cannot rescue a
  safety or correctness failure.
- Keep mapping-attributed harm treatment-only and require a blinded
  mapping-to-error adjudication trace.
- Invalidate affected trials for digest mismatch, failed evidence equivalence,
  learner-visible keys or rubrics, missing primary observations, arm deviation,
  or compromised scoring blindness.
- Stop the instrument for systemic leakage, invalid source identity, broken
  equivalence validation, or unusable telemetry.
- Never stop, extend, select, or reweight because interim observations look
  favorable.

## Candidate design family

The source supports a counterbalanced first-exposure design as an illustrative
shape, not as an owner decision. The owner must explicitly select and justify
the experimental design, assignment method, counterbalancing method, primary
estimand, population, sample, and analysis policy.

The intended comparison surface is:

```text
P1a boundary-error safety
  -> must survive the owner-selected non-inferiority rule
P1b analogy-harm sentinel
  -> must remain below the owner-selected critical-event rule
P2 correctness family
  -> evaluated only after P1a and P1b survive
secondary observations
  -> descriptive; cannot reverse an earlier failure
```

`P2` remains a family of semantic fidelity, return fidelity,
counterfactual discrimination, and unfamiliar-transfer accuracy. No composite
exists until the owner freezes component ordering or weights, denominators,
missing-data handling, benefit threshold, and uncertainty method.

## Scoring and adjudication

Exact categorical fields should be machine-scored where the frozen key permits.
Semantic judgments require condition-blinded independent scoring. Original
scores, disagreement, escalation, adjudication, and unresolved outcomes must be
retained. Reliability thresholds and scorer escalation rules remain explicit
owner decisions rather than implicit conventions.

## Participant protection boundary

Population, eligibility, recruitment, consent or review requirements,
participant stop rules, burden limit, and contact procedures remain unresolved.
No recruitment or live collection may begin from this candidate. Any applicable
human-research or ethics review is a separate prerequisite, not something this
repository validator can supply.

## Result language

Before live evidence exists, the strongest permitted statement is:

> We define a falsifiable candidate protocol for one frozen ACFT.

A structural test may say only that the instrument passed its recorded
structural conditions. An accepted preregistration still does not establish an
effect. Failed, invalid, mixed, or harmful outcomes must remain visible and
cannot be reframed as confirmation.
