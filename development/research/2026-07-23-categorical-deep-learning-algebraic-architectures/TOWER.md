## Classification rule

Every load-bearing claim is classified as one of:

1. `inherited` — established substrate attributed to prior work;
2. `defined` — source supplies typed data and laws;
3. `proved` — source presents a theorem under explicit hypotheses;
4. `sketched` — construction or coherence is not fully expanded;
5. `worked-example` — a selected family is constructed, without a general theorem;
6. `asserted-unproved`;
7. `conjecture`;
8. `position-or-aspiration`;
9. `compiled-local-witness`;
10. `blocked`.

Tower closure means the research question is adjudicated. It does not mean the
paper's general program or its higher-categorical Lean formalization is complete.
