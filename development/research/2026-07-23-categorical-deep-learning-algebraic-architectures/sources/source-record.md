## Title parsing

The paper's first-page footnote says the title must be parsed as:

> Categorical Deep Learning is an Algebraic {Theory of All Architectures}

not as “an {Algebraic Theory} of All Architectures.” The tower therefore does
not treat the title as a claim that a completed algebraic theory captures every
architecture.

## Source status

The abstract and Sections 1.1–1.2 explicitly frame the work as an opinion and a
position. The appendices contain definitions, theorems, sketches, an unproved
corollary, a conjecture, and worked constructions. Those statuses are preserved
individually in the [claim ledger](../claims/claim-ledger.md).

## Ancestry boundary

The paper attributes:

- `Para` to Cruttwell et al. and Capucci et al.;
- geometric deep-learning symmetry framing to Bronstein et al.;
- the surrounding monad, (co)algebra, fold/unfold, free-monad, and universal
  algebra machinery to established categorical literature.

The paper-specific contribution assessed here is the proposed combination and
ML interpretation, not origination of those components.
