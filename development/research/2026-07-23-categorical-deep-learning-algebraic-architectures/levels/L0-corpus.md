This chain intentionally uses “selected.” The source does not prove a
classification or discovery theorem for all neural architectures.

## Required hypotheses

A downstream use must state:

- category `C`;
- monoidal parameter category `M`;
- the action of `M` on `C`;
- the endofunctor or monad and any strength;
- the initial/final/free objects used;
- strictness, connectedness, and preservation assumptions where invoked.

The paper moves among `Set`, `Vect`, `Smooth`, and general actegories. Those
contexts are not interchangeable without a bridge.
