# Related Work and Local Precedent

## External ancestry

- Cruttwell et al., *Categorical foundations of gradient-based learning*:
  `Para`/categorical learning foundation.
- Capucci et al., *Towards foundations of categorical cybernetics*:
  parametric/cybernetic categorical setting.
- Bronstein et al., *Geometric Deep Learning*: symmetry/equivariance framing.
- Standard universal algebra and coalgebra literature: monads, initial/final
  semantics, folds, unfolds, and free monads.

## Local separation

The existing functor-stack tower concerns fully faithful staged translations,
dual residue, and source grounding. It already treats `Para`/lens/backprop
framing as `borrow-carefully`. This paper neither validates nor extends that
tower's distinct claims.

The adjacent Morrow categorical-learning tower audits an underdefined
architecture proposal. The present paper supplies lawful external categorical
definitions but does not retroactively provide missing categories, functors,
naturality, adjunctions, or execution semantics to that proposal.
