# Local Definitions

These terms are scoped to this tower and are not CAV2 authority.

## Paper-specific synthesis

The paper's proposed combination and ML interpretation of inherited categorical
machinery. It does not mean the paper originated the machinery.

## Selected architecture construction

A typed recursive or corecursive construction that begins from a chosen
endofunctor and parametric cell. It is not architecture discovery and does not
establish universal coverage.

## Compiled type boundary

A Lean artifact that validates the shape and laws of a deliberately small
interface. It proves neither the larger source theory nor a neural implementation.

## Candidate typed coupling

A future, unpromoted relation between a declared source kind and target kind,
with direction/variance, preservation condition, implementation mapping, owner,
evidence, counterexample, and promotion effect. The phrase is local audit
vocabulary, not paper terminology or a current CAV2 definition.

## Authority delta zero

The research changes no canonical definition, ontology kind, schema, machine
view, lifecycle rule, implementation obligation, or public artifact.
