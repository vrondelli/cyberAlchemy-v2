CyberAlchemy v2 · Authority

What authority is — and where to start forcing

Authority is the system's answer to one question: which of all this stuff is the system allowed to treat as true going forward? Everything below follows from that.

TL;DR — Let AI write freely. Force the gate at exactly one place: where candidate output is about to govern the next step. That boundary is the promotion gate. Force there, and nowhere earlier.

1 · What authority is

Authority is a permission to make something count — bound to an owner and a scope.

In any working system you have a flood of material: notes, drafts, claims, half-finished ideas. Authority is the line that says this part is settled, build on it — and that part is just a candidate, lean on it at your own risk.

The rule cav2 actually writes down: “authority moves by owner route, not by proximity, usefulness, or repetition.” Something being handy, or being said a lot, never makes it binding on its own.

2 · Why it exists — the AI problem it solves

An AI agent produces confident, fluent, plausible output by the page. Some is right, some is subtly wrong — and you can't tell which by reading, because it all sounds equally authoritative.

Then a second agent reads the first's output as fact and builds on it. A third builds on the second. Three steps later you have a tower of binding-sounding claims that no human ever approved, and the wrong one at the bottom is now load-bearing.

That's drift becoming authority by proximity, usefulness, and repetition — exactly the failure authority is built to stop.

3 · How it impacts human–AI work

Authority is the firebreak. It splits the work into two surfaces — and decides who leads on each:

Capture surface · AI leads

Write freely

  • Syntheses, candidate cards, exploration
  • Cheap, high-volume, throw-away
  • Nothing here is binding
Authority surface · Human leads

Governs the work

  • Definitions, rules, decisions
  • Only entered by deliberate owner route
  • Everything downstream relies on it
▲ the only crossing is the gate — and crossing is never automatic

The payoff is bounded human attention. You don't police every sentence the AI writes — you'd drown. Your attention concentrates on the few “should this become a rule?” moments. Everything else stays quarantined as candidate, where being wrong costs nothing.

4 · Where to start forcing

Force the gate at one boundary: where output crosses from describing to governing.

Start forcing when output is about to…Leave it free when…
become a premise the next step builds on as if true it's exploration, a draft, a synthesis card, a search result — anything flagged candidate and cheap to throw away. Let agents run unsupervised here.
define or redefine a term, rule, or decision
be hard to reverse if it's wrong
go load-bearing — many things will depend on it

The ergonomics: force everywhere and you drown in gates; force nowhere and you get drift. The skill is forcing at the fewest, highest-leverage chokepoints and leaving everything upstream frictionless.

→ The first place to force, concretely: the promotion gate — the single move from candidate to authority (a definition / constitution clause / decision). Force that one gate hard and it protects everything downstream. Put it there, and nowhere earlier.

5 · It happened live

In the session this page came from, AI agents produced two confident claims — “cav2 is a reflexive meta-authority mold” and “no authority without a lifecycle.” Fluent. Plausible. Sounded canonical.

The authority system held them as candidate. When one was pushed toward becoming a real rule, a human review caught that it secretly just re-worded an existing rule, and re-scoped it into a narrower definition instead.

The good content got absorbed; the overreach got caught at the gate instead of silently calcifying into the foundation. That is authority doing its job.
