# Recursivity Promotion Verdict Findings

Status: audited finding
Date: 2026-06-21
Dispatch: `2026-06-21-recursivity-promotion-verdict`

## 2026-06-22 Definitions-Governance Resolution

This verdict remains valid for the vague label `recursivity`: do not promote the
unbounded term. CyberAlchemy v2 resolved the blocker by promoting
`CAV2-D30: bounded recursive self-application`, with `recursivity` only as an
alias for the bounded meaning.

The promoted term must preserve the discrimination tests below: it is not
ordinary recursion, generic self-hosting, `CAV2-D17` reflection tower method, or
`CAV2-D20` Craft method.

## Short Answer

**DO NOT PROMOTE.**

The phenomenon is witnessed, but the current label `recursivity` is not yet
definitionally settled and does not yet pick out a distinct semantic object
apart from already named neighboring concepts.

This finding does not mutate canon. It preserves `recursivity` as a candidate
for definitions-governance term selection and boundary drafting.

## Future Drafting Notes

Draft toward a term that names a distinct semantic object, not just a family
resemblance among recursive-looking cases.

Required discrimination tests:

- not ordinary recursion
- not self-hosting in the generic software sense
- not merely framework-unification `recursive self-application`
- not CAV2-D17 reflection tower method
- not CAV2-D20 Craft method
- not generic `method`

Preserve the strongest witnessed common structure:

- same governed process
- applied to artifacts of that process
- across distinct altitudes
- with authority boundaries preserved rather than collapsed

Treat tower behavior, Craft ledger governance, and engine/process
self-application as neighboring evidence fields that may support an umbrella
term, an imported alias, or deliberate deferral, but do not yet by themselves
settle which of those it is.

Any future wording should make clear whether the target object is:

- an invariant
- a method family
- a source-imported alias
- a local umbrella over several already named mechanisms

## Next Valid Move

Run definitions-governance term selection for the witnessed phenomenon:

- `recursivity` as candidate term
- `recursive self-application` as source-space precedent term
- CAV2-D17 and CAV2-D20 as non-overlap boundaries
- ledger primitive review as blocked downstream consumer

Do not promote `recursivity` until the term selection and boundary tests are
resolved.
