## Agent 1 — Abramsky, Samson

## Explorer report — explicit meaning hierarchies from embedding neighborhoods

### Bottom line

This corpus offers a strong **representation and non-collapse contract** for displaying several explicit, parallel views over stable semantic identities. It does **not** provide a hierarchy-induction method. Its honest contribution to a GNN ontology evaluator is therefore downstream of discovery: it can type, preserve provenance for, challenge, compare, and project candidate hierarchy edges produced elsewhere. It cannot derive those edges from embedding neighborhoods.

### 1. Candidate pieces that directly help

- **Typed node records.** The node contract requires stable identity, role, sources, status, two-axis confidence, challenge protocol, residue, and forbidden collapse. This is a useful target schema for reifying GNN-discovered clusters or concepts as reviewable candidates: [NODE-MODEL.md:12-27](cyberAlchemy-v2/ontology/NODE-MODEL.md#node-concept-contract), with explicit challenge shape at [NODE-MODEL.md:29-55](cyberAlchemy-v2/ontology/NODE-MODEL.md#node-concept-contract).

- **Typed edge records and endpoint validation.** Candidate relations must name source, target, direction, owner, evidence, authority effect, and forbidden collapse: [RELATION-MODEL.md:13-26](cyberAlchemy-v2/ontology/RELATION-MODEL.md#relation-contract). Relations without complete endpoints and semantics must remain residue rather than implicit dependencies: [RELATION-MODEL.md:73-77](cyberAlchemy-v2/ontology/RELATION-MODEL.md#endpoint-rule). This directly helps turn “nearby in embedding space” into explicit, falsifiable relation proposals.

- **Cycle/value checks.** Every proposed relation must classify its value effect and cycle policy; unknowns remain typed residue: [RELATION-MODEL.md:28-43](cyberAlchemy-v2/ontology/RELATION-MODEL.md#value-cycle-guard). For hierarchies, this is useful for distinguishing intended DAG-like taxonomies from lawful cyclic relation systems.

- **Parallel views without semantic contamination.** The Inventory synthesis proposes three separately stored views over stable identities—governed semantic, authored learner map, and non-semantic navigation—and requires learner/navigation edge removal to leave semantic bytes unchanged: [interactive synthesis:81-104](.arcanum/inventory/entries/interactive-ontology-learning-map-strategy-result-synthesis.md#accepted-architecture). This is the clearest bridge to parallel hierarchies: multiple explicit projections may coexist, but their edges need view identity and must not silently rewrite source semantics.

- **Branch/view contract.** A branch records its role, sources, allowed readings, forbidden moves, owner, and residue: [BRANCH-AS-VIEW.md:6-24](cyberAlchemy-v2/ontology/BRANCH-AS-VIEW.md#branch-contract). This can model parallel hierarchies such as taxonomic, functional, evidential, pedagogical, or navigation views—provided each is explicitly labeled as a view.

- **Non-collapse rules.** Branch membership is neither node type nor promotion state, and a branch map is not source authority: [BRANCH-AS-VIEW.md:36-46](cyberAlchemy-v2/ontology/BRANCH-AS-VIEW.md#forbidden-collapses). Likewise, ontology projection is explicitly a read model whose projection cannot change authority: [TAXONOMY-CROSSWALK.md:22-34](cyberAlchemy-v2/ontology/TAXONOMY-CROSSWALK.md#candidate-object-crosswalk).

### 2. Governance/read-model scaffolding only

- The ontology folder itself is a **candidate authority-model boundary**, not an authority owner, and cannot promote or redefine definitions, schemas, runtime contracts, or movement edges: [README.md:3-18](cyberAlchemy-v2/ontology/README.md), [README.md:20-31](cyberAlchemy-v2/ontology/README.md#repository-objective-role).

- The initial node concepts concern authority artifacts, workflows, evidence, projections, and residue—not domain-semantic concept classes for a learned ontology: [NODE-MODEL.md:61-76](cyberAlchemy-v2/ontology/NODE-MODEL.md#initial-node-concepts).

- The allowed relations are largely promotion, ownership, evidence, projection, and drift-routing predicates, not hierarchy predicates such as `is_a`, `subclass_of`, `part_of`, or typed abstraction: [RELATION-MODEL.md:45-58](cyberAlchemy-v2/ontology/RELATION-MODEL.md#allowed-candidate-relations).

- Both crosswalks are explanatory mappings only. Classification does not promote objects: [TAXONOMY-CROSSWALK.md:14-20](cyberAlchemy-v2/ontology/TAXONOMY-CROSSWALK.md#crosswalk-rules). Candidate relations do not create executable/vault edges: [RELATIONSHIP-CROSSWALK.md:6-24](cyberAlchemy-v2/ontology/RELATIONSHIP-CROSSWALK.md#purpose).

- The Inventory synthesis is explicitly a non-authority read model: [interactive synthesis:15-20](.arcanum/inventory/entries/interactive-ontology-learning-map-strategy-result-synthesis.md). Its immutable JSON/compiler/UI recommendation is implementation scaffolding, not hierarchy discovery: [interactive synthesis:124-143](.arcanum/inventory/entries/interactive-ontology-learning-map-strategy-result-synthesis.md#concrete-witness-and-build-recommendation).

### 3. Missing hierarchy-induction operations

The corpus does not specify:

1. how embedding neighborhoods become candidate concepts;
2. how to choose or learn parent-child predicates;
3. hypernym/subclass/part-whole tests or negative witnesses;
4. abstraction-level estimation and root selection;
5. polyhierarchy admission and multiple-parent criteria;
6. transitive closure/reduction, redundant-edge pruning, or cycle repair;
7. sibling coherence, disjointness, coverage, or orphan checks;
8. alignment between parallel hierarchies and detection of cross-view contradictions;
9. confidence calibration from geometric evidence to semantic commitment;
10. human adjudication protocols for competing induced hierarchies;
11. benchmarks or gold fixtures measuring hierarchy quality.

The strongest available constraint is only that relation endpoints and semantics must be explicit and incomplete relations remain residue ([RELATION-MODEL.md:73-77](cyberAlchemy-v2/ontology/RELATION-MODEL.md#endpoint-rule)).

### 4. Explicit residue

- **Hierarchy discovery remains external.** Embedding similarity is evidence for a candidate relation, never the relation’s meaning.
- **No canonical semantic hierarchy vocabulary exists in this corpus.** Exact projection vocabulary is explicitly blocked/missing: [interactive synthesis:161-170](.arcanum/inventory/entries/interactive-ontology-learning-map-strategy-result-synthesis.md#dispositions-and-reuse).
- **Parallel hierarchy semantics are unresolved.** The corpus supports separately stored views, but supplies no lawful correspondence/alignment operations between them.
- **Projection versus discovery must remain separated:** discovery proposes concepts/edges from model evidence; validation challenges their semantics; projection renders accepted or candidate records into named views. Projection cannot manufacture discovery, proof, authority, or promotion ([interactive synthesis:96-104](.arcanum/inventory/entries/interactive-ontology-learning-map-strategy-result-synthesis.md#accepted-architecture)).
- **Empirical validation is absent.** All runtime, performance, determinism, browser, and accessibility checks remain `NOT_RUN`: [interactive synthesis:137-143](.arcanum/inventory/entries/interactive-ontology-learning-map-strategy-result-synthesis.md#concrete-witness-and-build-recommendation).
