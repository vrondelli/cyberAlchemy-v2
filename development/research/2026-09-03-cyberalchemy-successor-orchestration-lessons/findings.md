## Non-canonical comparison types and cardinalities

These terms normalize the comparison only; they create no canonical definition. One `SemanticWork` identifies one immutable meaning/version of an objective and governed graph; separate owner decisions may authorize zero or more Runs, and meaning-changing bytes create a new identity. One `Run` is one orchestration lifecycle for one immutable Dispatch and may contain many actions and attempts; a new Run inherits no acceptance or effect authority. One `Attempt` is one try to admit or execute one action; retry or resume creates a distinct Attempt while preserving the semantic-work/action identity. A DomainSpec `TaskSession` is a fresh context for exactly one admitted SWU Attempt and at most one terminal receipt; it is not generally equivalent to a Run. One Attempt may contain zero Invocations when host admission fails before provider/agent binding, so no invocation or agent identity may be fabricated.
