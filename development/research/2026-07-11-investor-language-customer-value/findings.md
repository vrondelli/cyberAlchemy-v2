# Investor Language And Customer Value Findings

## 1. Executive Verdict

**`GO` for a bounded commercial experiment. `REPAIR` before investor circulation. `KILL` unsupported claims as current assertions, without treating unwitnessed but coherent hypotheses as terminally dead.**

The commercially testable proposition is:

> **We are testing whether engineering leaders will pay for an external, fixed-scope review of one consequential software release.**

The candidate offer is a **fixed-scope release review sprint** producing a **versioned release review** for one identified release, environment, deployment population, evidence cutoff, and customer-authorized release owner. Customer specialists confirm applicable requirements and interpret specialist results. CyberAlchemy assembles and classifies available results, gaps, exceptions, and triggers. The customer-authorized release owner retains deployment authority. ([Agent 2, “Customer Chain” and “Baseline And Pilot Design”](research.md#agent-2---grove-andrew---technology-company-customer-economics); [business plan, technology-company scenario and offer](../../business-plan/2026-07-investor-bp/business-plan.md#scenario-2-a-technology-company-at-the-release-gate); [operating plan, Engagement Stages](../../business-plan/2026-07-investor-bp/operating-plan.md#engagement-stages))

This is a coherent service design, not a witnessed commercial wedge. The repository records no demonstrated economic buyer, paid pilot, collected price, accepted customer result, repurchase, measured operational improvement, or validated differentiation. ([Agent 5, “Transaction Audit”](research.md#agent-5---porter-michael---b2b-positioning); [diligence registry, Commercial Evidence Queue](../../business-plan/2026-07-investor-bp/diligence-evidence-registry.md#commercial-evidence-queue); [source trace, Missing Primary Evidence](../../business-plan/2026-07-investor-bp/source-trace.md#missing-primary-evidence))

The release-review experiment is recommended because it is more precisely specifiable than the other two scenarios, not because demand, access, value, event frequency, or competitive advantage has been established. Live-premises operations and confidential professional workflows remain full diligence hypotheses, not primary investor markets. ([Agent 1, “Evidence Verdict”](research.md#agent-1---drucker-peter---live-premises-customer-economics); [Agent 3, “Core Finding”](research.md#agent-3---christensen-clayton---confidential-workflow-customer-economics); [Agent 5, “Recommended Position”](research.md#recommended-position))

### Monetary taxonomy

Every monetary statement uses exactly one of these tags:

- **CUSTOMER-BASELINE:** customer records and customer-approved valuation methods.
- **MEASURED-PILOT:** observed paid-pilot results with scope, comparison, burden, confounders, and observation window.
- **EXTERNAL-BENCHMARK:** external context that is not CyberAlchemy demand, savings, or return.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** unvalidated price, budget, mechanism, unit model, or financial expectation.
- **PROHIBITED:** a monetary claim that current evidence cannot support.

### Separate nonmonetary status vocabulary

These are research statuses, not monetary tags:

- **repository-supported**
- **externally documented**
- **unwitnessed candidate**
- **proposed test protocol**
- **missing evidence**
- **definitionally unsound**

## 5. Lead Buyer, Payor, And Validation Contract

### Falsifiable lead hypothesis

> A VP Engineering or equivalent executive accountable for a consequential release will purchase a fixed-scope release review from an engineering productivity, platform, quality, or operational-risk budget when a qualifying release has a fixed deployment window, cross-system review burden, an authorized release owner, and a bounded source-access path.

The same person may be user, buyer, budget owner, and signer, but the test must not assume that role collapse. ([Agent 2, “Customer Chain”](research.md#customer-chain); [Agent 5, “Transaction Audit”](research.md#transaction-audit))

### Required validation fields

Record for every prospect:

- organization profile and release type;
- buyer title, responsibility, and success measure;
- day-to-day user and customer-authorized release owner;
- budget name, budget owner, remaining authority, and fiscal timing;
- signing authority and procurement approvers;
- purchase trigger and required deployment window;
- current review steps, roles, tools, labor, and failure points;
- incumbent tools and why configuration is insufficient;
- source-access, security, privacy, and contracting route;
- offered scope, unchanged price rule, discount, decision, and refusal reason;
- release-event frequency and expected future purchase frequency;
- whether the prospect would repurchase without promotional pricing.

A prospect is not qualified merely because an engineering leader expresses interest. It requires an identifiable buyer, authorized release owner, qualifying release, plausible access path, and presentation of the same fixed offer. ([Agent 5, “Transaction Audit”](research.md#transaction-audit); [operating plan, Customer Entry Gate](../../business-plan/2026-07-investor-bp/operating-plan.md#customer-entry-gate))

## 6. Lead Before/Intervention/After Chain And Added Burden

### Before

The release owner and specialists gather records from Git, tickets, CI, security, observability, documentation, and prior incidents; determine which requirements apply; resolve stale or conflicting results; negotiate exceptions; and record approval through existing tools and meetings. The actual sequence and burden must be observed, not presumed. ([Agent 2, “Customer Chain”](research.md#customer-chain))

### Intervention

CyberAlchemy defines the agreed boundary, inventories proposed sources, identifies candidate requirements, records checks and limitations, classifies results, structures exceptions, and drafts stop or rollback triggers. Customer specialists must still confirm applicability and interpret specialist results; the release owner must still approve the boundary. ([Agent 2, “Baseline And Pilot Design”](research.md#baseline-and-pilot-design); [operating plan, Delivery Loop](../../business-plan/2026-07-investor-bp/operating-plan.md#delivery-loop))

### Added burden

The sprint adds access review, source mapping, specialist-confirmation sessions, exception review, trigger assignment, security and procurement work, and CyberAlchemy coordination. It may duplicate existing review during shadow-mode pilots. All customer and CyberAlchemy setup, review, rework, and adoption hours must be recorded. ([Agent 2, “Customer Chain” and “Baseline And Pilot Design”](research.md#agent-2---grove-andrew---technology-company-customer-economics))

### After

The release owner receives a versioned record showing the release boundary, applicable requirements, current results, unresolved items, accepted exceptions, approved deployment boundary, and observable triggers. The owner may preserve, narrow, pause, retest, deploy, or roll back the release. No particular verdict proves value. ([Agent 2, “Bottom Line”](research.md#bottom-line); [operating plan, Engagement Stages](../../business-plan/2026-07-investor-bp/operating-plan.md#engagement-stages))

### Counterfactual and proof rule

The counterfactual is the competent internal process using existing tools without CyberAlchemy. A pilot passes on customer value only when a preregistered operational measure improves relative to comparable releases after added burden and known confounders are disclosed. ([Agent 2, “Baseline And Pilot Design”](research.md#baseline-and-pilot-design))

## 7. Plain-Language Offer And Completion Rule

### Company description

> CyberAlchemy is testing whether engineering leaders will pay for a fixed-scope review that organizes the requirements, results, unresolved items, exceptions, and rollback triggers for one consequential software release across the systems they already use.

### Initial offer

> In a fixed-scope release review sprint, CyberAlchemy assembles a versioned review of customer-confirmed requirements, current results, unresolved items, authorized exceptions, and observable stop or rollback triggers for the customer-authorized release owner.

These statements describe an experiment without claiming demand, operational improvement, category ownership, or measured value. ([Agent 4, “Plain-Language Vocabulary Map”](research.md#plain-language-vocabulary-map); [Agent 5, “Recommended Position”](research.md#recommended-position))

The deliverable is complete when the boundary and cutoff are explicit; every confirmed requirement names a source; every requirement has a classified result; checks state their limits; exceptions contain complete authority, scope, duration, compensation, and monitoring fields; the deployment boundary is recorded; triggers name observable events, sources, actors, responses, windows, and overrides; and unresolved items remain visible. Completion proves disciplined assembly only. ([Agent 2, “Customer Chain”](research.md#customer-chain); [operating plan, Quality Gates](../../business-plan/2026-07-investor-bp/operating-plan.md#quality-gates))

| Approximately `$19,000` expected annual AI-enabled legal-professional value | **EXTERNAL-BENCHMARK** | Vendor-sponsored expectation, never CyberAlchemy savings. ([Agent 3, “External Demand Signals”](research.md#external-demand-signals); [Thomson Reuters analysis](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en-us/posts/technology/future-of-professionals-2025/)) |
| `$4.4M` reported global average breach cost | **EXTERNAL-BENCHMARK** | Not a release benchmark and not CyberAlchemy return. ([Agent 2, “Monetary Claim Register”](research.md#monetary-claim-register); [IBM Cost of a Data Breach](https://www.ibm.com/reports/data-breach)) |
| Savings, ROI, payback, incident prevention, revenue protection, or margin improvement | **PROHIBITED** | Requires measured customer economics and defensible attribution. ([Agent 2, “Avoided Risk”](research.md#avoided-risk)) |
| Pause or rollback treated as avoided loss | **PROHIBITED** | No defensible counterfactual exists merely because deployment changed. ([Agent 2, “Avoided Risk”](research.md#avoided-risk)) |
| Validated margin, repeatable economics, or software leverage | **PROHIBITED** | Requires comparable paid work, complete cost classification, and measured service-burden reduction. ([financial assumptions, Financial Promotion Gates](../../business-plan/2026-07-investor-bp/financial-assumptions.md#financial-promotion-gates)) |

Measures must remain separate; do not combine them into a synthetic “decision quality” score. Paid acceptance supports willingness to pay for one scope and price. Repurchase supports perceived continuation value. Neither proves quantified return, incident prevention, market frequency, or differentiation. ([Agent 2, “Claim Separation”](research.md#claim-separation))

## 10. Commercial Falsification Gates And Empty Transaction Witness

The thresholds below are a **proposed test protocol**, not market facts.

### Gate 1: Commercial entry

Freeze release-inclusion criteria, buyer-authority criteria, deployment-window requirements, access conditions, disqualifiers, fixed scope, price rule, and test dates. Present the same offer to **10 qualifying prospects**.

- **Pass:** at least 2 sign and pay.
- **Fail:** fewer than 2 sign and pay.

Budget absence, contracting refusal, security rejection, incumbent preference, and unwillingness to purchase count as failed offers, not exclusions. ([Agent 5, “Transaction Audit”](research.md#transaction-audit); [operating plan, Customer Entry Gate](../../business-plan/2026-07-investor-bp/operating-plan.md#customer-entry-gate))

### Gate 2: Customer value

Before each pilot, preregister comparable releases, observation windows, numeric thresholds, labor methods, revision and rework definitions, setup-burden limits, CyberAlchemy burden, confounders, and the material missed-requirement condition.

After **3 paid pilots across at least 2 customers**:

- **Pass:** at least 2 pilots meet a preregistered operational threshold without unacceptable burden or a material missed in-scope requirement, and at least 1 customer repurchases at a non-promotional price.
- **Fail:** fewer than 2 pilots meet a threshold, or no customer repurchases.

This preserves separate evidence for commercial entry, customer value, and continuation. ([Agent 2, “Baseline And Pilot Design”](research.md#baseline-and-pilot-design); [operating plan, Operating Metrics](../../business-plan/2026-07-investor-bp/operating-plan.md#operating-metrics))

## 13. Claim Boundaries And Forbidden Claims

The following are prohibited as present-tense supported claims:

- validated demand, willingness to pay, event frequency, wedge, or differentiated value;
- unique, first, only, owned category, or proven moat;
- improved release operations without comparable measured evidence;
- release correctness, safety, compliance, certification, or incident prevention;
- improved judgment without an observable definition;
- repeatability, lower adaptation burden, retained-record leverage, or software leverage before measurement;
- ROI, payback, avoided loss, revenue protection, or margin improvement;
- feasible access before provenance, freshness, completeness, permission, privacy, and security are established;
- reach inferred from transfer hypotheses;
- transfer of customer authority to CyberAlchemy;
- “derived” requirements without a documented deterministic rule;
- generic obligations without identifiable requirement sources;
- completed review as proof of customer value;
- finished platform, current platform revenue, autonomous governance, or provably correct agents;
- audit readiness, external assurance, legal safety, medical safety, or regulatory approval;
- financing readiness before amount, instrument, burn, runway, and founder inputs are complete.

These boundaries consolidate the transcript’s monetary and non-claim registers and the investor package’s forbidden-claim registry. ([Agent 2, “Claim Separation”](research.md#claim-separation); [Agent 3, “Monetary Claim Register”](research.md#monetary-claim-register); [diligence registry, Forbidden Claim Register](../../business-plan/2026-07-investor-bp/diligence-evidence-registry.md#forbidden-claim-register))

## 14. Founder Decisions And Unresolved Evidence Gaps

The founders must resolve:

1. Names, roles, biographies, legal status, and relevant release-review experience.
2. Frozen release-inclusion criteria.
3. Buyer, payor, budget-owner, signer, and release-owner criteria.
4. Deployment-window, access, and disqualification rules.
5. Initial customer segment and event-frequency test.
6. Standard scope, duration, price rule, acceptance criteria, and walk-away rules.
7. Customer-specialist confirmation and interpretation powers.
8. Access, provenance, privacy, security, retention, insurance, and contracting boundaries.
9. Numeric operational thresholds for each pilot.
10. Unacceptable setup burden and material missed-requirement definitions.
11. Non-promotional repurchase price rule.
12. Current cash, liabilities, founder compensation, burn, financing amount, instrument, and runway.
13. Technical capabilities permitted in diligence and those remaining private.
14. The result that stops the release wedge rather than causing another narrative revision.

The missing evidence remains buyer interviews, paid acceptance, collection, complete delivery, comparable customer baselines, measured outcomes, added-burden accounting, repurchase, a dated comparator record, and founder/company facts.
