This is a dispatch because it requires synthesis across more than three
evidence surfaces, protects the parent from a 3,600-file raw corpus, isolates
candidate ontology organization from authority, and supports parallel
computational, formal-semantic, and ontology-governance investigation.

  - `Lakatos, Imre` attacks precedent and owner placement.

The explorer anti-bias axis is a composite of methodology and source corpus:
computational repository enumeration, formal Lean semantics, and private
ontology governance. The skeptic anti-bias axis is attack vector: precedent,
non-vacuity, and definitional soundness. The exact sheet contains every
pairwise predicted disagreement.

## Dependency flow

The fixed research spine is:

`explorers -> synthesizer <-> skeptics -> writer -> auditor`

The synthesizer-to-skeptics zig-zag has `loop_cap: 2`. A missing exact source
or selector may traverse the already-declared synthesizer-to-explorers feedback
edge once. The auditor may return one bounded revision to the writer. The whole
dispatch has `max_loops: 2`.

## Outputs and successor boundary

- `research.md`: strategist-appended verbatim explorer returns.
- `findings.md`: cited synthesis with exact corpus coverage, semantic map,
  ontology handoff, verdict matrix, owner blockers, and D65 gaps.

The research dispatch does not write an ontology. After accepted findings,
two successor objectives remain separate:

1. Fast-forward the clean nested repository's local `main` to the exact
   verified remote head through a git-authorized route.
2. Select an ontology owner and author a candidate CyberAlchemy-v2 ontology
   through the applicable definition/construction/governance route.
