### Required exact surviving record definitions

- `GitCommit`: one Git commit object identified by its full object ID.
- `PathOccurrence`: tuple `(commit_oid, repository-relative path, blob_oid)` established by that commit’s tree; it is provenance, not semantic independence or authority.
- `SourceBlob`: immutable byte sequence identified by Git blob ID. Equal blobs establish byte identity only, not equal role, owner, or authority.
- `SourceAuthority`: explicit owner-bound designation over exact source bytes and scope. It is never inferred from directory, branch, package, or blob identity.
- `LeanDeclarationSource`: tuple `(commit_oid, path, blob_oid, namespace-qualified declaration name, declaration kind, exact source selector)`. It claims source presence only.
- `AssumptionDependency`: a named axiom/hypothesis dependency attached to a declaration; it must not be represented as a proved proposition.
- `CompiledTarget`: exact package root, target name, toolchain, dependency closure, commit, and source set intended for elaboration. Declaration presence and `defaultTargets` do not instantiate it.
- `BuildReceipt`: immutable result binding command/toolchain, compiled target, commit, inputs, exit status, and logs. It proves only that exact build execution.
- `ProvedProposition`: proposition elaborated by Lean under an exact environment and assumption set, evidenced by a matching build receipt. It is neither product truth nor implementation conformance.
- `ResearchFinding`: analyst-authored bounded claim with exact supporting selectors, method, uncertainty, and claim ceiling. Clustering is a finding, not ontology identity.
- `OntologyCandidate`: digest-bound proposed record with mandatory `record_kind ∈ {concept, relation, invariant, schema, mapping}`, exact definition, provenance, scope, owner unresolved/proposed, contradictions, and authority effect `none`.
- `Projection`: deterministic derived artifact binding input digests, transformation identity, output digest, declared loss, and authority disclaimer. It is not source authority or admission.
- `AdmittedMeaning`: exact ontology-candidate digest plus authorized meaning-owner decision, admitted scope, effective ontology/package identity, and supersession state. No research result, Lean proof, validation, or projection creates it.
- `ImplementationConformance`: independent evidence that exact implementation bytes satisfy an admitted specification. Compilation alone cannot establish it.
- `ProductCapability`: separately adopted product commitment; neither a Lean proposition nor ontology candidate implies one.
- `LocalBranchMovement`: mutation of a local ref/worktree only.
- `Publication`: authorized mutation of a remote/publication surface with an effect receipt. A local fast-forward is not publication.
