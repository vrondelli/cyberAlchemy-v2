# Distill

## One-sentence model

A proposed dual-memory active-learning system routes familiar inputs through
small shared-backbone specialists, escalates ambiguous inputs to an LLM, gathers
examples, trains new specialists, and hypothesizes DNG-based model generation;
category theory currently supplies organizing metaphors plus one reconstructable
free wiring category, not proof of the architecture.

## Compositional spine

`input → pinned ANN candidates → declared wiring path → gate`

`low support → coverage-gap record → curiosity strategy → labels/examples`

`validated training → specialist node + wiring/provenance edges + embedding`

`optional hypothesis → DNG encode/decode → derived specialist or minted model`

## Borrow carefully

- graph/vector separation;
- stable records for instances, gaps, examples and provenance;
- explicit fast/slow escalation;
- separately validated graph update;
- source-versus-derived distinction for minted artifacts;
- active acquisition strategies as experiment candidates.
