## Sharpest residue

R4 and R10 must remain separate. Even a fully lawful category/adjunction would
not show that learned embeddings encode semantic hierarchy. Conversely, a useful
empirical hierarchy proposer need not realize the source’s adjunction claims.

R9 also cannot hide under DNG notation. The source itself labels minting the
“most ambitious capability” and leaves its implementation in roadmap Phase 4
(PDF pp. 23, 32).
