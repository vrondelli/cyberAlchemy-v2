## Classification rule

Every load-bearing item receives exactly one primary audit status:

1. `defined-with-laws` — required data, typing, laws, and at least one witness
   are present.
2. `architecture-interface` — the source gives an operational role or data flow,
   but not a formal or implemented witness.
3. `local-inference` — a conservative reconstruction from source-backed
   material, explicitly not source meaning.
4. `analogy-only` — categorical language offers intuition but its mathematical
   obligations are absent or ill-typed.
5. `open-residue` — evidence, decisions, or tests required for adjudication are
   missing.

`defined-with-laws` is deliberately strict. Notation and a plausible diagram do
not satisfy it.
