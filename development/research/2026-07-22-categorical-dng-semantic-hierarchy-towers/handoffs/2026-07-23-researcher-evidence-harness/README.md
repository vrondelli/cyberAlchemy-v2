## Result Ceiling

This package supports authoring a Research Evidence Harness. It does not
establish or authorize:

- a Ponder adapter or target dataset;
- gold relation, order, ancestor, or facet labels;
- an executable policy;
- a DNG transfer or reproduction;
- semantic hierarchy or parallel-hierarchy alignment;
- a scalar TNAS metric;
- model training, production mutation, publication, or promotion.
