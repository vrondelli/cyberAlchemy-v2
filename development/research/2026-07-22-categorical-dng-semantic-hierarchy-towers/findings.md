**One-line answer:** The sources provide a falsifiable experimental decomposition, not a ready Ponder algorithm: an as-yet-unwritten adapter may serialize a frozen Ponder projection through an authored evaluation schedule and feed a calibrated predicate-candidate scorer; separately specified deterministic policies alone admit typed relations, operational order, or facets; no semantic witness or target benefit exists yet.

The prior bridge remains the governing non-collapse precedent: proximity is not typed relation, operational order is not semantic containment, and a facet view is not an order ([prior bridge findings](../2026-07-22-semantic-hierarchy-bridge/findings.md)). ## 3. Exact definitions and non-collapse rules

- **Authored evaluation schedule**: a versioned total or partial sequence used only to serialize a *frozen* projection for evaluation. It asserts neither semantic order, history, hierarchy, DNG forward-pass equivalence, nor a learned semantic property. Its required counterfactual holds nodes and relations fixed while changing only the declared schedule; an output change is reported as **serialization sensitivity**. This is a target-specific repair of the DNG event-pattern boundary, not a paper-derived ontology meaning ([DNG bridge decisions](towers/dynamic-neural-graph-inference/bridge-decisions.md); [DNG adapter witnesses](towers/dynamic-neural-graph-inference/examples/adapter-witnesses.md)).
- **Predicate-candidate scorer**: a calibrated, abstaining function over a predeclared tuple `(source_id, predicate_id, target_id, direction, projection_digest)`. It emits a score, uncertainty, and evidence reference for a *typed relation proposal only*. It does not accept a relation, create authority, decide order, decide facets, or turn a latent state into semantics. The need for gold predicates, review, calibration, and hard negatives is explicit in the DNG bridge obligations ([DNG bridge decisions](towers/dynamic-neural-graph-inference/bridge-decisions.md)).
- **Accepted relation**: the deterministic outcome of a versioned policy applied to an eligible scored candidate and its evidence envelope. It is never a model output. The score remains evidence for a proposal; acceptance and rejection must be reproducible policy outcomes.
- **Operational precedence**: a relation over process stages or operations only. It does not mean containment, ancestry, abstraction, `is_a`, parenthood, semantic level, or a facet. CLS `composes_after` can at most be input evidence for such a separately governed policy ([CLS bridge decisions](towers/categorical-learning-system/bridge/bridge-decisions.md)).
- **Heterogeneous views**: the first experiment produces one operational-order view and one multi-label facet view. These are heterogeneous views, **not parallel hierarchies**. Genuine parallel hierarchies require a second independently order-bearing view plus an explicit mapping and evidence criterion ([prior bridge findings](../2026-07-22-semantic-hierarchy-bridge/findings.md)).
- **Componentwise view-comparison ledger**: a record of comparable components, denominators, mappings, applicability, drift, and N/A reasons. Do not call it *alignment* unless an explicit correspondence relation, evidence, and criterion exist. Order-versus-facet components are N/A; no aggregate scalar is permitted ([prior bridge findings](../2026-07-22-semantic-hierarchy-bridge/findings.md)).

Category, functor, naturality, adjunction, and growth language is removed from the operative experiment. `Path(G)` may remain as isolated declared-wiring syntax: a free category can reconstruct path concatenation from a typed wiring graph, but it says nothing about neural execution, learned meaning, or hierarchy ([CLS witnesses](towers/categorical-learning-system/examples/smallest-witnesses.md); [CLS notation bridge](towers/categorical-learning-system/notation/notation-bridge.md)). The Lean preservation taxonomy is a negative boundary only: no local source supplies the missing Vect/latent-to-semantics interpretation ([CLS learning pack](towers/categorical-learning-system/learning-pack.md)).

## 4. What each paper contributes

### Categorical Learning System

The proposal contributes bounded *interfaces*: model-neighborhood generation, calibrated coverage-gap bookkeeping, provenance and training-lineage records, directed composition-path proposals, and an operational-path projection after an independent acceptance decision. Its graph/vector split is useful precisely because it does not entitle geometry to become semantics ([CLS bridge decisions](towers/categorical-learning-system/bridge/bridge-decisions.md); [CLS learning pack](towers/categorical-learning-system/learning-pack.md)).

It does **not** provide a relation scorer, parent/ancestor policy, facet policy, hierarchy constructor, cross-view comparator, DNG implementation, semantic latent, or model-minting evidence. Curiosity is out of scope for the first experiment; it may later be tested as a governed data-acquisition policy, with the proposal’s stated policy-gradient sketch treated as incomplete ([CLS claim ledger](towers/categorical-learning-system/claims/claim-ledger.md); [CLS residue](towers/categorical-learning-system/residue/open-residue.md)).

### Dynamic Neural Graph Encoding of Inference Processes

The paper contributes one conditional encoder pattern: ordered, edge-conditioned message passing with recurrent node memory over a representation whose add/delete events have concrete target semantics. In the paper, those events are consecutive neural-network layers and numeric weight/bias representations. The target-side counterpart is not “DNG for ontologies,” but an **ordered recurrent typed-graph encoder** if, and only if, an adapter specifies a frozen projection, tensorization, event schedule, features, labels, and head ([DNG notation bridge](towers/dynamic-neural-graph-inference/notation-bridge.md); [DNG bridge decisions](towers/dynamic-neural-graph-inference/bridge-decisions.md)).

No Ponder adapter currently specifies tensorization, add/delete events, memory, pooling, prediction head, or stage mapping. If a frozen graph is merely serialized without graph changes, it must be called an ordered recurrent typed-graph encoder, not DNG. DNG paper results remain paper-reported rather than rerun; they do not support a target benefit claim ([DNG claim ledger](towers/dynamic-neural-graph-inference/claim-ledger.md); [DNG residue](towers/dynamic-neural-graph-inference/residue/open-residue.md)).

## 6. Corrected algorithm path

The bounded path is an experiment plan, not an implementation claim:

1. **Re-freeze one projection.** Restore or rebind the IOLM core and record a projection digest; do not call an unreplayed snapshot a witness.
2. **Create `PONDER-ONTOLOGY-EVALUATOR-MINIMAL-FIXTURES.v0`.** It must be a proposed fixture package, not Ponder gold data by assertion.
3. **Specify a finite adapter.** Define input schema, tensorization, retained metadata, add/delete behavior (if any), memory, pooling, head, feature/label split, and adapter digest. A graph that only serializes is not DNG.
4. **Author the schedule plus counterfactual.** Publish the schedule’s stages and a fixed-graph alternative schedule; changed outputs are serialization sensitivity.
5. **Score predeclared predicate tuples.** The scorer produces proposal score, uncertainty, and evidence only; unknown is abstention/evaluation, never a negative label.
6. **Apply independent policies.** Relation acceptance, direct-parent/ancestor admission, and facets are three policy outcomes. Cycle rejection is policy conformance, not learned quality.
7. **Project heterogeneous views.** Emit one operational-order view and one multi-label facet view while preserving source/candidate/rejected/unknown states.
8. **Compare applicable components without a scalar.** Produce a componentwise view-comparison ledger; order/facet comparisons are N/A unless a mapping criterion is separately supplied.

## 8. Minimal fixture, experiment, and falsification contract

### Dataset and leakage contract

Maintain separate relation, direct-parent, ancestor, and multi-label facet datasets. Unknown is an abstention/evaluation state, not a negative. Direct-parent and ancestor labels must not leak into one another; no parent/ancestor closure may cross split boundaries. Split groups by source, provenance, and time. Calibrate only on validation. Authority, review, and promotion fields are forbidden model inputs, although they may remain auditable labels or outcome fields ([DNG adapter witnesses](towers/dynamic-neural-graph-inference/examples/adapter-witnesses.md); [DNG bridge decisions](towers/dynamic-neural-graph-inference/bridge-decisions.md)).

### Matched baselines

- typed-rule signature compatibility;
- ANN cosine `k=10`, inverse-distance vote, tie => abstain;
- set-record encoder with mean + max pooling;
- static typed GNN;
- ordered arm with identical fields, head, capacity, and budget, with only ordering changed.

### Local proposed preregistration values

These are proposed local thresholds, **not source facts**: exactly one primary predicate; at least five seeds; at least 25 examples per mandatory hard-negative slice; superiority only if lower paired 95% CI is at least `+0.03 F1`; equivalence only if 90% CI lies within `+/-0.01` and latency or memory improves by at least 20%; `ECE <= 0.05`; non-abstained error `<= 0.10` at coverage `>= 0.80`; and every hard-negative slice has recall and specificity `>= 0.80`.

The ordered arm is falsified and stops if it lacks reproducible material gain, fails leakage/reference controls, fails calibration/selective-risk thresholds, or gains only under the schedule counterfactual. Its DNG-paper precedent cannot rescue a failed target arm ([DNG claim ledger](towers/dynamic-neural-graph-inference/claim-ledger.md)).

### Policy envelopes and view comparison

Every policy must declare input/schema, eligible signatures, threshold, unknown/tie treatment, constraint order, checks, rejection codes, output states, overrides, version, and digest. View comparison must declare formulas, denominators, mapping, applicability, drift, and N/A reasons. It must not aggregate components. These requirements preserve the prior bridge’s non-collapse boundary ([prior bridge findings](../2026-07-22-semantic-hierarchy-bridge/findings.md)).
