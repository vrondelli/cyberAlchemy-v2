# 80VinteBot Friend Pilot Offer Findings

## Decision

**Interview first. Make no pilot offer now. All three candidates are `KILL pending discovery`.** The leading discovery hypothesis is a bounded synthetic configuration comparison: a check of one AI-agent setup, using made-up conversations and no production activity, to help an authorized 80VinteBot owner decide what to do with that setup. It is not yet executable because no named configuration, pending decision, authority, comparison baseline, access path, or joined synthetic trace has been witnessed. (Hewitt, **Evidence Boundary**, **Concrete Workflow Trace**, and **Recommendation: Smallest Non-Vacuous Pilot**, [research transcript](/home/vrondelli/projects/domainspec-core/cyberAlchemy-v2/development/research/2026-07-16-80vintebot-friend-pilot-offer/research.md); [80VinteBot product](https://www.80vintebot.com.br/))

CyberAlchemy's possible contribution is a governed decision record and explicit evidence boundary, not novel evaluation technology. NIST already supplies documented testing and go/no-go precedent; discovery must establish whether CyberAlchemy adds decision evidence beyond 80VinteBot's current machinery. (Hewitt, **Evidence Boundary** and **Candidate Classes: AI Sales-Agent Behavior/Release Assurance**, [research transcript](/home/vrondelli/projects/domainspec-core/cyberAlchemy-v2/development/research/2026-07-16-80vintebot-friend-pilot-offer/research.md); [NIST AI RMF Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/))

## Customer Problem And Decision Chain

**Plain-language hypothesis:** before changing one agent setup, the authorized owner may lack one joined record showing what changed, how the current and proposed setups handled the same made-up conversations, what each finding means, and why the owner chose to proceed, hold, or retest. Discovery must confirm that this is a real problem and that existing evaluation machinery does not already solve it. (Hewitt, **Concrete Workflow Trace** and **Recommendation: Smallest Non-Vacuous Pilot**, [research transcript](/home/vrondelli/projects/domainspec-core/cyberAlchemy-v2/development/research/2026-07-16-80vintebot-friend-pilot-offer/research.md))

- **Before:** name the decision, authorized body, current configuration, proposed configuration, requirements, same-case comparison baseline, and owner's pre-review decision.
- **Intervention:** run both frozen configurations against identical approved synthetic cases; record input, response, qualification, proposed CRM action, escalation, signed record fields, environment, and timestamps; execute no production action.
- **After:** the same authorized body records `proceed`, `hold`, or `retest`, reasons, evidence used, disputes, unresolved items, burden, and deletion confirmation. A changed decision is not necessarily a better decision.

(Hewitt, **Candidate Classes: AI Sales-Agent Behavior/Release Assurance**; Goldratt, **Recommendation** and **Measurement Protocol**, [research transcript](/home/vrondelli/projects/domainspec-core/cyberAlchemy-v2/development/research/2026-07-16-80vintebot-friend-pilot-offer/research.md))

## Discovery Interview

1. What real decision is approaching: change an agent setup, accept an onboarding, or retain, qualify, or remove a claim?
2. What exact object, configuration, and version does it concern?
3. Who is authorized to decide, and whose additional approval is required?
4. What observable behaviors, records, operational definitions, and tolerances must exist before that decision?
5. What evaluation, staging, logging, and version-comparison machinery already exists?
6. Can the mystery-shopper scenarios, rubric, report, or WhatsApp execution machinery be reused safely against an 80VinteBot agent with no production messages or writes?
7. What decision evidence would CyberAlchemy add beyond the current report or an incumbent evaluation platform?
8. Can current and proposed configurations run identical synthetic cases with requirements, evaluator rules, and environment frozen?
9. Can input, response, qualification, proposed action, escalation, record fields, and timestamps be joined without production credentials or effects?
10. Which fields are permitted, who may access them, and who confirms deletion on what date?
11. What time caps apply by role to founder, Product, Engineering, CS, and CyberAlchemy?
12. What result would cause stop, rejection, or refusal of another run?

(Christensen, **Exact Discovery Questions**; Hewitt, **Candidate Classes**; Goldratt, **Gates**, [research transcript](/home/vrondelli/projects/domainspec-core/cyberAlchemy-v2/development/research/2026-07-16-80vintebot-friend-pilot-offer/research.md); [mystery shopper](https://agencia80vinte.com.br/cliente-oculto))

Discovery ends with **no offer** unless every required witness below can be instantiated.

## Conditional Scope And Exclusions

Only after `GO` may the parties consider one current and one proposed agent configuration, a proposed cap of 12 synthetic cases, one proposed CRM action, one human-escalation path, a proposed 10-business-day calendar cap, and a proposed four-hour customer cap. These caps are hypotheses, not demonstrated feasibility. One retest is comparable only if configuration, requirements, cases, evaluator rules, and environment remain frozen; otherwise it is a new run. ## No-Fee Exchange And Safeguards

- **Exchange:** a signed zero-fee memo sets proposed calendar and burden caps, one pre-freeze clarification, bounded clarification support, access conditions, measurement, and fault-free exit. Free work does not purchase praise, referral, case-study rights, or future work. (Goldratt, **Fair No-Fee Exchange**, [UK pilot checklist](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/unlocking-space-for-investment-growth-hub/track-3-pilot-to-production-checklist))
- **Data:** synthetic identities and permitted fields only; named access; purpose limitation; no unrelated reuse or model training; defined retention and deletion confirmer. Necessity, security, and accountability are handling requirements, not an LGPD compliance approval. (Goldratt, **Fair No-Fee Exchange**, [LGPD principles](https://www.gov.br/saude/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/lgpd/principios))
- **IP and confidentiality:** each party retains background IP; 80VinteBot retains its data and configurations; CyberAlchemy retains methods and templates; the customer receives internal-use rights to the final deliverable; access is need-to-know and system findings remain confidential; jointly created material requires advance definition. (Goldratt, **Fair No-Fee Exchange**, [WIPO collaboration guidance](https://www.wipo.int/web-publications/wipo-guide-to-trade-secrets-and-innovation/en/part-vi-trade-secrets-in-collaborative-innovation.html))
- **Authority:** only the authorized 80VinteBot person or body decides what to do. CyberAlchemy records evidence and recommends; it does not release, approve, certify, or authorize. (Hewitt, **Candidate Classes: AI Sales-Agent Behavior/Release Assurance**, [research transcript](/home/vrondelli/projects/domainspec-core/cyberAlchemy-v2/development/research/2026-07-16-80vintebot-friend-pilot-offer/research.md))
- **Friendship:** either party may stop without fault or publicity. Case-study permission is separate, optional, post-receipt, mutually worded, and not evidence of payment, usefulness, endorsement, or demand. (Goldratt, **Fair No-Fee Exchange** and **Bias And Prohibited Claims**, [rapport study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8535677/))

## Conditional Pilot Stages

1. **Discover:** identify the decision, object, authority, current machinery, access conditions, burden, and added evidence. Close with no offer if any essential element is absent.
2. **Authorize and freeze:** sign `AUTH-01`; freeze versions, fields, cases, requirements, evaluator rules, environment, tolerances, metrics, and caps.
3. **Establish baseline:** run the current configuration on the same synthetic cases and record the owner's pre-review decision.
4. **Compare and capture:** run the proposed configuration, record the bounded trace, and make no production write or message.
5. **Adjudicate:** the domain owner classifies findings; the decision body records its post-review decision and reasons.
6. **Close:** validate delivery, use, outcomes, total burden, limits, stop/repeat status, and deletion; do not expand from this friend run.

(Hewitt, **Recommendation: Smallest Non-Vacuous Pilot**; Goldratt, **Recommendation**, **Measurement Protocol**, and **Gates**, [research transcript](/home/vrondelli/projects/domainspec-core/cyberAlchemy-v2/development/research/2026-07-16-80vintebot-friend-pilot-offer/research.md))

## Measurement And Gates

Keep two baselines distinct. The **comparison baseline** is the current configuration running the same synthetic cases; it is mandatory, and its absence means `STOP`. The **improvement baseline** is historical comparable decision work: elapsed time, minutes by role, evidence-search time, revision rounds, and rework. Without it, report only delivery and owner use, not operational improvement. (Goldratt, **Measurement Protocol**, [Magenta Book](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-magenta-book/magenta-book-central-government-guidance-on-evaluation-html))

The counterfactual is the competent current process. Record the owner's decision before and after the review and the reasons. A single pre/post or historical comparison is descriptive and vulnerable to confounding, not causal proof. (Goldratt, **Measurement Protocol**, [Magenta Book](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-magenta-book/magenta-book-central-government-guidance-on-evaluation-html))

Metrics remain separate: delivery; owner use and contribution; operational time/burden/search/revision/rework; unsolicited repeat request; actual referral; and signed, collected payment. Do not create a composite trust or decision-quality score. Do not use false-positive or false-negative labels without preregistered independent reference answers and scoring rules; use `owner-confirmed`, `owner-disputed`, `unresolved`, and `later-discovered in-scope miss`. (Hewitt, **Candidate Classes: AI Sales-Agent Behavior/Release Assurance**; Goldratt, **Measurement Protocol**, [research transcript](/home/vrondelli/projects/domainspec-core/cyberAlchemy-v2/development/research/2026-07-16-80vintebot-friend-pilot-offer/research.md))

Total burden includes customer and CyberAlchemy minutes by role for access/security, preparation, cases, meetings, specialist adjudication, coordination, revision, adoption, rework, and closeout. Monetize only with customer-approved rates. Confounders include friendship, courtesy, founder attention, novelty/Hawthorne effects, easy-case selection, simultaneous prompt/model/API changes, synthetic-versus-production differences, changing requirements, unusually good documentation, staff learning, workload, and evaluator-authored tests. (Goldratt, **Measurement Protocol** and **Bias And Prohibited Claims**, [rapport study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8535677/))

- **`KILL_PENDING_DISCOVERY`:** current verdict for all candidates.
- **`GO_TO_PROPOSAL`:** every artifact has a name, owner, stable ID, version, operational definition, tolerance, and required signature; observable access and burden conditions are accepted.
- **`STOP`:** no comparison baseline or authority; production credentials, messages, or writes become necessary; non-permitted data appears; burden cap is breached; required record fields or deletion confirmation are absent; or audit, assurance, certification, compliance, security, or legal-approval expectations arise.
- **`REPEAT_USE`:** record only an unsolicited request as continuation interest.
- **`OPERATIONAL_REPEAT`:** one preregistered operational measure improves against the improvement baseline after total burden, with no material in-scope miss discovered during the observation period and no tolerance breach.
- **`EXPAND`:** prohibited from this friend pilot alone.

(Goldratt, **Gates** and **Bias And Prohibited Claims**, [research transcript](/home/vrondelli/projects/domainspec-core/cyberAlchemy-v2/development/research/2026-07-16-80vintebot-friend-pilot-offer/research.md))

## Explicit Non-Claims

This work cannot establish production reliability, product correctness, audit coverage, assurance, security properties, compliance, certification, legal approval, unbiased behavior, ROI, savings, avoided loss, revenue protection, revenue impact, conversion impact, market demand, willingness to pay, or performance beyond the tested configurations, cases, evaluator rules, and environment. A proposed CRM action does not witness production execution; record completeness means only completeness against the pilot's signed fields. (Hewitt, **Candidate Classes: Cannot Prove**; Goldratt, **Measurement Protocol** and **Bias And Prohibited Claims**, [research transcript](/home/vrondelli/projects/domainspec-core/cyberAlchemy-v2/development/research/2026-07-16-80vintebot-friend-pilot-offer/research.md))

**A free pilot cannot validate willingness to pay, ROI, demand, certification, compliance, or revenue impact.** Free acceptance, friendship, owner use, a changed decision, report polish, case-study consent, repeat interest, and referrals cannot substitute for a signed and collected purchase or broader independent evidence. (Goldratt, **Measurement Protocol** and **Bias And Prohibited Claims**, [AEA zero-price review](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257%2Fjep.23.3.21), [NBER WTP study](https://www.nber.org/papers/w30433))
