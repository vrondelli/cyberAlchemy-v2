# 80VinteBot Friend Pilot Offer Research Strategy

## Goal

Determine the strongest zero-fee CyberAlchemy pilot to offer 80VinteBot that
addresses one consequential current workflow, can run as a bounded shadow-mode
engagement, produces measurable delivery and customer-value evidence, and
protects relationship, data, IP, decision authority, and claim boundaries.

The result must contain one recommended offer, ranked alternatives, explicit
non-claims, discovery questions, exchange terms, a pilot protocol, metrics,
stop/go/expand gates, and a plain-language founder conversation.

## Evidence Boundary

Public evidence presents 80VinteBot as a small Brazilian AI sales and
communications SaaS combining multichannel AI agents, CRM and pipeline actions,
onboarding, training, optimization, and a free AI mystery-shopper funnel. No
private workflow, access, pain, consent, buyer authority, internal architecture,
or willingness to pay is witnessed.

A no-fee pilot may test delivery, usefulness, burden, measured operational
change, repeat use, or referral. It cannot validate willingness to pay.

## P1 Trigger

The dispatch is justified by synthesis, context protection, isolation, and
parallelism. Customer desirability, technical assurance feasibility, and
constraint-first pilot economics require independent research before synthesis.

## Lanes And Agents

### Explorers - parallel

- `Christensen, Clayton`: empirical customer-workflow and jobs-to-be-done
  analysis over the current public product, method, pricing, mystery-shopper,
  social, and legal footprint.
- `Hewitt, Carl`: adversarial end-to-end AI-agent behavior, release, data,
  tool-action, escalation, and third-party dependency analysis.
- `Goldratt, Eliyahu`: constraint-first no-fee pilot economics and
  relationship-safe exchange design.

The explorer anti-bias axis is a composite of methodology and source corpus,
specialized to customer desirability, technical feasibility, and operational
economics. Every pair carries an explicit predicted disagreement in the
registered prompts.

### Synthesis

`Rosen, Robert` reconciles explorer returns into a candidate matrix and
recommends exactly one pilot only if it survives. If public evidence is
insufficient, the output becomes an interview-first route.

### Reviewers - parallel

- `Popper, Karl`: precedent gate.
- `Gigerenzer, Gerd`: non-vacuity gate.
- `Quine, Willard Van Orman`: definitional-soundness gate.

The reviewer anti-bias axis is attack vector. Precedent never kills a candidate;
only a missing concrete witness or definitional collapse may do so.

### Writing And Audit

- `Senge, Peter`: writes `findings.md` only.
- `Brandenburg, Martin`: audits `findings.md` only and owns final approval.

## Dependency Flow

`explorers -> synthesizer <-> reviewers -> writer -> auditor`

One synthesizer-reviewer zig-zag and one auditor-writer revision are allowed.

## Candidate Classes

1. AI sales-agent behavior and release assurance.
2. Client onboarding and configuration repeatability.
3. Product, privacy, performance, and customer-report claim-to-evidence review.
