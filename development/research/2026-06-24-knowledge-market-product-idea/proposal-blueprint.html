Executive Brief

CyberAlchemy Evidence Exchange is a local-first exchange/workbench for reusable knowledge labor. It lets individuals, groups, projects, local AI nodes, and governed ontologies register reusable work, observe when that work is used, review the claimed relationship, and seed credit for future recognition or reward.

The product is not a generic knowledge marketplace, truth market, or DAO payout layer. It is a governed evidence exchange: a place where a project can say, "this artifact, ontology, research tower, domain-logic module, consulting package, or review actually helped produce that downstream artifact, and here is the receipt."

The strategic move is to separate reuse, credit, and authority. Reuse says a downstream work used an upstream work. Credit says reviewers accepted or revised the contribution weight. Authority says an owner route promoted something into relied-on CyberAlchemy authority.

The first proof should be small: one source work W0 is retrieved through a governed local AI surface, contributes to downstream artifact W1, emits a candidate ObservedUseReceipt, gets reviewed, and updates credit seeds while authority remains unchanged.

Long-term: local AI nodes share, retrieve, distribute, and improve governed knowledge across groups and individuals. Tokens may later fund review, reward distribution, or contribution-backed project capital only after hard readiness gates exist.

The Frustration

A team finishes a strong artifact with help from old research, a consultant's domain model, a group ontology, a prior project, and a local AI node that selected the right context. The final artifact looks like one new output. The shoulders disappear.

Knowledge does not compound visibly

Reusable work is copied, summarized, or invoked, but the system cannot show what made the new work possible.

Contribution and authority collapse

Useful work can start feeling authoritative without owner-routed promotion.

Rewards come too early

Without receipts, review, privacy, and anti-gaming controls, payout logic rewards theater.

Non-research labor is under-modeled

Made projects, domain logic, consulting, maintenance, challenge, and packaging work need credit surfaces too.

A Day With It

A group is building a compliance workflow for a client. Their local AI node retrieves a Tower substrate that organizes the domain, a prior project artifact with tested decision logic, and a group ontology that names the client's roles, controls, and evidence types.

The team saves a downstream artifact: W1-client-compliance-routing-spec. The host emits candidate receipts saying which upstream works were used as context, which local AI node retrieved them, which run produced the draft, and which downstream artifact digest was saved.

A reviewer opens the workbench. One edge is accepted, one is revised because the context was useful but minor, and one is deferred because the ontology included private client material that cannot be exported. The accepted and revised edges update credit seeds. Nothing is promoted to authority. Nothing is paid out.

The first proof must show one artifact-to-artifact reuse path with host-observed evidence, privacy-safe references, a review action, and a credit seed that has no authority effect.

Why Chase This

Most knowledge markets start with price, reputation, or content inventory. Evidence Exchange starts with governed reuse: who used what, through which surface, with which privacy policy, reviewed by whom, with what authority effect.

Why it is credible

  • CyberAlchemy already separates evidence, ontology, research, generated projections, implementation success, and repetition from authority movement.
  • Inventory already gives a reusable lookup substrate for candidate evidence sets and synthesis without making those read models authority.
  • The Evidence Exchange finding already converges on a narrow thesis, core loop, credit-first model, MVP sequence, and token adapter gates.
  • Tower System creates a high-value candidate asset class: source-backed domain substrates and persona candidates.

Why it is still a bet

  • Host-observed reuse has to feel natural in a real local AI workflow.
  • Credit allocation has to be explainable, conserved, and reviewable before tokens exist.
  • Tower assets, persona candidates, and node-to-opposable-stance behavior remain witness-gated.
  • Privacy and redaction must show lineage without leaking protected context.

System Blueprint

Evidence Exchange has two ledgers and one bridge. The credit ledger records participation. The authority ledger records owner-routed promotion. The handoff bridge raises attention without promoting anything by itself.

Credit ledger

  • Reuse, review, maintenance, packaging, challenge, and distribution.
  • Observed reuse credit, verification credit, maintenance credit, packaging credit, distribution credit, novelty seed, and review confidence.
  • Useful before money, with conserved budgets and review rationale.

Authority ledger

  • Owner-routed promotion, rejection, retirement, contradiction, and projection.
  • Definitions governance, constitution governance, discipline governance, decision gates, and promotion policy.
  • Unaffected by use count, token weight, score, price, or ontology placement.
Register W0
  -> retrieve W0 through governed local AI node
  -> produce and save W1
  -> emit candidate ObservedUseReceipt
  -> apply privacy/export policy
  -> reviewer accepts, revises, rejects, or defers edge
  -> update credit seed
  -> optionally raise authority handoff request
  -> optionally route future reward adapter after gates

Verification Plan

Question Method Pass signal Fail signal
Can host-observed reuse be captured without fake precision? Build or simulate one governed local AI retrieval path from W0 to W1. Receipt has stable IDs, digests, actor/run metadata, privacy policy, trust label, and review status. Receipt is manual attribution, unverifiable, too invasive, or too hard to create.
Can the user understand the product before tokens are mentioned? Prototype the workbench around lineage, review, and credit seeds only. User can explain what built on what and what got reviewed without payout language. User asks first about price, tokens, ownership, or marketplace listings.
Can credit stay separate from authority? Create a fixture where a highly reused artifact requests authority attention. Workbench raises an authority handoff but does not promote. Score, use count, ontology placement, or reviewer majority changes authority status.
Can privacy-safe lineage be useful? Export a receipt view with private source text, prompts, client data, memory, and wallet context redacted. Viewer understands the relationship without seeing protected content. Redaction removes all value or leaks protected context.
Can Tower assets earn credit only after witness proof? Run Tower D1/D2/D3 witness design against one candidate substrate. Tower contribution is credited only through witnessed query/persona/stance/verdict behavior. Tower artifact earns credit by reputation or label alone.
Can future rewards be deferred without weakening the product? Test recognition, maintenance priority, review sponsorship, and validation funding as non-token incentives. Contributors still see value in credit and review attention. Users treat the system as useless without immediate payout.

MVP Or Stage Sequence

  1. Credit-only lineage witness. Prove saved artifacts can show what they built on, where they were reused, which evidence supports the edge, and which trust tier applies.
  2. Evidence Exchange workbench. Register work, inspect receipts, apply privacy-safe export, and let reviewers accept, revise, reject, or defer edges.
  3. Review, challenge, and credit seeds. Add rationale refs, confidence labels, conserved credit budgets, duplicate-path checks, reciprocal-review checks, and challenge paths.
  4. Authority handoff lane. Show when high-credit or strongly reviewed work deserves owner attention while routing promotion through CyberAlchemy gates.
  5. Tower witness track. Add Tower substrates, personas, stance formation, scoring, and verdict records only after witness proof.
  6. Optional reward adapter gate. Explore tokens, payout, reward pools, or knowledge-as-project-capital only after the graph is valuable without money and all hard gates are passed.

Risks And Non-Goals

Risks

  • Observation risk: work outside governed host surfaces cannot honestly be rewarded.
  • Privacy risk: lineage can leak private material unless export policy is first-class.
  • Gaming risk: self-declared edges, collusion, reciprocal review, synthetic novelty, spam, and clustered reviewers can inflate credit.
  • Double-counting risk: ancestor chains can overclaim unless credit is conserved, damped, and deduped.
  • Token/legal risk: payout, DAO flows, tax, securities, and money transmission can reshape or block rewards.

Non-goals

  • No truth market, authority market, generic DAO marketplace, or Tower rebrand.
  • No authority-by-score, token, price, usage, ontology, or popularity.
  • No token or payout MVP.
  • No public marketplace before privacy, consent, attribution, legal, identity, and anti-gaming controls exist.
  • No raw prompt, private memory, wallet context, page content, client data, or source text export by default.

Next Decisions

  1. Decide the first host-observed surface: Living Archive context use, delegated task reads/returns, Augmentor context use, ResonantOS local node retrieval, or Tower witness track.
  2. Freeze the minimum candidate ObservedUseReceipt fields for a Stage 0 fixture.
  3. Decide the first asset class: project artifact, Work node, domain logic module, consulting package, individual ontology, group ontology, Tower substrate candidate, or review/verdict record.
  4. Define the privacy/export policy before any public or contributor-facing view.
  5. Choose initial reviewer actions, trust labels, confidence labels, and credit conservation parameters.
  6. Decide whether CyberAlchemy Evidence Exchange should remain product language or enter definitions-governance as a formal term.
  7. Keep reward adapter design blocked until the credit graph proves useful without money.
