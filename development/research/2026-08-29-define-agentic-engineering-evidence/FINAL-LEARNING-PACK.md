# Final Learning Pack

## Start Here

The most defensible present statement is:

> Recent research gives us a strong reason to test Define as a task-specific,
> structured meaning layer connected to evidence. It does not show that generic
> documentation helps, and it does not yet prove that CyberAlchemy Define works.

## What Changed In Our Model

Before this tower, the natural comparison was Define versus freeform work. That
comparison is too weak. A positive result could come from extra information or
extra attention.

The tower therefore introduces four conditions:

1. ticket only;
2. the same selected facts as prose notes;
3. the same facts as a Definition Pack; and
4. the same pack plus runtime evidence gating.

This makes three questions visible: information, structure, and enforcement.

## Product Meaning Preserved

- Define owns clarification and the Definition Pack.
- The evidence gate is a separate candidate runtime behavior.
- A pack may expose open meaning; it need not manufacture closure.
- Implement, abstain, ask, and escalate may all be correct outcomes.
- The experiment measures downstream use, not document completeness.

## Evidence We Should Observe

- plan fidelity to goal, intent, context, concepts, ownership, and tests;
- hidden-case implementation correctness;
- semantic defects and scope violations;
- unnecessary changes and incorrect abstention;
- owner corrections, review rejection, and rework;
- human comprehension and ability to extend the work;
- total time, tokens, and artifact length.

## Honest Stop

The prepared schemas, joined validators, regressions, synthetic fixtures, and
Node 26-derived validation container prove only that the experiment can record,
join, reject known integrity failures, and calculate these observations. A live
pilot still requires task selection, execution identity, reviewers,
privacy/cost boundaries, and a separate owner decision.
