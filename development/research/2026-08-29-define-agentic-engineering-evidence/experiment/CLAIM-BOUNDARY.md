# Claim Boundary

## Before A Live Run

Permitted:

> The research corpus and dry-run mechanics support running a controlled Define
> pilot.

Blocked:

- Define improves plans or implementations.
- Define saves time or increases productivity.
- The wizard, Definition Pack, or evidence gate is effective.
- The prepared test evaluates the full Define workflow; it evaluates the
  Definition Pack representation and a separate gate, not interactive
  questioning or owner decision quality.
- Synthetic metrics are customer or product evidence.

## After One Valid Pilot

At most:

> Under the named tasks, model, harness, reviewers, and protocol, condition X
> differed from condition Y by the reported task-level results.

Still blocked:

- general developer productivity;
- customer value or product-market fit;
- causal attribution when information parity or condition separation failed;
- cross-model, cross-language, professional-team, or longitudinal claims;
- changing the product thesis, canonical definitions, or KPIs automatically.

## Promotion Rule

Any claim update requires a separate owner review of validated live data,
protocol deviations, counterevidence, and replication limits.
