# Tower Model

Status: `closed-local-research-prepared-experiment`

Authority effect: `none`

## Research Question

Does a task-specific Definition Pack help a downstream planner and implementer
preserve goal, intent, context, concepts, evidence, ownership, open questions,
and permission limits better than:

1. the original ticket alone; and
2. the same additional facts presented as ordinary prose notes?

A fourth condition tests a separate question: whether runtime enforcement of
the pack's evidence obligations adds value beyond the pack itself.

## Working Hypothesis

A Definition Pack is useful when it changes observable downstream behavior:
fewer semantic mistakes, fewer hidden assumptions, less owner correction, and
better human understanding at acceptable total cost.

This is a hypothesis to test. A complete-looking pack is not evidence that the
hypothesis is true.

## Competing Explanations

| Branch | Explanation | Discriminating comparison |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | The ticket already contains enough meaning. | ticket only versus equivalent notes |
| B | More information helps, regardless of structure. | equivalent notes versus Definition Pack |
| C | Define's explicit structure preserves meaning across handoff. | Definition Pack versus equivalent notes |
| D | Runtime enforcement, not the pack, produces the benefit. | Definition Pack versus pack plus evidence gate |
| E | Any gain comes from extra time, attention, or document length. | total-cost and information-parity controls |

## Levels

### L0 — corpus

Freeze the 2026 arXiv evidence, clearly labeled 2025 foundations, current local
Define meaning, product outcomes, and E10 predecessor experiment.

### L1 — residue

Separate what the sources demonstrate from what remains unknown: generic
context versus task-specific meaning, information versus structure, structure
versus enforcement, completion versus understanding, and mergeability versus
causal quality.

### L2 — empirical handoff

Prepare a four-condition, paired-by-task experiment with frozen inputs, blinded
review, append-only records, synthetic pass/fail fixtures, metric calculations,
and an explicit live-run gate.

## Source Labels

- `primary-source`: directly reported by an included study or local source;
- `related-source`: a relevant source outside the central empirical set;
- `local-inference`: a conclusion drawn for this research question;
- `operator-reading`: practical guidance for Define;
- `analogy`: a useful comparison that is not an identity;
- `open-residue`: an unanswered empirical or ownership question.

## Closure Target

The tower closes when another operator can:

1. trace every load-bearing research statement to a source;
2. distinguish the Definition Pack from the evidence gate;
3. explain the four experiment conditions and what each comparison isolates;
4. validate one synthetic passing record and at least two failing records; and
5. identify the separate approval required before a live run.
