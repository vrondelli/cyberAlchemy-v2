# Standard Distill — Define Outcome Experiment

Mode: `Standard`

Proposal tracks: `1`

Recursive rounds: `2`

## Objective And Output

- **Seed point:** “Does Define make plans and implementations better?”
- **Target context:** first bounded CyberAlchemy Define product experiment.
- **Output artifact:** one experiment contract plus validated dry-run evidence
  mechanics.
- **Optimization goal:** isolate Define's contribution without confusing it
  with more information, runtime enforcement, or document length.

## Discovery Baseline

- The Define package already states product meaning and a Definition Pack
  contract.
- E10 already establishes a controlled-comparison precedent.
- Recent studies support evidence-conditioned work and expose failures in
  generic context, action bias, human repair, and understanding.
- No source directly proves CyberAlchemy Define's outcome.

## Round 1 — Broad Layer Split

**Proposer claim:** test the full Define workflow, Definition Pack, interactive
questions, and evidence-gated execution together against freeform work.

**Evidence/assumption:** the product value may emerge from the complete workflow.

**Balancer objection category — causal attribution:** a positive result could
not tell whether more information, structure, questioning, or enforcement
caused the change.

**Reconciliation:** separate information, structure, and enforcement into four
conditions. Keep the interactive wizard outside this first experiment unless it
is applied equally while producing the notes and pack.

## Round 2 — Smallest Coherent Unit

**Proposer claim:** one task across four conditions is the smallest unit that
preserves causal contrasts.

**Evidence/assumption:** every condition can share one frozen task, source
packet, fact-set manifest, hidden truth card, and review rubric.

**Balancer objection category — generalization and stochasticity:** one task
cannot support a stable product claim.

**Reconciliation:** retain one four-condition task block as the smallest unit,
but require a six-block pilot with two negative controls and four semantic
pressure cases before any continuation decision.

## Selected Optimization Point

```text
four-condition task block
    x six frozen tasks
    -> paired task-level pilot
```

Smaller loses the ability to isolate structure or enforcement. Larger adds
models, interfaces, and organizational workflows before the central product
claim is tested.

## Recomposition Proof

The Definition Pack is a CyberAlchemy product output. Better plan fidelity,
fewer implementation semantic defects, lower reconstruction burden, and better
understanding are observable parts of the existing reviewable-next-step and
work-continuity outcome chain. The experiment therefore tests one bounded arrow
in the product thesis without claiming to validate the full product.
