# L2 — Closure Plan

## Documentary Closure

1. Freeze source records and local candidate hashes.
2. Separate direct claims, inferences, operator readings, and residue.
3. Distill the broad product hypothesis to one experimental intervention.
4. Prepare four information-controlled conditions.
5. Define metrics for plan quality, implementation quality, human burden,
   understanding, and cost.
6. Validate the run schema with synthetic pass and fail fixtures.
7. Stop before selecting live tasks, models, reviewers, or spending budget.

## Live-Run Handoff

A later owner must freeze:

- six tasks: two low-ambiguity controls and four high-ambiguity tasks;
- one source packet and hidden truth card per task;
- task-class justification written before condition assignment;
- one model, harness, tool version, and repository commit;
- clean planner and implementer sessions;
- two blinded reviewers;
- randomization seed and assignment manifest;
- time, token, privacy, and external-effect limits.

No live row may be appended until the pass fixture returns zero and every fail
fixture returns nonzero for its expected rule.
