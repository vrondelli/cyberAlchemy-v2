## Core research question

Can Way Clear be generalized as an ontological readiness projection across
`Definition`, `Criterion`, `Requirement`, `DefinitionOfDone`, `Architecture`,
`Design`, and `UXPlan` without treating those artifact kinds as interchangeable?

The candidate being tested is not `WayClear(artifact)` as a global completeness
claim. It is closer to:

```text
WayClear(artifact, closureProfile, transition, scopeDeclaration, snapshot)
  -> reason-complete, digest-bound receipt
```

The receipt would project over evidence already admitted by the relevant owners.
It would not manufacture truth, validate evidence by itself, grant approval,
complete execution, promote an artifact, or authorize release.

The central hypothesis is:

> One shared obligation/evidence/dependency substrate can support distinct,
> artifact-kind ClosureProfiles. Clear means that no in-scope blocking reason
> remains for one named transition at one immutable snapshot—not that the world,
> artifact, or program is universally complete.

The explorer group runs in parallel.

2. **Simon, Herbert — lifecycle and acceptance**

3. **Alexander, Christopher — architecture, design, and UX evidence**
   - Models concerns, viewpoints, decisions, alternatives, rationale, risks,

   - Separates deterministic evidence from review and human findings.
   - Anti-bias role: challenges claims of universal machine closure where context
     or human evidence remains necessary.

Every explorer must bind load-bearing external claims to official primary
technical sources or primary research. Inventory summaries are discovery leads,

3. **Quine, Willard Van Orman — definitional non-collapse**

## Candidate ontology boundary

The research will evaluate, not assume, the following shared candidates:

- entities: `Artifact`, `ArtifactKind`, `StatementKind`, `Obligation`, `Decision`,
  `Claim`, `Evidence`, `EvidenceClass`, `Owner`, `AuthorityEffect`,
  `LifecycleState`, `ScopeDeclaration`, `ClosureProfile`, `ClosureQuery`,
  `ReasonCode`, and `WayClearReceipt`;
- relations: `Dependency`, `Refines`, `Satisfies`, `Covers`, `ConflictsWith`,
  `BlockedBy`, `VerifiedBy`, and `InvalidatedBy`; and
- epistemic/lifecycle candidates: `open`, `fog`, `closed`, `stale`,
  `superseded`, and `out_of_scope`.

These names are candidate vocabulary only. They are not new canonical definitions.
The research must merge, rename, defer, or kill them when live owners, formal
semantics, or fixtures do not support a distinct concept.

The seven profiles must remain separate:

| ClosureProfile | Distinct load-bearing question |
|---|---|
| Definition | Is meaning sufficiently bounded, non-circular, owned, and usable in the named scope? |
| Criterion | Is there a decidable or explicitly judged observable with a named evidence class? |
| Requirement | Is the normative obligation identified, traceable, verifiable, scoped, and dispositioned? |
| DefinitionOfDone | Is completion defined by non-self-certifying evidence and the correct owner boundary? |
| Architecture | Are concerns, viewpoints, decisions, tensions, dependencies, and consequences dispositioned for the named phase? |
| Design | Are requirements, rationale, alternatives, constraints, risks, and validation obligations bound? |
| UXPlan | Are research questions, participants or evidence sources, measures, accessibility boundaries, risks, and decision owners explicit? |

This makes Ontological Way Clear a profile-indexed query over typed relations. It
does not create a single governed-artifact supertype with one universal checklist.

## Finite witness and evidence closure

The candidate must survive a paper-executable graph containing all seven artifact
kinds and at least:

- one positive all-clear dependency chain;
- circular-definition and missing-observable mutants;
- unverifiable-requirement and self-certifying-DoD mutants;
- unresolved-architecture-tension and unbound-design-rationale mutants;
- UX-plan missing-question and human-evidence-boundary mutants;
- stale receipt, conflicting owner, out-of-scope, contradiction, and open-world
  unknown cases; and
- both false-clear and false-block expectations.

Each verdict must be deterministic for an immutable input snapshot, return every
in-scope remaining reason, preserve stable ordering, bind source and rule digests,
and leave source state unchanged. A surviving primitive or relation must change at
least one discriminating verdict.

Evidence closure is `exact-binding`: each load-bearing positive row must carry an
immutable source identity, exact selector or structured pointer, explorer-return
reference, evidence scope, and `observed`, `executed`, or `unverified` proof status.
Unresolved evidence cannot be silently upgraded into clear.
