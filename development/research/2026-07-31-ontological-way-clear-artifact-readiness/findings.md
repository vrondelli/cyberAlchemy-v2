# Goal

Determine whether and how a generic ontological Way Clear can provide reason-complete, evidence-bound readiness across `Definition`, `Criterion`, `Requirement`, `DefinitionOfDone`, `Architecture`, `Design`, and `UXPlan` without collapsing their meanings, owners, evidence classes, or lifecycle gates.

# TL;DR

**Paper-evaluated final verdict — KEEP, RENAME, MERGE, KILL, AND DEFER.** A thin private candidate survives as **Evidence-Bound Evaluation Query (`EBEQ`)**: a pure, exact-bound computation over one `ResourceSnapshot`, one purpose-specific `EvaluationProfile`, an evidence baseline, an evaluator, a declared finite scope, and a named purpose. It returns an append-only `EvaluationReceipt` with a complete component/reason account under those bindings and the invariant `authority_effect: none`. It is neither an ontology nor a universal readiness predicate. [Proposed, paper-evaluated: [repair §2](receipts/ontological-way-clear-synthesis-repair.md#2-repaired-bottom-line-and-final-name); closure EB-07](receipts/evidence-closure.json)]

The seven resource families share query mechanics, not meanings. Each needs its own profile, semantic owner, clauses, evidence compatibility rules, and lifecycle ceiling. No receipt implies truth outside scope, owner approval, execution completion, lifecycle movement, promotion, publication, release, deployment, or production readiness. # Context

The existing Way Clear is much narrower than the requested capability: it deterministically sorts decision-frontier nodes and reports whether in-scope `open|fog` items remain. Its output does not bind artifact kinds, evidence classes, owners, completeness declarations, lifecycle components, or reason completeness. The source and schema were inspected but not run in this dispatch. [Observed, not executed: [Agent 1 §3 and §7](research.md#agent-1--formal-ontology-and-closure-semantics); closure EB-01](receipts/evidence-closure.json)]

The broader question could not be answered by extending that Boolean. The three explorer lenses exposed distinct tensions: formal semantics must preserve open-world unknowns and bounded local completeness; lifecycle semantics must separate coherence, evidence, approval, admission, execution, promotion, and release; architecture/design/UX evidence must retain human judgment ceilings. Synthesis then required three adversarial gates—precedent, non-vacuity, and definitional soundness—and one bounded repair loop. ## Final Claim Ceiling

> For one exact resource snapshot, evaluation profile, evidence baseline, evaluator, declared finite scope, and named purpose, EBEQ evaluated every applicable clause and returned the complete component and reason set permitted by those exact bindings.

## Shared Query-Local Vocabulary

The smallest repaired substrate is structural and query-local, not a universal ontology. [Proposed, reviewer-repaired: [repair §3](receipts/ontological-way-clear-synthesis-repair.md#3-smallest-repaired-vocabulary)]

- `ResourceSnapshot`: exact resource ID, selector, `ResourceKind`, revision, digest, media type, baseline and currentness. It merges and retires `EvaluatedResource` and `ArtifactVersion`; it is not a canonical superclass.
- `ResourceKind`: owner-declared selector family—one of the seven listed below. It is never inferred from filename, icon, route, grouping, or presentation.
- `EvaluationProfile`: immutable purpose-specific clause contract with version, digest, owner, applicability, criticality, component targets, evidence compatibility and invalidation law.
- `Obligation`: one profile clause with an exact subject, applicability rule, evidence constraints, component targets and disposition.
- `Claim`: an exact proposition with independently recordable support and refutation.
- `EvidenceRecord`: immutable, typed, scoped, baseline-bound evidence or challenge with provenance, currentness and validity.
- `OwnerDecision`: external scoped judgment with authority basis, operation, expiry/revisit condition and challenge route.
- `ScopeDeclaration`: roots, datasets, relation allow-list/directions, exclusions, as-of time, semantic bounds and boundary law.
- `CompletenessDeclaration`: optional exact subject/query-pattern declaration whose issuer, trust, expiry and invalidation policy remain deferred.
- `Dependency`, reified `SatisfactionClaim`, `EvaluationQuery`, `ComponentVerdict`, partitioned `Reason`, and append-only `EvaluationReceipt`.

`StatementKind` is only an optional source-domain annotation until an owner supplies a discriminating clause and fixture. Independent `EvaluationRole` has no admitted verdict effect and is not in the kernel. `LifecycleState` remains an external reference. [Killed/demoted by paper effect test: [non-vacuity observable-effect test](receipts/reviewer-non-vacuity.md#candidate-observable-effect-test); [repair §3](receipts/ontological-way-clear-synthesis-repair.md#3-smallest-repaired-vocabulary)]

## Relation and Component Non-Collapse

The surviving kernel relations have narrow laws. `hasKind` selects profile semantics without authority. `imposes` and `appliesTo` make an obligation evaluable without asserting satisfaction. `supports` and `challenges` set separate evidence bits. `verifiedBy` binds evidence and method without approval. `invalidatedBy` changes current admissibility without rewriting history. `dependsOn` is traversed only under profile scope, direction, criticality and cycle law. `covers` means addressed scope only. `conflictsWith` is symmetric, irreflexive and non-transitive. Satisfaction survives only as a reified `SatisfactionClaim(subject, obligation, method, evidence, profile, baseline)`. [Proposed/repaired: [repair §4](receipts/ontological-way-clear-synthesis-repair.md#4-final-core-relation-laws)]

The component vector is:

```text
[AC, ES, OA, TA, EC, P, R]
 AC artifact_coherence       ES evidence_sufficiency
 OA owner_approval           TA transition_admission
 EC execution_completion     P  promotion
 R  release
```

`AC` and `ES` are computed. `OA` validates an external decision. `TA` is a computed, non-authoritative projection. `EC`, `P`, and `R` validate external records only where the selected profile requires them. Every clause names its component targets; no component implies or mutates another. A consumer owned by a transition may interpret `TA`, but EBEQ has no universal `may_proceed`, selects no work, and authorizes no mutation. [Observed/proposed: [Agent 2 §§5–8](research.md#agent-2--lifecycle-and-acceptance-semantics); [repair §5](receipts/ontological-way-clear-synthesis-repair.md#5-repaired-component-algebra); closure EB-03](receipts/evidence-closure.json)]

## State and Reason Algebra

Claim evidence is paraconsistent and paper-proposed:

```text
K(c) = (support, refute)
(0,0) unknown   (1,0) supported   (0,1) refuted   (1,1) conflict
```

`fog` is unknown plus an explicit unresolved-discrimination record. `open` is a known applicable obligation not discharged: it is `UNSATISFIED` only with a failing witness or licensed absence-as-failure; otherwise `UNKNOWN`. `stale` means historically present but non-current. `superseded` selects a successor and moves the old input to history. `out_of_scope` requires an exact scope rule. `blocked` is a derived overall verdict, never authored lifecycle state. `error` means the evaluation contract itself failed. [Proposed: [Agent 1 §5](research.md#agent-1--formal-ontology-and-closure-semantics); [synthesis §8](receipts/ontological-way-clear-synthesis.md#8-state-and-reason-algebra)]

The definitional repair separates two previously ambiguous terms:

- `invalid_evidence`: a profile-required evidence artifact is proven to violate its method/schema/binding invariant. The affected `ES` clause becomes `UNSATISFIED`; a critical use yields `BLOCKED`.
- `invalidated`: a typed event removes historical evidence from current admissibility without changing its historical result. The current gap becomes `UNKNOWN` or `STALE`; only a valid completeness declaration proving no admissible replacement makes it `UNSATISFIED`.

Broken evaluator/method bindings are `ERROR`, not invalid evidence. [Repaired, paper-evaluated: [definitional state audit](receipts/reviewer-definitional.md#statereason-non-collapse-audit); [repair §5](receipts/ontological-way-clear-synthesis-repair.md#5-repaired-component-algebra)]

Per-component states are `SATISFIED`, `UNSATISFIED`, `UNKNOWN`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `STALE`, `SUPERSEDED`, or `ERROR`. Overall precedence is:

```text
ERROR         evaluator/query contract invalid
BLOCKED       else any applicable acceptance-critical clause definitively UNSATISFIED
INDETERMINATE else any critical UNKNOWN, STALE, conflict, unbound owner,
              incomplete scope, or incomplete traversal
CLEAR         else every applicable clause evaluated, every required component
              SATISFIED, every exclusion explicitly NOT_APPLICABLE,
              no failure reason, and reasons_complete == true
```

Five non-overlapping collections replace one overloaded reason list:

1. `blocking_reasons`: proven critical unsatisfaction.
2. `uncertainty_reasons`: unknown, stale, conflict, incomplete baseline or scope.
3. `error_reasons`: evaluator/query contract failures.
4. `boundary_residue`: exact nonblocking exclusions and permitted boundary edges.
5. `history_residue`: superseded inputs, previous verdicts and invalidation history.

Each entry binds component, clause, subject, evidence, criticality, repair owner and next action. Each collection is independently stable-sorted and deduplicated. `reasons_complete` covers every applicable clause and every visited allowed node/edge; a runtime cutoff forces it false and forbids `CLEAR`. Exact benign exclusion is boundary residue, not failure. [Repaired, paper-evaluated: [non-vacuity gaps](receipts/reviewer-non-vacuity.md#gaps-blockers-and-residue); [repair §6](receipts/ontological-way-clear-synthesis-repair.md#6-reason-and-residue-partitions)]

## Bounded Open World and Local Completeness

Missing facts remain unknown. RDF/OWL do not license global absence-as-failure, and SHACL closed shapes constrain selected focus-node predicates rather than establish dataset completeness. A `CompletenessDeclaration` may license a negative conclusion only for its exact subject/query pattern, dataset, issuer, evidence, baseline, time, expiry and invalidation bindings. [Observed primary-source boundary and proposed policy: [Agent 1 §§3,5–6](research.md#agent-1--formal-ontology-and-closure-semantics); closure EB-02](receipts/evidence-closure.json)]

Accordingly, the same absent required fact has two lawful outcomes: without a valid local completeness declaration it is `UNKNOWN` and the result is `INDETERMINATE`; under a matching trusted declaration it is `UNSATISFIED` and may make the result `BLOCKED`. A declared semantic depth may bound scope; an accidental resource cutoff cannot. Issuer trust, pattern law, expiry and invalidation remain deferred, so no implementation may silently assume a closed world. [Paper-evaluated/proposed: [repair §8 cross-cutting corrections](receipts/ontological-way-clear-synthesis-repair.md#8-corrected-paper-witness-expectations); closure EB-11](receipts/evidence-closure.json)]

## Authority, Human Evidence, and Public/Private Boundaries

An `EvaluationReceipt` is evidence about evaluation, never authority. It may bind an existing owner decision, but cannot manufacture approval, create authority, move lifecycle state, repair an input, select work, execute, promote, publish, deploy, release, or certify production readiness. Historical receipts are immutable; currentness changes produce a new receipt. [Observed/proposed: [Agent 2 §§5–9](research.md#agent-2--lifecycle-and-acceptance-semantics); closure EB-04](receipts/evidence-closure.json)]

Machine checks can establish exact syntax, bindings, traces, schema conformance, test outcomes, accessibility checks within their scope, and presence of qualified human receipts. They cannot manufacture concern completeness, trade-off adequacy, comprehension, usability, preference, risk acceptance, or generalizability. UXPlan in particular must not substitute browser or automated accessibility results for required human evidence. [Observed: [Agent 3 §§4–7](research.md#agent-3--architecture-design-and-ux-evidence); closure EB-05](receipts/evidence-closure.json)]

EBEQ remains private candidate research. Any public Arcanum extraction requires a separate boundary decision and generic, scrubbed contracts and synthetic fixtures. No private paths, owners, or project-specific prose may cross unchanged. The present work authorizes no public extraction and no private canonical promotion. [Deferred boundary: [precedent missing-source defects](receipts/reviewer-precedent.md#missing-source-defects-and-residue); [repair §9–§10](receipts/ontological-way-clear-synthesis-repair.md#9-exact-binding-reviewer-closure-delta); closure EB-12](receipts/evidence-closure.json)]

## Cross-Resource Dependency DAG

The paper witness uses a bounded dependency graph, not semantic equivalence:

```text
Definition ──grounds──────────────► Requirement
Requirement ──operationalized-by──► Criterion
Requirement ──addressed-by────────► Architecture
Requirement ──constrains──────────► Design
Architecture ──constrains─────────► Design
Design ──evaluated-by─────────────► UXPlan
Criterion ──covered-by────────────► DefinitionOfDone

Each applicable obligation ──verified-by──► independent terminal EvidenceRecord
Profiles requiring judgment ──bind────────► exact current OwnerDecision
```

These labels are profile-local dependency types, not promoted kernel relations. No arrow implies satisfaction, approval, completion, kind conversion, or lifecycle movement. Cycles supported only by their own members are `BLOCKED` with `CIRCULAR_SUPPORT` when cycle membership and support completeness are known; incomplete cycle knowledge is `INDETERMINATE` with cycle and completeness reasons. ## EvaluationQuery and EvaluationReceipt Contract

An `EvaluationQuery` must bind at least:

- exact `ResourceSnapshot`, selector, baseline and currentness;
- `EvaluationProfile` ID/version/digest and named purpose;
- evaluator identity/version/digest;
- evidence and owner-decision manifests;
- `ScopeDeclaration`, optional trusted `CompletenessDeclaration`, relation directions and semantic bounds;
- canonicalization and hash algorithms, as-of time, and resource budgets.

Evaluation is pure, deterministic, stable-ordered, duplicate-free, and append-only. A profile or evaluator digest mismatch is `ERROR`; an exhausted declared runtime budget is `INDETERMINATE` with `reasons_complete: false`. [Proposed/repaired: [Agent 1 §6](research.md#agent-1--formal-ontology-and-closure-semantics); [repair §5](receipts/ontological-way-clear-synthesis-repair.md#5-repaired-component-algebra)]

An `EvaluationReceipt` must bind the query digest; all input/profile/evaluator/canonicalization identities; every component and clause result; the five reason/residue partitions; traversal and exclusion manifests; completeness/currentness; prior receipt or supersession references; evaluation time; deterministic receipt digest; and literal `authority_effect: none`. It records no universal `may_proceed`. A transition-owning consumer may use `TA` under its own contract, but the receipt itself performs no state change. [Proposed/repaired: [synthesis §7](receipts/ontological-way-clear-synthesis.md#query-component-and-receipt-contract); [repair §§5–6](receipts/ontological-way-clear-synthesis-repair.md#5-repaired-component-algebra)]

## Current Way Clear Migration

Migration is a design proposal only. [Deferred: [synthesis §11](receipts/ontological-way-clear-synthesis.md#11-migration-from-current-openfog-way-clear); closure EB-12](receipts/evidence-closure.json)]

1. Freeze current behavior as `LegacyDecisionFrontierProfile/v0`; do not reinterpret legacy `clear` as EBEQ evidence.
2. Adapt each legacy node into an obligation candidate while preserving source ID, scope, state and digest.
3. Map `open` to applicable/not discharged plus `OPEN_OBLIGATION` and `LEGACY_CAUSE_UNTYPED`; map `fog` to `UNKNOWN` plus `UNRESOLVED_FOG`; map legacy `closed` to no positive conclusion plus `LEGACY_CLOSED_UNVERIFIED` until re-evaluated.
4. Treat `scope != in_scope` as `NOT_APPLICABLE` only when an exact declaration reproduces it; otherwise emit `SCOPE_UNDECLARED`.
5. Add profile, evaluator, evidence, owner, completeness, canonicalization and semantic-bound identities; add component verdicts, typed clauses, manifests, five reason/residue partitions and `authority_effect: none`.
