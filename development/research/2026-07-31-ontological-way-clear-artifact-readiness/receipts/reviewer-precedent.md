### Bottom line

**Verdict: pass-with-precedent-residue.** EBCQ is precedent-clean only as a private, candidate-local, pure bounded query and receipt projection with `authorityEffect: none`. It is not a new general ontology, lifecycle, approval gate, canonical definition system, or public Arcanum capability.

Its important separations already have owners: lifecycle axes, authority, gates, traceability, canonical definitions, readiness audits, Task Session admission, and standard-level validation/provenance/assurance vocabulary. The proposed composition is not itself deployed. Any future move must reuse those owners rather than promote the candidate’s parallel labels.

### Duplicate-gate and seam findings

1. `ClosureProfile` plus `mayProceed` is nearest to an existing gate, especially Work Pack Readiness and Task Session admission. It is safe only as a subordinate evaluator projection for an exact named profile/transition. If it selects work, authorizes mutation, advances lifecycle state, or creates a generic “clear” status, it duplicates and conflicts with those owners. [synthesis §7, lines 216–292](cyberAlchemy-v2/development/research/2026-07-31-ontological-way-clear-artifact-readiness/receipts/ontological-way-clear-synthesis.md#seven-component-verdicts); [Work Pack Audit §§194–214](arcanum/spells/work-pack-readiness-audit/README.md).

2. `WayClearReceipt` overlaps established receipt patterns but is not a duplicate if it is an append-only, evaluation-local evidence/projection artifact. It must not be registered as an authority kind, receipt of approval, or successor/closeout receipt unless the owning contract explicitly adopts it. The closed authority-kind rule otherwise default-denies authority. [synthesis §7](cyberAlchemy-v2/development/research/2026-07-31-ontological-way-clear-artifact-readiness/receipts/ontological-way-clear-synthesis.md#query-component-and-receipt-contract); [Authority Model §§48–69](cyberAlchemy-v2/authority/AUTHORITY-MODEL.md).

3. `LifecycleState`, `owner_approval`, `promotion`, and `release` are existing lifecycle/authority concerns, not EBCQ-owned states. EBCQ’s “external input” framing is precedent-aligned; any local schema or edge law remains deferred. [synthesis §7, lines 218–246](cyberAlchemy-v2/development/research/2026-07-31-ontological-way-clear-artifact-readiness/receipts/ontological-way-clear-synthesis.md#seven-component-verdicts); [Lifecycle Model §§8–20, 56–62](cyberAlchemy-v2/ontology/LIFECYCLE-MODEL.md).

4. The proposed `EvidenceClass` taxonomy and two-bit conflict algebra are not supplied by SHACL, OWL, PROV, SACM, or current local definitions. They are candidate-level and must not be presented as standards-derived or deployed. [synthesis §8, lines 247–257](cyberAlchemy-v2/development/research/2026-07-31-ontological-way-clear-artifact-readiness/receipts/ontological-way-clear-synthesis.md#unresolved-formal-limits).
