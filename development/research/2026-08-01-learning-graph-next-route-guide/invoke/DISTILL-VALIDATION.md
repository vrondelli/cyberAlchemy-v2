## Discovery Baseline

Accepted research already removes the universal ontology/product framing. The
remaining feature has two implementation domains: deterministic projection
generation and mutation-admitted presentation in one existing host. Mutation
admission, semantic admission, destination-owner admission, and human evidence
remain explicit and disjoint external gates.

## Proposer Pass

First unit:

> Freeze the projection schema and structural validator, then prove killed
> vocabulary, unknown endpoints, and authority-like suggestion fields fail
> before any compiler or host work.

This is smaller and more reversible than resolving sources, generating a
snapshot, or changing the host.

## Atomicity And Split Result

Ten mutation SWUs remain, each with one independently reviewable behavior:

1. structural rejection;
2. exact binding and replay;
3. positive-fixture ceiling;
4. abstention/question/suggestion response;
5. host reader/currentness;
6. semantic DOM structure;
7. responsive/non-color presentation;
8. focus/view/inspector parity;
9. keyboard interaction;
10. reproducible browser evidence.

No SWU bundles independently acceptable structure, responsive layout, state
projection, and interaction behavior.

authoritative. Guardrails: visible proof tiers, mandatory abstention, no action
control, authority-field schema negative, event/network observation, and later
