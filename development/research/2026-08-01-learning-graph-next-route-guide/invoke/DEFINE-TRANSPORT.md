## Decision And Gaps

The feature is defined as a generated read model, not a new ontology or
product. Learner-view mutation admission, owner-resolved semantic evidence,
destination-owner identity/admission, session-text retention, real-source adapter evidence,
human comprehension, accessibility acceptance, and promotion remain outside
the Define evidence ceiling.
