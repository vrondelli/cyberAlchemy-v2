# UX Plan: Learner Projection Guide

## Selected Predicate

- Natural person: yes.
- Reads/navigates/decides what evidence question to ask: yes.
- Rendered semantic contract: changed.
- Design selection required: yes.

## Audience And Job

Initial audience: internal product owner, researcher, or builder who can follow
system vocabulary when attached to one subject. The job is to see why a fixture
claim is limited and carry a more precise verification question to the correct
governed owner.

## Journey

1. **Orient:** a compact rail separates semantic concept, expectation,
   observation, finding, session, gap, and workflow-admission layers.
2. **Focus:** select `LegacyLimitRule` through the existing navigator/search.
3. **Inspect:** review exact path, digest, pointer, owner, proof tier, and
   invalidation for each record.
4. **Abstain:** explain why identity cannot be decided.
5. **Revise:** display the exact v1 and v2 questions with the deterministic
   premise set.
6. **Suggest:** show a visibly non-executing verification package.
7. **Recover:** on stale/invalid input, remove derived content and point to the
   failed binding.

## Semantic Presentation Rules

- Every record shows class and proof tier in text, not color alone.
- Geometry and proximity carry no semantic or authority meaning.
- `ABSTAIN` is visually primary when an identity verdict is unsupported.
- The suggestion heading says “Candidate evidence route — display only”.
- No action button is attached to the destination owner string.
- Graph, Outline, and Table presentations expose identical record IDs and
  proof-tier labels.
- Focus changes only presentation state.

## Keyboard And Assistive Mechanics

- Existing search, navigator, view tabs, inspector, help, and guide link remain
  reachable by keyboard.
- The learner rail follows document order and has semantic headings.
- Focus changes move or announce context without trapping keyboard focus.
- Stale/invalid diagnostics are programmatically associated with the hidden
  derived section.
- Acceptance uses automated mechanics plus manual review; it does not claim
  full accessibility conformance.

## Privacy And Retention

L0/L1 use only the frozen self-report and question fixtures from the revised
synthesis. No personal identifier, durable learner profile, telemetry of
confusion, or session-question persistence is admitted. Any real learner input
requires a separate session/privacy owner decision and data-lifecycle design.

## UX Evidence Plan

Mechanical candidate evidence:

- keyboard reachability;
- visible text labels independent of color;
- exact focus/inspector synchronization;
- stale-state recovery;
- Graph/Outline/Table parity;
- zero outbound requests and execution-like events.

Deferred human evidence:

- whether learners distinguish the layers;
- whether revised questions are more evidence-bound;
- whether confidence rises without boundary accuracy;
- retention and transfer beyond the fixture.

No mechanical browser result proves comprehension or accessibility acceptance.
