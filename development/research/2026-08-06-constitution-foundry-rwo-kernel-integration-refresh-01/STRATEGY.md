## Preliminary architecture hypothesis

Foundry is a typed private domain composition over the thin recursive kernel, not a subsystem whose semantics or authority belong to the kernel.

- `Author`, `Prove`, `Decide`, and `Cast` may be leaf or composite Work definitions.
- The kernel owns immutable structural composition, addressed commands, accepted structural events, attempts, bounded routing, a rebuildable cursor, and non-authoritative projection.
- Foundry owns constitution cases, native source/IR, obligations, backend-report interpretation, case posture, and domain transitions.
- Cedar, Lean/FOL, Datalog, bounded solvers, and other validators remain separately owned leaf adapters or Work units with native proof ceilings.
- Meta-Authority may validate prerequisites and recommend owner routes as ordinary gate Work, but it cannot ratify a constitution or authorize casting.
- Existing constitution and authority owners issue separate ratification and casting-authorization artifacts.
- Mint owns the no-write plan, transaction/effect execution, effect journal, recovery classification, and target truth.
- A structural release event never means validated, ratified, authorized, installed, observed, promoted, or true.
- The orchestration journal/cursor and Foundry case reducer may share accepted artifact references, but they have different subjects and neither manufactures domain truth.

This remains a research hypothesis. No recursive Work compiler/reducer, FoundryWorkAdapter, end-to-end fixture, owner act, backend execution, Mint execution, or target effect is proven.
