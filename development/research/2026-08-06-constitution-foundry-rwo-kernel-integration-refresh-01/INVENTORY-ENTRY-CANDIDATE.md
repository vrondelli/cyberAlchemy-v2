## Accepted Architecture Result

Constitution Foundry should sit above the thin Recursive Work Orchestrator as a
typed domain adapter. The kernel owns structural composition, addressed
commands, attempts, finite routing, structural events, accepted structural
history, replay, cursor reconstruction, and non-authoritative projections.
Foundry retains native constitution source and IR, obligations, backend
interpretation, five-axis case posture, and semantic transition rules.

Cedar, Lean, Datalog, and bounded solvers remain separate typed leaf adapters
with distinct proof ceilings. Meta-Authority is only a readiness validator and
route adviser, not a new authority kind. Existing owners separately issue
ratification and casting authorization. Mint alone owns no-write effect
planning, target writes, its effect journal, and recovery classification.

journal, inherited authority, kernel-owned semantic truth, merged ratification

installed, observed, promoted, or true.
