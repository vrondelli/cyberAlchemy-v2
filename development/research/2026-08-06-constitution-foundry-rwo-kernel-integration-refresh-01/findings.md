## Exact command, event, gate, effect, and receipt timeline

1. Adapter proposes an immutable root run for one exact case/artifact cut: `work_requested(root) → work_started(root)`.
2. Author receives `command_delivered`, starts one attempt, and emits manifest/IR/obligation references; contractual admission may release Author but says nothing about translation fidelity.
3. Prove fans out addressed backend commands. Each returns a typed report ref preserving obligation, projection, profile, tool/version, trusted base, bounds, and ceiling. Unsupported/unknown/timeout/translation-error remain domain statuses.
4. Aggregation evaluates obligation coverage, not arrival count: `gate_evidence_emitted → work_released|work_blocked`.
5. Readiness and owner-route Work emit assessment/advice; neither is ratification.
6. Ratification import admits a separately owner-issued decision reference or emits `reference_rejected → work_blocked`.
7. Mint planning returns `effect_plan_reference_emitted`; this is no-write and not permission.
8. Casting-authorization import admits a second owner act bound to the exact candidate, validation, plan, target pre-state, conditions, and expiry. Rejection produces **zero** `effect_attempt_requested` events.
9. Preinstall gating checks the frozen tuple and may release one addressed Mint command.
10. Mint rechecks authority and target pre-state under its lock, applies or recovers finite effects, and returns its own receipt: `effect_attempt_requested → effect_attempt_started → effect_result_reference_emitted → attempt_terminal`.
11. The ACI journal accepts only structural events and receipt references. Foundry validates the artifact and derives its casting axis; journal acceptance does not mean installed.
12. Independent observer, attestor, and cold-interpretation Work emit their distinct references. Failed observation cannot rewrite a prior Mint outcome.
13. `work_terminal(root)` means only that the declared structural branch ended.
