term: shared definition

Accepted Meaning

Role

A reviewed meaning that carries commitment inside a named scope.

Receipt

The decision that accepted it, the owner of review, and the evidence beneath it.

Edge

Connects to the rules, tests, promises, and warnings it affects.

Limit

If action changes, route to decision. The map cannot approve.

phrase: working shorthand

Split Before Promotion

Role

A phrase that compresses a question, not yet a settled concept.

Receipt

The note, conversation, or source where the phrase appeared.

Edge

Referent, status, owner, and action consequence still need separation.

Limit

It must be clarified or retired. Fluency is not authority.

Edge

Limit
