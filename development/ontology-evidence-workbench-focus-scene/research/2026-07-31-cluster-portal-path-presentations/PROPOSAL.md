## Goal

Select one bounded, source-backed, testable graph presentation in which Body
War contexts may appear as understandable areas or clusters, cluster cards or
icons may act as portals, and Path may reveal branch-like cluster projections.
The result must preserve exact predicates, projected direction, provenance,
keyboard recovery, and explicit complete-graph access without inventing
ontology meaning.
