# Body War focus-scene novice test

Status: **pending real participants**

This protocol compares the default Body War ontology graph (condition A) with
the first guided focus scene (condition B). The automated browser evidence is
complete, but it does not establish improved human comprehension.

## Participants and isolation

- Recruit three people who have not worked on the Body War ontology.
- Record only anonymous labels `P1`, `P2`, and `P3`.
- Give every participant both conditions, counterbalanced as `A/B`, `B/A`,
  `A/B`.
- Reset to a fresh browser context before each condition.
- Do not explain the Start Tour control before the discoverability observation.

## Questions

Ask the same three questions in each condition:

1. What four business areas organize the first Body War journey?
2. Where does Identity and Consent lead?
3. Does the displayed relation change the authority of either ontology node?

Expected answers:

1. Candidate Geometry Evidence, Identity and Consent, Suggested Track Route
   Expansion, and Score Preview.
2. Authentication and Consent.
3. No. The portal is a presentation relation; its authority effect is `none`.

## Procedure

1. Open condition A at the default graph and start the answer timer.
2. Record the three answers and total elapsed seconds.
3. Reset the browser.
4. Open condition B at the same default graph. Without pointing to the control,
   ask the participant to find a view that helps them understand the first
   journey. Record whether Start Tour is found within ten seconds.
5. Once the focus scene is open, start the answer timer and record the same
   three answers and total elapsed seconds.
6. Record one short observation about confusion, occlusion, or unclear wording.

## Gates

- Correctness: at least 7 of 9 answers are correct in the focus condition.
- Speed: the focus-condition median is at least 30% faster than the baseline
  median.
- Discoverability: all 3 participants find focus within 10 seconds.

All three gates must pass. Until real observations are entered, the result
remains `pending`.

## Visual risks to observe

- The default graph is visibly dense.
- At 1440×1000, the six-portal panel covers part of the lower focus canvas.
- The accepted build displays a knowledge-graph freshness warning. Treat it as
  an evidence-boundary warning, not as a participant answer.

Do not fill the table with simulated participants or promote this protocol to a
human-comprehension claim without real observations.
