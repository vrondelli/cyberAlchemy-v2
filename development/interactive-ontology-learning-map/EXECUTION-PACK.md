## Receipt contract

Every executed SWU returns:

- SWU/task/slice/layer IDs;
- exact files changed;
- validation commands and raw result references;
- acceptance verdict;
- unresolved residue and authority effects;
- non-regression checks;
- next selected SWU or stop reason.

For W0C-UI, each receipt must also name its typed output handle and the exact next
consumer. A1 emits `iolm-fixture-boundary/v1`, A2 emits
`iolm-demo-snapshot/v1`, A3 emits `iolm-interactive-viewer/v1`, and A4 emits the
terminal `iolm-browser-acceptance/v1` handle.

## Stop conditions

- owner or host boundary changes;
- candidate posture/provenance is missing, or a candidate receipt can satisfy an authority gate;
- during L0A only, the exact DomainSpec `Sg` is missing, empty, profile/source/graph-mismatched, or lacks its prerequisite receipts;
- a local fixture, feature table, or mapping is used to reconstruct a DomainSpec semantic relation;
- an unapproved local mapping is admitted instead of retained as residue;
- any `Sg -> G -> Sg_canonical` retraction mutant passes;
- candidate vocabulary is treated as canonical without a decision;
- private output enters `arcanum/`;
- rejected relation enters semantic export;
- learning/layout change alters semantic bytes;
- a gate is summarized from another gate's evidence;
- a task needs a wider write scope than its contract;
- IOLM authors, repairs, or substitutes an upstream evidence handle, or a narrow receipt is offered for the BTE breadth slot;
- the demo escapes its three pinned allowlisted fixture paths, serves before the binding preflight passes, or writes outside its exact sidecar/evidence scopes;
- demo automation marks keyboard, focus, reduced-motion, rendered-DOM parity, visual sufficiency, accessibility, usability, or full browser validation passed;
- a workable-example receipt is offered for 00C-B, 00C-C, G-DSV2-SG, `CROSS_FEATURE_UNSUPPORTED`, promotion, or publication;
- A2, A3, or A4 starts without its exact predecessor receipt, or more than one UI SWU is selected;
- a rebind lifecycle reuses the historical A1-A3 receipts, binds a fixture path outside the three Key-and-Door allowlisted paths, or continues after the refresh snapshot drifts;
- A4 discovers an implementation defect and edits A1, A2, or A3 files instead of returning a defect receipt to that owner;
- public packaging or production integration is pulled into the private witness.
