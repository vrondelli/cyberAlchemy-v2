# CyberAlchemy - Investor Business Plan

## Executive Summary

CyberAlchemy is building a governed evidence-to-action business for teams using
AI-assisted workflows and business metrics they cannot confidently review,
explain, assign to an owner, or use for a consequential decision. The first
commercial hypothesis is a fixed-scope Metric Trust / governed AI Work
Readiness engagement. A customer brings one blocked metric or workflow and one
accountable decision owner. CyberAlchemy returns a source chain, ambiguity and
confound notes, explicit obligations and checks, a readiness verdict, an owner
decision record, a reusable receipt, and a recommended next action.

The immediate business is services-led because services are the fastest honest
way to discover what buyers will fund, what evidence they need, which controls
repeat, and where software genuinely removes delivery labor. Test pricing is
$2.5k-$7.5k for a diagnostic, $15k-$35k for a core sprint,
$25k-$75k for a broader readiness package, and $8k-$20k per month for a
future continuation retainer. These are planning ranges, not observed prices.
There is no validated platform or license revenue in the current model.

Behind the offer is a broad proprietary and public substrate. DomainSpec v2 is
the private typed derivation and validation surface. CAV2/CyberAlchemy v2 is the
private authority, promotion, and claim-governance spine. Public Arcanum
capabilities such as Craft and goal provide ledgered continuation, fail-closed
routing, receipts, and owner approval boundaries. DomainSpec Lean and adjacent
formal research contribute bounded witnesses, counterexamples, and a culture
of refusing claims that outrun proof. Prototype and research surfaces in UI,
IDE work, confidential workflows, metric trust, local-node receipts,
multi-objective reasoning, taxonomy, model-use, and anti-exploitation show how
the same discipline can transport across domains.

That breadth is strategically meaningful but must be interpreted correctly.
It is not evidence of product-market fit, a completed platform, or many ready
revenue lines. It is evidence that a small team can explore heterogeneous
systems while preserving authority, evidence, and continuation boundaries.
The founders report that much of this body of work was produced by two
dedicated people over roughly three months. That is a powerful velocity thesis,
but it remains a diligence claim until commit chronology, prior-work
accounting, AI-assistance accounting, and fresh technical receipts are bundled.

The long-term vision is a system that helps build other systems, including
agentic systems whose claims can be inspected and challenged. CyberAlchemy
uses `verifiable` only where a named check, validator, receipt with a failure
mode, integrity specimen, or bounded formal witness exists. It uses `provable`
only where a named property has a bounded formal witness. The company does not
claim provably correct agents, a formally verified platform, certification,
compliance, external assurance, or a complete end-to-end assurance pipeline.

The next 90 days determine whether this becomes an investable commercial
engine. In 30 days, the company must complete 5-10 buyer interviews, a bounded
readiness specimen, fresh technical receipts, and a founder-complete financial
model. In 60 days, it must sign and deliver one paid pilot with a fixed SOW and
accepted receipt bundle. In 90 days, it should test up to three paid pilots
using the same core bundle and measure reuse, labor/cycle-time change,
second-workflow pull, and retainer interest.

The financing ask is intentionally unresolved in this draft. The founders must
set the legal entity, current cash, compensation, monthly burn, desired
instrument, runway target, and milestone budget. The eventual ask should fund
buyer validation, three paid pilots, receipt productization, fresh bounded
technical specimens, and contracting/security foundations, not a broad
platform build ahead of evidence.

## 1. Company Overview

### One-Sentence Business

> CyberAlchemy helps teams turn one distrusted business metric or AI-assisted
> workflow into source-linked evidence, owner-routed decisions, readiness
> gates, and reusable receipts.

### Why The Company Exists

AI makes it inexpensive to generate code, analyses, documents, plans, and
decisions. It does not automatically make those outputs reviewable, owned,
correctly scoped, or safe to act on. In many organizations, the limiting
question is no longer, "Can the model produce something?" It is, "Can an
accountable person understand what supports this output, what could invalidate
it, and whether it is ready for this specific action?"

CyberAlchemy exists to close that gap. Its working discipline binds an artifact
to sources, obligations, checks, owners, gates, decisions, non-claims, and
continuation records. The intended result is not a generic confidence score.
It is a bounded decision package that another person can inspect, challenge,
accept, narrow, retest, or reject.

### Present Business And Future Thesis

The present business hypothesis is a paid, fixed-scope service. The future
thesis is a governed evidence-to-action platform built from the patterns that
actually repeat in paid work.

```text
paid diagnostic or sprint
  -> evidence and owner-decision receipt
  -> reusable schema, gate, check, route, or template
  -> lower variance and delivery labor
  -> second workflow or continuation demand
  -> productized platform component
```

If the reuse and buyer-demand steps do not occur, CyberAlchemy should remain a
specialized services business or revise the thesis. The platform is not an
inevitable promotion.

### Proprietary And Public Boundary

CyberAlchemy operates from a private umbrella and a public capability library:

- **DomainSpec** is the private typed-intent, derivation, and validation
  surface.
- **CAV2/CyberAlchemy v2** is the private authority spine: vocabulary,
  source ownership, promotion gates, and stable repository semantics.
- **Arcanum** is the public capability library for governed work patterns such
  as ledgers, routes, tasks, receipts, and composition.
- The closed-loop intent-to-reality thesis remains private and incomplete.

Private mechanisms and confidential project material must not leak into public
Arcanum or public-facing copy. Investor diligence can inspect a bounded private
data room under appropriate confidentiality controls.

## 2. Problem And Customer

### The Concrete Problem

A BI lead, operations owner, product leader, or AI-workflow owner has a number
or generated output that affects a real action: allocate spend, ship a feature,
approve a document, change a process, escalate a risk, or trust an automated
step. The output may look plausible, but the team cannot answer one or more of
these questions:

- Where did the result come from?
- Which denominator, definition, or source version governs it?
- What ambiguity, confound, or missing evidence could change it?
- Which checks were run, and what failure would they detect?
- Who owns the decision to use it?
- What does a pass mean, and what does it not mean?
- What should be retained so the next review starts from evidence rather than
  reconstructing a chat?

The blocked decision is:

> Can we use this named metric or AI-assisted workflow output for this named
> business action, under this scope, with this owner?

### Initial Customer Profile

The first reachable buyer pocket is deliberately narrow:

| Role | Pressure | Budget logic | User/beneficiary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BI or analytics lead | A metric is disputed, poorly defined, or unsafe for an operating decision | Analytics quality, transformation, decision support | Operators and executives using the metric. |
| Operations owner | A workflow crosses tools or teams without a clear evidence/approval trail | Process improvement, risk reduction, operational readiness | Staff responsible for the workflow. |
| CTO, product, or engineering leader | AI-assisted work is moving faster than review, ownership, and evidence practices | Engineering productivity, platform, quality, AI governance | Builders and reviewers of AI-assisted systems. |
| Sensitive-workflow owner | Confidential artifacts require custody, approval, retention, and traceability | Security, legal operations, risk, internal tooling | Authors, reviewers, and accountable approvers. |

This profile is a hypothesis. It becomes a market claim only after interviews
and paid evidence identify the actual economic buyer, urgency, budget owner,
procurement route, and replacement behavior.

### Three Illustrative Customer Scenarios

These are composite scenarios, not customer evidence. They show how the same
CyberAlchemy operating unit could enter three different environments without
pretending that three vertical products already exist. In every case, the
system begins from one consequential decision, integrates with the tools the
customer already uses, binds evidence to an accountable owner, and leaves a
receipt that can be inspected and reused.

#### Scenario 1: A Live Premise Before The Next Operating Window

It is Thursday afternoon at a multi-site hospitality and venue operator. The
regional operations lead must decide how to staff, stock, and promote each site
for the weekend. The company already has a point-of-sale system, bookings,
staff schedules, inventory exports, campaign reports, and local spreadsheets.
It does not have one number everyone trusts. Bookings and walk-ins use different
time windows. Cancellations are treated differently by each location. A
promotion appears successful in the marketing report but may have shifted
existing demand rather than created it. Managers are debating the dashboard
instead of making the operating decision.

CyberAlchemy does not ask the operator to replace those systems. A bounded
Metric Trust engagement connects to exports or read-only interfaces, records
which source owns each definition, makes denominator and cancellation rules
explicit, and traces the weekend decision back to the evidence that supports
it. Named checks surface missing data, stale inputs, cross-site definition
drift, and unsupported assumptions. The regional operations lead remains the
decision owner and receives a pass/flag/block view for each load-bearing input.

The output is not a new dashboard claiming to know the business better than
the operator. It is a decision receipt: what sources were used, which rules
were applied, what remains uncertain, who approved the action, and what should
be measured after the weekend. If the next planning cycle reuses the same
source map, denominator rules, checks, and receipt shape, the engagement begins
to leave productizable operating residue.

Commercial entry: a paid diagnostic around one disputed operating metric,
followed by a Metric Trust Sprint and, only if the buyer requests continuity, a
monitoring or repeated-decision retainer.

#### Scenario 2: A Technology Company At The Release Gate

A software company is preparing a release that touches customer billing. AI
assistants contributed code, tests, migration notes, and incident analysis.
The engineering team has pull requests, CI results, issue tickets, security
scans, feature flags, architecture decisions, and reviewer comments. Each
surface contains part of the truth, but the release owner cannot answer a
simple question without reconstructing several conversations: are the
obligations implied by the release intent actually covered, and who is willing
to own the remaining risk?

CyberAlchemy binds the release intent to explicit obligations and collects
evidence from the existing Git, issue-tracking, CI, test, security, and
observability surfaces. Deterministic checks can verify named structural facts;
bounded formal witnesses can attach only where a specific property justifies
them. Missing evidence remains visible rather than being converted into a
confidence score. The release owner sees which obligations passed, which are
flagged, what failed, and what requires a human override or stop.

The resulting release-readiness receipt links the decision to source versions,
checks, reviewer decisions, exceptions, and rollback conditions. It does not
claim the software is correct or incident-free. It makes the release decision
inspectable and gives the next release a governed starting point. Repeated
releases can test whether obligation patterns, gates, and repair paths reduce
review labor and variance.

Commercial entry: an AI Work Readiness Sprint around one release or one
AI-assisted engineering workflow, with expansion only after the same receipt
core proves reusable.

#### Scenario 3: A Confidential Workflow Awaiting Approval

A legal, financial, healthcare, or internal-risk team uses AI to prepare a
document from sensitive source material. A draft exists, but the accountable
reviewer cannot easily see which sources informed it, whether every required
section was reviewed, who had custody at each step, which version was approved,
or what must be retained. The workflow may be useful, but usefulness is not the
same as readiness for a high-consequence action.

CyberAlchemy integrates at the workflow boundary: identity and role records,
the document or case system, approval events, version metadata, retention
rules, and model-use records when they can be captured safely. The engagement
can use digests, redacted metadata, and customer-controlled evidence rather
than centralizing confidential content. It makes custody, source use, reviewer
authority, required checks, exceptions, and retention explicit before the
owner is asked to approve the output.

The receipt records which bounded checks ran, what the reviewer approved, what
was excluded, and what the package does not establish. It can support review
and audit preparation, but it does not certify legal, medical, financial, or
regulatory compliance. If the customer repeats the workflow, the reusable
asset is the governed approval and evidence pattern, not the confidential
content itself.

Commercial entry: an advanced confidential-workflow readiness package only
after authentication, custody, retention, and approval boundaries can be
contracted and tested safely.

#### One Integration Pattern Across All Three

| Layer | Live premises | Technology company | Confidential workflow | Stable CyberAlchemy responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Existing systems | POS, bookings, schedules, inventory, campaign reports | Git, issues, CI, tests, security, observability | Identity, document/case system, approvals, retention, model-use records | Integrate through bounded exports, interfaces, or customer-controlled evidence; do not demand wholesale replacement. |
| Blocked decision | Staff, stock, and promote the next operating window | Release, pause, narrow, or roll back | Approve, revise, reject, or retain the document/workflow output | Bind one decision and one accountable owner. |
| Evidence problem | Definitions, time windows, denominators, cancellations, confounds | Obligations scattered across tools and conversations | Source use, custody, version, approval, and retention are unclear | Build a source registry and make ambiguity/missing evidence visible. |
| Governed action | Readiness gate for each operating input | Obligation coverage and release gate | Custody, review, and approval gate | Run named checks, preserve human authority, and classify pass/flag/block/veto. |
| Receipt | Sources, rules, owner action, post-window measure | Versions, checks, exceptions, owner decision, rollback | Sources/digests, custody, reviewer decision, exclusions, retention | Record scope, evidence, decision, failure modes, privacy boundary, and non-claims. |
| Compounding test | Reuse next planning cycle | Reuse next release | Reuse next confidential case/workflow | Measure adaptation cost, labor/cycle-time change, and buyer pull before product promotion. |

### Current Alternatives

Customers currently combine some of the following:

- internal analysts and domain experts;
- spreadsheets, dashboards, tickets, documents, and manual review checklists;
- data-quality, lineage, observability, MLOps, or governance tools;
- AI governance and risk-management platforms;
- consultants, auditors, legal/security reviewers, and systems integrators;
- requirements, test-management, and traceability tools;
- agent frameworks and coding assistants;
- formal-methods or correctness specialists for narrow high-risk properties.

These alternatives are often strong in their own category. CyberAlchemy's
intended edge is the joined delivery unit: source-linked obligations, owner
authority, explicit gates, receipts, continuation, and bounded formal or
deterministic checks where appropriate. That intended edge is not yet a proven
competitive advantage.

## 3. Solution And Offer

### Lead Offer: Metric Trust / AI Work Readiness Sprint

The sprint is a fixed-scope engagement around one buyer-owned decision.

Inputs:

- one metric or AI-assisted workflow;
- one named decision and accountable owner;
- relevant sources, systems, and access boundaries;
- known risks, definitions, and current workarounds;
- agreed acceptance criteria, timing, price, and stop rule.

Outputs:

1. A source and evidence registry.
2. A current-state metric/workflow map.
3. Definition, denominator, ambiguity, and confound notes.
4. Derived obligations and named checks.
5. A readiness scorecard with pass/flag/block/veto results.
6. An owner decision record.
7. A privacy, custody, retention, and reuse boundary.
8. A receipt bundle with explicit non-claims.
9. A next-action recommendation and current-to-target plan.

The receipt records what was examined, what happened, who decided, and what
remains uncertain. It does not prove that source data is true, certify a system,
or replace the customer's accountable owner.

### Offer Ladder

| Stage | Offer | Test range | Business purpose |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Entry | Readiness diagnostic | $2.5k-$7.5k | Qualify the decision, sources, owner, and sprint scope. |
| Core | Metric Trust / AI Work Readiness Sprint | $15k-$35k | Deliver the complete bounded receipt and owner verdict. |
| Expansion | Advanced/confidential workflow readiness | $25k-$75k | Add broader custody, security, approval, retention, or multi-artifact gates. |
| Continuation | Monitoring/review retainer | $8k-$20k/month | Keep evidence, decisions, and checks current after a successful sprint. |
| Future | Platform/license | Not priced | Productize repeated patterns only after paid reuse is measured. |

### Qualification And Refusal

CyberAlchemy should refuse or reframe work when no accountable owner exists,
the desired output is an unbounded transformation, source/custody access cannot
be made safe, the customer demands a certification or legal guarantee the
company cannot provide, or success cannot be expressed as a falsifiable
decision package.

## 5. Market Entry And Competition

### Beachhead

CyberAlchemy will begin with founder-led sales to BI, operations, product,
engineering, and AI-workflow leaders who can point to one distrusted metric or
blocked workflow. The beachhead is defined by a decision condition, not an
industry-wide TAM:

```text
named owner
+ consequential blocked action
+ accessible evidence
+ budget path
+ fixed receipt-shaped scope
= qualified opportunity
```

The market-entry proof is not a report estimating millions of potential users.
It is the ability to find 5-10 relevant buyers, convert one to a paid sprint,
and repeat the same core bundle across three paid cases.

### Competitive Categories

| Category | Why buyers use it | CyberAlchemy's intended distinction | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BI/data quality and observability | Monitor data, lineage, tests, and incidents | Join technical evidence to a named owner decision and reusable readiness receipt | Buyer preference and integration value untested. |
| AI governance/risk platforms | Inventory models, policies, controls, and risk | Start from one bounded workflow and attach source, gate, owner, decision, and non-claim | No compliance or governance-platform comparison claim yet. |
| Consulting and systems integration | Supply expertise and transformation capacity | Fixed receipt-shaped scope designed to expose reusable schemas and checks | Repeatability and margin untested. |
| Requirements/test/traceability tools | Link requirements, work, tests, and defects | Typed derivation plus claim/authority boundaries across AI-assisted work | Product integration and adoption untested. |
| Agent frameworks and coding assistants | Automate or accelerate work | Govern the evidence and decision boundary around assisted work | No claim of superior model performance. |
| Formal-methods/correctness specialists | Prove named properties of critical systems | Use bounded formal witnesses inside a broader readiness package when justified | CyberAlchemy does not prove whole agents or systems correct. |

Kodamai is a relevant named comparator in the narrow correctness/specification
area, particularly because its team and funding context may differ materially.
It cannot responsibly be described as CyberAlchemy's only competitor. The
competitive set is broader and changes quickly. Any claim of leadership,
uniqueness, firstness, or exclusivity requires a dated comparator archive and a
narrowly defined capability.

### Why Now

The timing thesis has three parts:

1. AI-assisted work is spreading faster than many teams' review, ownership,
   provenance, and continuation practices.
2. Regulatory and management-system pressure increasingly emphasizes
   documentation, logging, oversight, traceability, risk, robustness, and
   accountability, even though CyberAlchemy does not claim compliance.
3. Generation is becoming cheap; inspectable decision evidence and accountable
   use remain expensive.

This timing thesis must be tested in buyer language. Standards attention alone
does not create a budget or customer.

## 6. Go-To-Market

### Initial Motion

The initial channel is founder-led, research-led outreach:

1. Interview the owner of a metric or AI-assisted workflow.
2. Identify the exact action that is blocked, the current workaround, urgency,
   and budget path.
3. Offer a paid diagnostic when the scope is unclear, or a direct fixed sprint
   when owner, evidence, and acceptance are clear.
4. Deliver the receipt and owner decision.
5. Ask whether the buyer wants a second workflow, continued monitoring, or no
   further work.
6. Use rejection and stop evidence to change the offer.

### Sales Assets

The first sales package should contain:

- a one-page problem/offer brief;
- a fixed-scope SOW with acceptance and stop rules;
- one sanitized readiness receipt specimen;
- an explicit non-claims box;
- a short demo of reversible, gated work;
- pricing bands and collection terms;
- a buyer interview and objection log.

### Expansion Discipline

Confidential workflow readiness, governance onboarding, creative evidence
scorecards, and retainers remain adjacent routes. They move forward only when
the same buyer problem, receipt shape, or reusable control appears in paid
work. They should not compete for headline attention during the first 90 days.

### GTM Demotion Gate

Demote or change the wedge if buyer calls do not repeatedly reveal an
accountable owner, consequential decision, budget path, and dissatisfaction
with the current workaround. Do not call interviews traction. Do not call an
unpaid pilot commercial validation.

## 7. Operations And Delivery

The complete operating contract is in `operating-plan.md`. The minimum delivery
unit is:

```text
one owner + one blocked decision + one bounded artifact
  -> source-linked evidence and named checks
  -> readiness verdict
  -> owner decision
  -> receipt and next action
```

Every engagement passes source, scope, check, receipt, claim, commercial, and
reuse gates. Every verification statement names its check and failure mode.
Every customer retains its authority boundary. Every delivery records what it
does not prove.

The two-founder team begins with one primary active sprint at a time. Parallel
delivery is a promotion earned by measured templates, stable quality, and
capacity evidence. Weekly reviews cover pipeline, scope/evidence, cash,
collections, claim exceptions, and stop decisions. Monthly reviews cover
funnel losses, unit economics, receipt completeness, reuse, witness freshness,
roadmap promotions, and runway.

to make the org chart look venture-shaped. The first hire should remove a
measured constraint in sales, delivery, or productization.

### Break-Even

Monthly cash break-even occurs when collected contribution from diagnostics,
sprints, advanced packages, and retainers covers monthly fixed cash operating
cost. It cannot be calculated until the founders set compensation, fixed cost,
and opening cash assumptions.

## 12. Defensibility And Compounding

CyberAlchemy's defensibility thesis is conditional residue, not a static secret
or an unsupported moat claim. Each engagement may leave:

- a reusable typed schema or obligation pattern;
- a source/definition mapping;
- a readiness gate and failure mode;
- a receipt structure;
- an owner route and decision pattern;
- a privacy/custody boundary;
- a bounded witness or counterexample;
- a repair path and measured adaptation cost;
- delivery data about scope, labor, cycle time, and buyer value.

If later engagements reuse these elements, require less labor, improve decision
quality, and create buyer demand for another workflow, the corpus can become a
real delivery and product advantage. If reuse does not happen, it is simply an
archive of prior work.

The proof ladder is:

1. Repository artifact exists.
2. Artifact is runnable and fresh.
3. Artifact contributes to a bounded customer delivery.
4. The same artifact or pattern is reused in another paid delivery.
5. Reuse reduces labor, cycle time, variance, or risk.
6. Buyers request continuation or experience meaningful switching cost.

Only the later steps support stronger defensibility language.

next decisive witnesses, not the inflation of the current claims.
