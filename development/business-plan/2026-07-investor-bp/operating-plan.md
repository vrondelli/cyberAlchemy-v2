# Operating Plan

## Operating Objective

Turn one buyer-owned, blocked metric or AI-assisted workflow decision into a
bounded evidence package, an explicit owner verdict, and a reusable delivery
receipt. The operating system is designed to learn whether this can become a
repeatable business. It is not autonomous governance.

## Customer Entry Gate

An opportunity qualifies only when all of the following are named:

- one metric or AI-assisted workflow;
- one concrete business action that is blocked or exposed;
- one accountable owner who can accept, reject, pause, or narrow the work;
- available source/data access or a clear access path;
- a fixed scope, price, acceptance criteria, and stop condition;
- a privacy, confidentiality, and reuse boundary;
- a budget path and contracting route.

If owner, action, access, or budget cannot be named, the opportunity remains a
research interview and does not enter the paid delivery pipeline.

## Delivery Loop

```text
qualify blocked decision
  -> bind owner, scope, sources, privacy, and stop rule
  -> baseline the current metric/workflow and its failure modes
  -> build source chain, confound/denominator notes, and obligations
  -> run named checks and record pass/flag/block results
  -> conduct owner review and record the decision
  -> issue receipt bundle and next-action recommendation
  -> measure reuse, labor, cycle time, and buyer pull
  -> promote, revise, repeat, or stop the offer
```

## Engagement Stages

| Stage | Main work | Required output | Gate / stop rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Qualification | Interview buyer and decision owner | Qualification note | Stop if no owner, blocked action, access path, or budget path. |
| 1. Contract and boundary | Fix scope, price, timing, custody, reuse, acceptance, and stop conditions | Signed SOW and boundary record | Stop before data access if legal/privacy terms are unresolved. |
| 2. Baseline | Capture current workflow, decision, sources, denominator/confounds, and failure modes | Baseline map | Flag if the question cannot be made testable. |
| 3. Evidence build | Create source chain, obligations, checks, and owner route | Evidence package draft | Flag missing sources and never substitute confidence language for evidence. |
| 4. Readiness gate | Run named checks and classify results | Pass/flag/block/veto table | A pass applies only to named gates and scope. |
| 5. Owner decision | Present evidence and alternatives | Owner decision receipt | The owner can keep, revise, pause, retest, reject, or mark inconclusive. |
| 6. Delivery receipt | Package evidence, decision, limits, and next action | Receipt bundle | Receipt must name scope, failure mode, privacy/reuse boundary, and non-claims. |
| 7. Learning residue | Measure delivery and reusable elements | Engagement retrospective | No platform/moat promotion without observed reuse and buyer pull. |

## Standard Receipt Bundle

1. Engagement identity, scope, buyer, owner, and decision.
2. Source registry and data-access boundary.
3. Current-state workflow or metric definition.
4. Denominator, confound, ambiguity, and missing-evidence notes.
5. Derived obligations and named checks.
6. Validation outputs with date, version, and failure mode.
7. Readiness scorecard with pass/flag/block/veto classification.
8. Owner decision and rationale.
9. Privacy, custody, retention, and reuse boundary.
10. Recommended next action and stop conditions.
11. What the receipt does not prove.
12. Reusable residue: templates, schema, check, route, or lesson that may be used again.

## Quality Gates

| Gate | Pass condition | Failure response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source gate | Every load-bearing statement links to a source or is labeled assumption. | Remove, relabel, or request missing source. |
| Scope gate | Decision, owner, artifact, time, and non-goals are explicit. | Pause work and narrow scope. |
| Check gate | Every verification claim names the check and failure mode. | Demote to observation or research. |
| Receipt gate | Bundle includes result, owner decision, limits, and privacy/reuse boundary. | Do not mark delivery complete. |
| Claim gate | Claim is no broader than source, scope, freshness, and verifier strength. | Demote or forbid the claim. |
| Commercial gate | Price, hours, costs, collections, and acceptance are recorded. | Do not use the engagement in financial validation. |
| Reuse gate | Reused elements and adaptation cost are measured. | Keep platform/defensibility thesis conditional. |

| Cycle-time/labor change | Tests productization leverage | Compare like-for-like deliveries after the first. |

## 30/60/90 Operating Plan

| Window | Work | Required evidence | Promotion decision | Demotion / stop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1-30 | Conduct 5-10 buyer calls; finalize SOW specimen; create one bounded readiness specimen; refresh selected technical receipts; finish financial input sheet | Interview notes, SOW, specimen, command receipts, founder cost/runway facts | Proceed if owner, urgency, budget path, and paid scope appear repeatedly | Narrow or change wedge if buyer problem remains vague or ownerless. |
| Days 31-60 | Sign and deliver one paid pilot; record hours/cost/cash timing; refresh comparator archive | Signed SOW, invoice/collection, delivery bundle, owner verdict, retrospective | Proceed if buyer accepts the bundle and reusable elements are visible | Demote if unpaid, unscoped, or entirely bespoke. |
| Days 61-90 | Complete up to three paid pilots with the same core receipt; test second workflow/retainer | Comparable receipts, reuse log, labor/cycle-time comparison, buyer continuation signal | Strengthen platform thesis only if reuse and buyer pull appear | Keep a services-only model, revise the bundle, or stop if reuse/value does not appear. |

## Weekly And Monthly Cadence

Weekly:

- pipeline and qualification review;
- active-delivery scope/evidence gate;
- cash and collections check;
- claim exception review;
- one decision on what to stop, narrow, or repeat.

Monthly:

- funnel and loss-reason review;
- unit economics and capacity variance;
- receipt completeness and reuse report;
- technical witness freshness;
- roadmap promotion/demotion decisions;
- runway update after actual cash movements.

## Stop Rules

Stop or materially revise the lead wedge when:

- buyers cannot identify a concrete blocked action or accountable owner;
- paid scope requires open-ended transformation rather than a fixed bundle;
- source access, privacy, or custody cannot be bounded;
- delivery repeatedly exceeds the high-end hour range without pricing power;
- buyers do not value the receipt or owner-decision package;
- the same core schema/check/receipt cannot be reused across three paid cases;
- the business requires certification/compliance claims before the formal route exists;
- founder capacity leaves no room for buyer development or productization.
