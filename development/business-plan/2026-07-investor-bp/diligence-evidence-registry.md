# Diligence Evidence Registry

## Purpose

This registry is the evidence boundary for the CyberAlchemy investor business
plan. It organizes what can be shown, what remains an assumption, and what must
not be claimed. Registry presence is not proof. A source becomes load-bearing
only when its maturity, scope, freshness, failure mode, and authority boundary
support the claim made from it.

## Status Vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Proven now | A bounded repository witness exists for the exact claim. |
| Assumption | A planning input selected for modeling and still requiring validation. |
| Forecast | A calculated outcome produced from stated assumptions. |
| Future-only | A roadmap or intended capability, not a current offer or capability claim. |
| Missing witness | The claim may be plausible, but the required buyer, runtime, financial, or external evidence is absent. |
| Forbidden | The wording exceeds the present evidence and must not appear as a supported claim. |

## BP Section Coverage

| BP section | Current evidence | Missing witness | Demotion gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Executive summary | Wedge, substrate, receipt, and witness-ladder research | Paid pilot; current receipts; funding ask | Remove any sentence implying platform completion, demand, or proven moat. |
| Company overview | DomainSpec/CAV2/Craft/Lean portfolio and public/private boundary | Legal entity and company-history facts, if required | Keep repository architecture separate from legal/corporate facts. |
| Problem and customer | Internal verticals and accepted problem synthesis | Buyer interviews with owner, urgency, workaround, budget, blocked action | Reclassify as research hypothesis if interviews do not confirm the pain. |
| Solution and offer | Fixed-scope package design and test ranges | Paid SOW, measured hours/cost, acceptance criteria, stop rule | Reclassify as bespoke research if the bundle cannot repeat. |
| Market and alternatives | Bottom-up BI/ops/AI buyer pocket and competitor categories | Dated comparator refresh and buyer density | No broad TAM, first, only, or unique language. |
| Go to market | Calls -> diagnostic -> sprint -> second workflow/retainer model | Conversion, cycle length, objections, collection timing | Narrow or change the channel if calls do not reach an accountable buyer. |
| Operations | Owner/source/gate/receipt delivery loop | One complete paid delivery receipt | Do not call it autonomous governance. |
| Team | Two-founder operating thesis and repository output | Names/roles, exact chronology, prior-work and AI-assist accounting | Treat output as discipline evidence, not traction. |
| Roadmap | 30/60/90 witness queue | Actual execution results | Kill or revise milestones whose evidence gates fail. |
| Financials | Bottom-up test ranges and capacity cautions | Actual burn, founder compensation, runway, tax/legal costs, collections, paid margins | Label every output as assumption or forecast until measured. |
| Funding ask | Milestone-based use-of-funds logic | Amount, instrument, runway target, current cash | Keep placeholder; do not invent a raise. |
| Risks/non-claims | Assurance, moat, and blind-spot findings | None for inclusion; external counsel for legal specifics | Reject any investor artifact that hides the non-claims. |
| Appendix/data room | Accepted findings and maturity registry | Fresh runnable receipts, buyer notes, SOW, comparator archive | Registry cannot be described as proof or external assurance. |

## Forbidden Claim Register

The following are forbidden as present-tense supported claims:

- finished or deployment-proven platform
- autonomous governance
- implemented Evidence Exchange or truth market
- certified, compliant, conformity assessed, or third-party audit-ready
- ISO/IEC 42001 or EU AI Act compliance
- formally verified platform or provably correct agents
- receipts prove truth
- current platform revenue, market pull, or traction without named evidence
- proven moat
- unique, first, or only without a fresh and narrowly scoped comparator record
- production legal SaaS or confidential-data-safe system
- live ad integration or proven creative lift
- shipping IDE plugin
- token-ready or validated economy
- public-ready DomainSpec v2 or CAV2
- many revenue routes are ready

## Registry Verdict

`FLAG`: sufficient to draft a claim-bounded business plan and operating model;
insufficient for investor-ready claims of demand, repeatability, platform
completion, assurance status, or defensibility. The next promotion event is not
more prose. It is buyer evidence plus one paid, receipt-backed delivery.
