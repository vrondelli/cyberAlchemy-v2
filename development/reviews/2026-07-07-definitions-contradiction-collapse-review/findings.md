## 3. Surviving yield — polish change-requests

R1 is the one **confirmed contradiction** (reinstated C4). R2–R5 are MINOR residuals the verifiers surfaced while refuting the other candidates — local hygiene/clarity edits inside a single entry. All are in-place; none changes the count. Ranked most-substantive first.

### R1 — D29 contradicts D16 (and itself) on what may relate coupled axes. **MAJOR** (confirmed contradiction — reinstated C4)
- **Source:** C4, refuted by all three verifiers, **reinstated by the coverage-auditor's pressure-test.**
- **Evidence — the bypass D29 licenses.** D29 Normative sentence 1: axes are independent *"unless an explicit typed relation, **gate, schema rule, validator, or owner decision** relates them."* D29 Formal: *"where `R_ij` is a typed coupling, **gate, schema rule, validator, or owner decision** with named endpoints, direction or symmetry, evidence, and effect"* — and `R_ij` is the bridge that licenses governing co-change `move(a_i) ⇒ move(a_j)`. So D29 permits a bare **owner decision / gate** (4 fields) to license governing co-change.
- **Evidence — what it contradicts.** (a) D29's own sentence 2: *"the dependency must be represented through `CAV2-D16` typed coupling invariant before it can govern work."* (b) `CAV2-D16`: *"must record a typed relation `R(A, B)` before the coupling can influence authority…"* with a **7-field** minimum (*source endpoint · target endpoint · relation type · direction or symmetry · owner route · evidence reference · validation/promotion/blocking effect*). Two gaps survive even under maximum charity: a **gate/owner-decision is a different ontological kind** than a "typed relation," and D29's `R_ij` drops **relation type** and **owner route** (4 vs 7).
- **Why the verifiers missed it:** all three quoted only D29's sentence 2 ("route through `CAV2-D16`") and none engaged D29's sentence 1 or its Formal `R_ij` enumeration. The earlier claim "D29 requires a typed relation, so no conflict" is unsupported — D29 says *both*, which is exactly the contradiction.
- **Fix:** Reconcile the enumeration, not just the field count. The governing-co-change bridge must **be** a `CAV2-D16` typed coupling; *gate / schema rule / validator / owner decision* should be named as the **instruments that record** that coupling (carrying all 7 fields), not as alternatives to it. Align D29 sentence 1, its Formal `R_ij`, and sentence 2 to one rule.

### R2 — D5 narrows D37's verdict set by SILENT omission. **MINOR** (hygiene)
- **Source:** C1 refutation residual (all three verifiers noted).
- **Evidence.** D37 permits an instance to narrow *"unless a narrower gate defines otherwise"*, and D5 does narrow — D5 Boundary: *"The gate may return pass, flag, or block."* But D5 never states it is *deliberately* excluding `defer`; the narrowing is inferable only by comparison to the parent.
- **Fix (one line):** Add to D5 Boundary an explicit "defines otherwise" note, e.g. *"`defer` is intentionally not used at this gate; deferral is handled by the authority promotion queue (`CAV2-D13`)."*

### R3 — D37 Domain-Context "pass/flag/block" wording is loose against its 4-verdict Normative. **MINOR** (wording)
- **Source:** C1 refutation secondary residual.
- **Evidence.** D37 Normative + Formal: minimum verdicts `pass`, `flag`, `block`, and `defer`. D37 Domain-Context: *"the pass/flag/block behavior used across research and handoff surfaces"* — omits `defer`. Verifiers read this as downstream-instance description (not a competing definition), so not a contradiction — but the wording invites the C1 misreading.
- **Fix (one line):** Either write `pass/flag/block/defer` in D37 Domain-Context, or mark the 3-verdict phrase as describing narrower instances (e.g. D5) rather than the gate primitive.

### R4 — D13's `rejection` is unmapped to the gate verdict lattice. **MINOR** (clarity)
- **Source:** C2 refutation residual (Quine + Wittgenstein).
- **Evidence.** D13 Boundary: items remain candidate-only until the owner records *"a pass, flag, block, deferral, or rejection."* D37's gate lattice is `pass/flag/block/defer` — no `rejection`. Because D13 self-declares it is *"not a promotion gate result"*, this is not a contradiction. But whether queue-`rejection` maps to gate-`block` or is a distinct queue-terminal state is left unstated.
- **Fix (one line):** Add a clause to D13 stating how `rejection`/`deferral` relate to gate verdicts (e.g. "queue `rejection` is a terminal owner decision, distinct from gate `block`; `deferral` maps to gate `defer`").

### R5 — D20 dual-kind membership deserves a cross-note. **MINOR** (navigation, optional)
- **Source:** C3 refutation residual ("a one-line cross-note would help").
- **Evidence.** D32 names D20 a `method` instance; D50 names D20 a `discipline` instance. Verifiers confirmed these are orthogonal axes and the dual membership is licensed — no defect. The only residual is discoverability.
- **Fix (optional one line):** Add to D20 a note that Craft method is an instance of `method` (`CAV2-D32`) on the structural axis and of `discipline` (`CAV2-D50`) on the authority-kind axis — orthogonal, both licensed. Not required for correctness.

### S1 — D14 (Source Space) + D15 (Project Space) into D11 (Tower Space)?
- **Source:** M2 refutation (Wittgenstein PARTIAL, Quine "redundancy pressure noted").
- **Standing distinction (cost of merging).** D14 Boundary: a source space is *"outside the current CyberAlchemy v2 authority surface"* and *"may be authoritative for itself while remaining candidate-only for CyberAlchemy v2."* D15 Boundary: a project space *"can hold authority only when its role permits it."* The two carry **opposite authority postures**, not merely an inside/outside label. - **Verdict:** Defensible either way. If the owner wants 48, this is the cleaner of the two merges — provided the posture opposition is preserved as an explicit field on D11.

### S2 — D44 (View-Bearing Concept) into D42 (Governed Artifact Tree)?
- **Source:** M5 refutation (closest call).
- **Standing distinction (cost of merging).** D44 is a **threshold predicate on a concept** — D44 Normative: a concept *"needs its own governed view or tree because it has distinct evidence, lifecycle posture, owner route, reuse surface, and challenge/drift behavior."* D42 is the **interface obligation on a tree** — D42 Normative: it *"exposes the governance interface required before its contents can promote knowledge."* "When does a concept earn a tree" vs "what must a tree expose" are complementary, not redundant. Merging folds the threshold rule into D42's admission clause and loses D44 as a citable standalone predicate.
- **Verdict:** Defensible either way. Weakest independent-existence case in the corpus, but not a defect.
