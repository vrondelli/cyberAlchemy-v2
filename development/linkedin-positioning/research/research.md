### Audience Needs

They should understand within seconds that CyberAlchemy helps complex knowledge or agent work leave durable structure behind. Builders and buyers need examples more than architecture: decision maps, source-backed guides, bounded experiments, and reviewable artifacts.

### Company vs Founder Voice

**Company page:** declarative, plural or institution-centered, offer-led. “CyberAlchemy creates…” It should name a designed method and intended direction, then bound claims by evidence.

**Founder experience:** first-person, contribution-led, reflective. “I lead/co-create…” It should describe the founder’s role in shaping the method, governance, research, product direction, and collaborations—not imply sole authorship of every artifact or a fully operating product suite.

The company owns the category proposition. The founder owns an honest account of stewardship and contribution.
