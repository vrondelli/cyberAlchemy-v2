# Research Credibility Constitution

Status: active local constitution
Owner: constitution-governance
Authority level: repository-local constitution
Selectors: `**`

## Scope

This constitution governs the credibility of ResonantOS economy research in this standalone
repository. **It is the local home of method authority** (source posture, promotion
discipline, non-collapse rules, authority separation) — owned here, NOT delegated to any
external framework.

## Rules

### REC-C1. Primary Source Boundary

Validation mode: review

The official ResonantDAO primary source is `https://resonantdao.com/whitepaper/`. Every other
source is supporting, related, analogy, local inference, or open residue.

### REC-C2. Claim <= Evidence

Validation mode: review

No artifact may claim a safer, more complete, more official, or more proven economy design
than the cited evidence supports.

### REC-C3. Research Before Mechanism

Validation mode: review

Mechanism sketches must stay in research lanes, residue maps, closure plans, or validation
requests until source, formal, behavioral, and authority gates are named.

### REC-C5. Failure Corpus First

Validation mode: review

Bounty, token, treasury, DAO-governance, exploit-defense, and market mechanisms must pass
through failed-project and attack-surface research before promotion. *(This is the closest
the domain has to a domain-specific credibility rule — honest read: it is a research-process
rule. Whether it is genuinely domain-ORIGINATED at the rule level, or a specialization of
the generic claim≤evidence / research-before-mechanism posture, is contestable; it is NOT a
new authority KIND regardless — Obs-A kind-level is decided in AUTHORITY-MODEL.)*

### REC-C7. No Scalar Reputation Collapse

Validation mode: review

The repo must not compress contribution, trust, expertise, governance authority, community
care, and financial eligibility into one unreviewed scalar.

### REC-C13. No Reciprocal Authority Laundering ⟵ *Obs-B positive control*

Validation mode: review

No claim may be promoted in this repository **or in a reciprocal CyberAlchemy v2 / framework
return package through a cyclic citation chain alone.** A return package may be candidate
evidence for an external owner route, but it must name source IDs, blocked uses, unresolved
review findings, public/private scrub status, and the independent owner route that would
decide any reuse.

The package must state that it **transfers no CAV2 / framework authority**, proves no
ResonantOS economy mechanism, contains no validation report unless one exists, and implements
no behavior unless a separate task route did so. *(Keeps the framework citation-only — the
constitution-level guard against the precedent's leak.)*

### REC-C14. Load-Bearing Term Tracking

Validation mode: hybrid

Every **load-bearing term** (a symbol/named quantity in a formal expression, a fixture
FAIL-region, a candidate invariant, a decidable rule, or the normative content of a claim
ledger entry) must resolve to a tracked authority entry (a canonical definition ID). A
load-bearing term first cited without a tracked entry must be created as a `candidate`
definition carrying an `Open / to-ground:` field and moved through the definitions lifecycle.
Citing one with no tracked entry is a drift defect the definition-drift audit must flag.
Candidate status is not validation.
