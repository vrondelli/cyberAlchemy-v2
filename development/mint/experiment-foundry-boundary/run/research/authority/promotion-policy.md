# Promotion Policy

Status: active local policy
Owner: ResonantOS economy research owner

Research promotes by evidence and owner route, not by elegance.

## Promotion Ladder

| State | Meaning | May promote to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source evidence | Original source material in its own context. | Source record. |
| Source record | Path, date, source kind, and boundary captured. | Source claim ledger. |
| Source claim | A claim with evidence and local reading separated. | Subtower synthesis. |
| Subtower synthesis | Bounded reading with source-kind labels. | Candidate invariant, workflow witness, or open residue. |
| Candidate invariant | A proposed condition research should preserve. | Validation request after collapse test and witness. |
| Workflow witness | A concrete scenario for testing behavior. | Evidence harness or external review. |
| Research recommendation | A source-backed proposal with risks and gates. | External owner decision. |

## Required Gates

| Gate | Required when | Standard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source-kind gate | A new source is added or cited. | Primary, supporting, related, analogy, inference, or residue is explicit. |
| Authority gate | A claim could affect economy policy / token / governance / payout decisions. | Route to **this repo's `authority/`** and mark non-policy until external owner review. *(Resolves locally — never to a framework.)* |
| Failure-corpus gate | A bounty/token/treasury/governance/exploit/market mechanism is proposed. | The proposal must pass through failed-project and attack-surface research before promotion. *(Domain risk control; honest read: a research-process gate, not a clean cav2-has-no-analog new owner function — it is the Failure-Corpus-First constitution rule operationalized.)* |
| Non-collapse gate | A status label (`research-pass/flag/block`) is attached to an artifact. | The label may route work but must not create validation proof, reward eligibility, governance weight, policy effect, or authority effect. |

## Promotion Blocks

Block promotion when:

- a metric / signal becomes governance authority without human review;
- the claim depends on an analogy without a bridge decision;
- the proposal skips the required failure-corpus precedent;
- the recommendation has no witness, falsifier, or audit route;
- **the promotion would resolve by traversing into a framework attachment** (no framework
  edge is a promotion path — Obs-B at the promotion level).
