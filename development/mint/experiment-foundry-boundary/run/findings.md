   discipline, non-collapse) **cannot author a genuinely-new authority KIND** — its kinds ARE specializations of
   cav2's universal meta-kinds. This is the Conant–Ashby re-fusion §6 named as the most-likely failure: the mold
   already *is* canonical "governed method," and method is all this domain's authority is.
2. **But 2/3 SURVIVED — the failure is domain-class-specific, not total.** process (a classification kind) and
   product (a temporal-commitment kind) produced autonomous, non-leaky, non-circular castings with owner routes
   cav2 lacks. Even the failing domain leaks nothing (Obs-B/C held everywhere) — the crack is *precisely and only*
   Obs-A for the methodological class.
3. **Honest scope:** the foundry works for domains bringing a **genuinely-new authority OBJECT** (a kind cav2
   doesn't have) and fails for domains that **only specialize cav2's meta-kinds**. Predominantly a **real boundary
   limit** (resonantos's authority is inherently a specialization), with a **criterion-scope residue**: a
   *content-level* Obs-A would credit resonantos's localized content (Failure-Corpus-First, non-scalar reputation,
   its non-collapse set) — a finding for a FUTURE criterion (test "autonomous localization of CONTENT, not KIND"),
   **NOT** an edit to this frozen one, and it does not rescue this run.

**Net for the cav2 owner:** the "two System-5s one recursion apart" boundary is **load-bearing for
object-authority domains and decorative for methodological ones.** The foundry thesis is **partially
