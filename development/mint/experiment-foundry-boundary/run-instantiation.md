   analog**) is *both* lexically new *and* owner-route new under F3(c) — uncontested. The "every row is a

   Decision-subtype) is **redundant to the verdict**. Two adjudicators reach the same HOLDS for product.

   **table FORMAT** (+ closure rule + lifecycle) — it does **not** "inherit kind-types" (the strategist's
   misquote). "Localize the kind ROWS" (SPEC) and "≥1 domain-originated kind" (Obs-A) are the **same
   requirement stated twice.** Re-authoring Obs-A would **delete the re-skin teeth (F4)** = goalpost-moving;
   **refused.**
