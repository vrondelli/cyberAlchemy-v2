# Universal-Baseline Partition — Universality-First Lens

## Angle & test (universality-probe)

For each `CAV2-D#` I read its **Normative Definition FORM** and applied one probe:
does the sentence quantify over *any/every governed action, artifact, axis, repo,
or claim* — i.e. is it a **method / invariant / primitive** that any governed repo
would need to author its own authority? If yes → **inherit-baseline**. A cav2
mention inside a universal method does **not** couple it to cav2 (guard honored):
I looked at what the definition *ranges over*, not at its home. A **localize**
verdict is only issued when the definition's subject *is cav2 itself* — its repo,
its extraction target, its import corpus, its self-model — such that seeding it
would re-fuse the two System-5 levels (the circularity guardrail, SPEC §Boundary).
**already** = Mint's SPEC MOLD row / templates already carry it (SPEC.md:52–95).

## Boundary notes on the two hardest calls (guard applied)

- **D6 vs D50 (localize vs inherit).** D50 *discipline* is the universal genus — any
  governed repo needs the notion of a stewarded recurring practice. D6 *stable
  repository stewardship* is a **populated card** whose subject is keeping *this
  repo* ledgered; seeding it would seed a cav2 discipline instance. SPEC agrees:
  "no recurring practice exists at mint-time — seed only the Growth-Rule + card
  schema" (SPEC.md:75). Genus inherits; the instance localizes.
- **D9/D1 (localize).** These *name cav2 or cav2's extraction target as their
  subject* — not "any repo." This is the guard's inverse case: not a universal
  method that merely mentions cav2, but a definition whose Normative Definition
  *ranges over cav2 itself*. Seeding = circularity (SPEC §Circularity guardrail).
- **Guard honored (no over-read of cav2 mentions).** D16/D17/D18/D20/D21/D23/D30/
  D34/D37 all *say* "CyberAlchemy v2 rule/method/invariant" in their home voice yet
  quantify over *any* action/object/layer/axis — I classified on the FORM (what the
  invariant ranges over), not the home label. These are the strongest inherit calls.
