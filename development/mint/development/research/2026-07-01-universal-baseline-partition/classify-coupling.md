# Universal-baseline partition of the 50 cav2 definitions — COUPLING-FIRST classifier

## Method (coupling-probe)

For each `CAV2-D#`, the test is **not** "did cav2 author it" (guard against
over-localizing a genuine universal). The test is: **does the definition's normative
content, its Domain-Context Voice, and its Primary Consumers wire it to cav2's OWN object
rows/tree/model/spaces** — so that seeding it into a minted domain would hand that domain
cav2's populated content (re-fusing the two System-5 levels, "the mold becomes a casting
of itself")?

- **inherit-baseline** — the normative statement is a **method/invariant/primitive FORM**
  that any governed repo's *own* actions/artifacts must satisfy. cav2 appears only as
  cited precedent, never as content the domain resolves to. Each such verdict is justified
  below by pointing at the *form* (a rule over `any` action/axis/artifact) and confirming
  the cav2-specific naming is only in the illustrative Domain-Context Voice.
- **localize** — the definition **is about cav2** (D9), cav2's own repo/tree/model/spaces
  (D1, D6, D41, D42, D19), or names cav2's own object rows/instances as its content. The
  domain authors its own; seeding cav2's instance re-fuses levels.
- **already** — Mint's templates/additions already carry the FORM (per `SPEC.md`
  inheritance table + additions #1/#2 and the constitution/promotion-policy/definitions
  templates).

## Guardrail check (did I over-localize a universal?)

D42, D43, D44, D50): each was localized **because its normative content or its substance-bearing
Domain-Context names cav2's own rows/tree/model/capabilities**, not because cav2 authored it. Where
the FORM was separable from cav2's instance (D3, D10, D11, D13, D14, D31, D40), I kept it
inherit-baseline and flagged the strippable cav2 wiring. D29/D22 are genuinely universal but land in
`already` (Mint already carries the non-collapse form) rather than a redundant fresh seed.
