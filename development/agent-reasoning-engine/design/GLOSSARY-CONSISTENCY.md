## Design-only clarifications

`Effect Dispatcher`, `Intent Projector`, `Authority Resolver`, `Catalog Loader`,
`Observation Admitter`, `Receipt Assembler`, and `Replay/Revalidation Service`
are component labels, not definition candidates. They describe responsibility
boundaries and receive no canonical IDs.

No glossary conflict blocks plan. The unresolved applicability owner,
challenge-closure policy, artifact admission, and anti-bias program remain
explicit planning residue.
