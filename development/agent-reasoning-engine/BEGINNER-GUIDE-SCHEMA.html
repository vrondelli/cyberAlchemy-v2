01 · Purpose

One writing contract, four promises

The schema turns a familiar research-dispatch lifecycle into a focused learning path. It preserves what the orchestrator author already knows while making the evidence-to-judgment boundary explicit.

Source-backed

Govern the investigation

Map sources read-only, challenge the exact sheet, and obtain explicit confirmation before registration.

Source-backed

Close the evidence

Preserve verbatim explorer returns, then synthesize, review, close citations, write, and audit.

Source-backed

Keep the seam visible

Closed findings are evidence; no current edge admits them into a reasoning snapshot.

Source-backed

Learn the separate job

Trace route advice and semantic judgment without mistaking either for permission to act.

The schema’s central lesson is short: Governed research is evidence—not semantic truth, automatic engine input, or permission.

05 · Guardrails

Clear language, strict claims

The schema must simplify how the project is explained without simplifying away the boundaries that make the explanation true.

What the guide may say

  • Two private local library packages have executable tests.
  • Each package has behavior that can be traced from fixtures.
  • Both return advice without product authority.
  • The larger engine remains proposed and blocked.
  • Orchestrator patterns provide useful, qualified comparisons.
  • A confirmed research lifecycle can be shown as a teaching example.
  • research.md preserves verbatim returns; findings.md follows evidence closure.

What it must never imply

  • The whole Agent Reasoning Engine is implemented.
  • The resolver and evaluator form an end-to-end runtime.
  • Matching words mean matching owners or schemas.
  • The orchestrator is currently integrated with the engine.
  • A result grants permission to act.
  • Passing tests prove integration, release, or deployment.
  • The next implementation task is selected.
  • The example is an active or registered dispatch.
  • Research closeout proves a finding true.
  • Findings flow into the reasoning engine automatically.
Human gate

Mechanical checks are not enough

The teammate should answer at least four of these questions accurately in their own words and trace one example from each package.

Why does research closeout not make a finding true or feed the engine?
Which decision remains after launch authorization?
How do recommendation and effect permission differ?
What does each local package return?
Where does each ACI comparison stop?
Why does existing ACI not prove integration?

07 · Reader terms

Five boundary terms worth keeping straight

These local explanations identify both the useful comparison and its stopping point. They do not create shared canonical definitions.

Research-dispatch bridge

A governed investigation used to introduce a different local job.

Do not confuse: closeout does not prove truth, automatic input, or an active run.

Teaching order

The ten questions answered from launch boundary to safe contribution.

Do not confuse: it is not a runtime pipeline.

Reasoning snapshot

The exact facts and rules held fixed for one semantic evaluation.

Do not confuse: it is not an ACI event or projection.

Semantic recommendation

The answer derived from the facts and rules used for one review.

Do not confuse: it is not exact-effect permission.

Teammate comprehension gate

The teammate retells both paths and boundaries in their own words.

Do not confuse: clarity does not authorize code work.
