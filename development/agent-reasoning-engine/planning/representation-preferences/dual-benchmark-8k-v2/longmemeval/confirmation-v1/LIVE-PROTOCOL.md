## Fixed decision rule

Require all8answer slots complete, all performed model/judge usage known and allocated, all requests within8K, all4treatment answers correct by the pinned native rubric, treatment accuracy nonregressing relative to control, and at least15% total input-plus-output savings including each arm's ingestion, answers and judges. Cached input and reasoning are included in provider parent totals, never subtracted or double-counted. Failed attempts remain in actual cost. Report per-task and aggregate metrics with fixed denominators. Infrastructure/unknown-source/accounting gaps yield inconclusive evidence; scientific failures become counterevidence, not dropped rows or relaxed gates.

The four-task confirmation remains bounded, not full LongMemEval clearance. ContextBench still requires its own perfect native outcome and15% savings. Four-worker capacity follows a separately reviewed stage after two-worker timing/storage evidence; worker count does not itself establish token efficiency.
