# Two-worker recovery campaign

Candidate generation supervisor. It requires a fresh evidence directory and a separate source directory, constructs their shared storage/persistence adapter, and freezes the fixed task IDs and two-worker count before execution. Both workers share the health stop. They finish or abort their current calls before the supervisor writes any final record. A failed task is never retried or replaced; tasks not dispatched after the stop remain explicit in the final denominator.

Successful pairs retain their existing result files. A compact global seal references every fixed pair, verifies the original arm result hashes and receipt accounting, and marks native scoring pending. The validator is not a native quality evaluator or full request-byte verifier. An execution failure retains attached receipts and known/unknown costs. If accounting is absent, contradictory or explicitly unknown, it preserves coherent known usage, keeps raw reports, marks the model-call count unknown and holds the full 192,000-token pair reservation rather than claiming zero spend. Reconciliation errors cannot remove a fixed outcome. Final persistence failure carries terminal outcomes back to the caller.
