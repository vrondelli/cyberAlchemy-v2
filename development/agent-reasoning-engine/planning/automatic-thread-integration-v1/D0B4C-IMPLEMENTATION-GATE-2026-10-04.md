## Why this is the next dependency

D0b.4b now proves the exact installed source tree and restricted structural
graph, but intentionally reports callback origin as unproved and establishes
no serving epoch. D0b.4d would otherwise bind a permit to a graph that can
change through Cordis, Loader or Agent Presets before the append/model boundary.
D0b.4c supplies the missing runtime identity and revocation primitive without
prematurely claiming Session control.

The source audit also found that a Session-only patch is insufficient and that
the D0b.4b manifest must be regenerated after the new private patch. Both are
inside this gate; neither may be treated as an incidental implementation edit.
