# W1-L1 — local containment and ACI conformance

Question: can local evidence stay explicitly non-integrated, and can verified
ACI-owned facts feed the evaluator without a second writer or receipt authority?

Tasks: [`TASK-ARE-ACI-CONFORMANCE-001`](../tasks/TASK-ARE-ACI-CONFORMANCE-001.md),
[`TASK-ARE-INPUTS-001`](../tasks/TASK-ARE-INPUTS-001.md),
[`TASK-ARE-GATES-001 / SWU-ARE-012, SWU-ARE-013, and SWU-ARE-015`](../tasks/TASK-ARE-GATES-001.md),
[`TASK-ARE-PROTOCOL-001 / SWU-ARE-003`](../tasks/TASK-ARE-PROTOCOL-001.md), and
[`TASK-ARE-REPLAY-001 / SWU-ARE-004`](../tasks/TASK-ARE-REPLAY-001.md).

`SWU-ARE-002` is superseded and cannot be selected. Integrated command/event,
join/close, receipt, cursor, and effect-intent facts are ACI-owned.

Two lanes are visible:

1. **W1A local-only containment:** `SWU-ARE-019` precedes any local
   `SWU-ARE-010/011/012/013/015` evidence. Every output remains
   `local_only_non_integrated`. Completing W1A cannot open W1B.
2. **W1B integrated conformance:** `SWU-ARE-016` admits a complete external
   fixture set; `SWU-ARE-017` and `SWU-ARE-018` then prove outbound
   semantic-reference and inbound ACI actor-cut mappings. `SWU-ARE-003` is
   optional and starts only after a Bus `PASS`. `SWU-ARE-004` joins accepted
   ACI facts and semantic artifacts for historical replay.

Entry gates:

- one explicitly selected SWU;
- complete Task Session closeout synchronization for that SWU;
- `G-ARE-LOCAL-CONTAINMENT` for W1A; or
- current external owner receipts and `G-ARE-ACI-CONFORMANCE` for W1B.

Blockers/gaps: external ACI/Bus/ACP/APT maturity and conformance; missing
closeout bindings; missing retained facts/bytes blocks replay but must not
trigger revalidation.

Evidence artifacts: local-only posture/identity zero-call matrix; external
fixture admission receipt; semantic-reference and actor-cut mapping reports;
optional Bus mapping report; intent/authority/entry/effort/technique reports;
immutable replay zero-call report; per-SWU receipts.

Promotion: G-ARE-L1 checks non-invention, sole-writer/receipt authority,
partial failure, timeouts, joins, publication/admission separation,
provenance, challenge transport, replay, and unchanged evaluator code/outcome
algebra. Local-only evidence alone cannot satisfy G-ARE-L1 integration.
