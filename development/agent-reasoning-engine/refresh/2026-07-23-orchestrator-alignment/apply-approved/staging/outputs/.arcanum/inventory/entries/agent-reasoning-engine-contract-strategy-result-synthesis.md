# Agent reasoning-engine contract research result

This is a non-authority Inventory read model of research dispatch
`2026-07-22-agent-reasoning-engine-contract`. It records the independently
accepted research contract without promoting a definition, constitution,
runtime, implementation task, or reasoning-engine claim.

## Accepted contract

The current CyberAlchemy Orchestrator checkout is not a pre-action reasoning
engine. It supplies governed dispatch and workflow coordination, but does not
own an executable relation that determines whether admissible facts support or
defeat a problem-level conclusion.

The smallest defensible first build/test slice is
**`semantic-judgment evaluator@1`**: a deterministic, replayable evaluator over
one finite, owner-authorized rule catalog with typed abstention, defeat,
deferral, escalation, invalidation, and a non-authorizing positive result. At
this research dispatch's 2026-07-22 closeout, its status was **UNWITNESSED
CANDIDATE** because the dispatch produced a research contract, not executable
witness evidence. The general pre-action reasoning-engine label remained KILL
for the first slice.

The accepted architecture keeps three non-substitutable layers:

1. a full CAV2-D37 entry Gate before any actor/evaluator call and a separate
   exact-effect Gate after the semantic receipt;
2. a replayable actor-protocol kernel that owns commands, events, attempts,
   joins, retries, journals, and idempotent effect delivery; and
3. a separately versioned semantic evaluator that owns applicability,
   admissibility, reduction, defeat, abstention, and its reasoning receipt.

The witness invariant is strict: entry `block` or `defer` produces zero actor
and evaluator calls; entry `pass` permits only the bounded run; no effect occurs
without both a positive semantic outcome and an exact-effect Gate `pass`.

## Current-state addendum — 2026-07-23

The original research conclusion was historically correct. SWU-ARE-001 now
supplies a bounded local L0 witness for `semantic-judgment evaluator@1`: two
rule families, typed counterfactuals, byte-identical replay, persisted order,
and a test-only idempotent effect boundary pass under its terminal receipt.

This change does not promote the whole reasoning engine. `G-ARE-L0` owner
promotion is still pending; the actor runtime, real effect adapter, executable
ACI binding, higher-layer durability, and anti-bias efficacy remain absent.

## Effort and anti-bias boundary

Effort is a controller, not evidence. The accepted T0-T4 routing ranges from
read-only preflight through bounded analysis, executable witness testing,
contested dialectic, and protected-effect review. Higher effort, more agents,
or consensus do not increase truth, confidence, or authority. Rule- and
effect-owned requirements may force higher tiers; missing ownership, evidence,
or exact effect binding blocks or defers.

An `AntiBiasVector` is intentionally excluded from the first witness. Until its
dimensions, interventions, observations, and validation semantics are governed
and tested, its absence is typed residue that blocks claims of bias measurement,
cancellation, or an anti-bias engine. Named challenge techniques and the CAV2-D40
challenge contract provide contestability, not proof or authority.

## Review and closeout

- Four explorers covered formal semantics, replay/effect boundaries, canonical
  admissibility, and adaptive effort routing.
- Lakatos, Gödel, and Quine rejected the initial broad framing, then returned
  PASS after the contract separated the kernel, semantic evaluator, and gates
  and added typed outcomes, counterfactuals, and non-promotion boundaries.
- The writer persisted the cited synthesis. Brandenburg required one bounded
  correction to gate ordering and the unwitnessed status, then returned
  `ACCEPT`.
- The close receipt records ten dispatch agents and one loop. Init-gate agents
  were infrastructure and are excluded from that count.
- Registration used the canonical appender because the installed `.claude`
  projection rejects the valid `synthesizer` role; this remains tooling drift,
  not reasoning-engine evidence.

## Promotion boundary

The accepted promotion contract requires executable positive, negative,
abstention, defeat, timeout, consensus-invariance, catalog-drift, revocation,
cycle, replay, revalidation, and exact-effect fixtures; a second independently
authored rule family running through unchanged evaluator code; persisted
gate/event ordering; executable or pinned applicability and challenge closure;
and explicit spec-owner plus decision-gate admission. The bounded L0 witness
satisfies part of this evidence family but does not provide the missing
whole-engine admission. Describe it as a witnessed local evaluator candidate,
not a functioning or promoted reasoning engine.
