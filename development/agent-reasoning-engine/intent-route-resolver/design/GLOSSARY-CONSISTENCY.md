# Glossary consistency

- `IntentRecord` retains the exact selected-local meaning and candidate ceiling
  from the parent engine.
- `RouteDisposition` is intentionally distinct from ResonantOS
  `RouteDecision`, Dispatch Spec, and Continuation Router output.
- `CapabilityRouteCatalogSnapshot` is intentionally distinct from the semantic
  evaluator's `RuleCatalogManifest`.
- `candidate` means one eligible advisory route, never accepted, approved, or
  executing.
- `ambiguous` preserves uncertainty rather than using confidence to hide it.
- No public Arcanum or canonical CAV2 definition is added or changed.

Verdict: **pass** with local candidate terminology only.
