# Beginner Guide Reader-Movement Result

## Result: `FLAG`

The treatment is directionally stronger than the baseline in this bounded
comparison. It preserves the technical and authority boundaries while making
the two implemented package jobs understandable before the detailed lifecycle
discussion.

The result remains `FLAG`, not `PASS`: one independent comparative review is
insufficient under the predeclared experiment rubric. This result neither
promotes the reader-movement contract nor establishes a general writing rule.

## Treatment change

The treatment:

- begins with the refund decision and the three separate permission questions;
- shows the resolver and evaluator as sibling relations before cataloging their
  files;
- introduces implementation names after the questions they answer;
- places each code-landmark table next to its package trace;
- moves detailed ledger/work-pack drift to the mutation boundary;
- merges repeated invariants and newcomer traps into correction pairs;
- preserves outcome and disposition enumerations as intentional reference
  surfaces; and
- ends by turning the opening caution into five observable newcomer abilities.

## Independent comparison

One JavaScript-capable-newcomer review recovered all five orientation answers
from both versions. The reviewer found the treatment easier because it surfaced
the sibling package relation and three-decision model earlier. The baseline's
first movement break was the early current-posture and lifecycle discussion;
the treatment delayed that break to an unexplained `SWU` in the claim-ceiling
table.

Comprehension and preference were recorded separately:

- **Comprehension:** 5/5 orientation tasks were recoverable from both versions;
  the treatment exposed tasks 1–3 earlier with less terminology held in memory.
- **Preference:** treatment, because the refund-first opening, sibling diagram,
  and adjacent trace/landmark sections were easier to follow.

The review requested three exact corrections. The treatment now expands
`Smallest Working Unit (SWU)` at first use, names private `cyberAlchemy-v2`
instead of unexplained `CAV2`, and describes the 90-minute section as tests plus
two package traces. Those corrections have not received a second independent
reader review.

## Residue and next evidence

To reach this experiment's `PASS` gate, at least one more independent target
reader must perform the same five tasks against the final treatment. The review
should also record first reread, completion time, and false-confidence errors.

Until then:

- keep the treatment as a candidate working guide;
- keep the reader-movement contract conditional and human-reviewed;
- do not copy private guide prose into public Arcanum; and
- do not infer lifecycle synchronization, implementation authorization,
  publication, deployment, or promotion from this experiment.
