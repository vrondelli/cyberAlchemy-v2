## Fixture Families

Every reusable hard-gate detector needs known-good, known-bad, representative
domain, and false-positive fixtures. Initial Body War fixtures remain project-
profile data and cannot promote the candidate core.

## Required Browser Matrices

1. Task/control/state/mode coverage from the frozen baseline.
2. Activation/result/dismissal/restoration for every interactive surface.
3. Relation semantics for default, selected, portal, Path forward/reverse,
   narrow, grayscale, forced colors and reduced motion.
4. Contrast measurements with token, state, graphical role, runtime and ratio.
5. Request/console/teardown ledgers proving loopback-only, no persisted token,
   no unexpected errors and owned cleanup.

## Planned Receipt Shape

The post-change receipt binds source/build/profile/baseline/runtime digests,
claim IDs, baseline/current locators, atomic delta, acceptance result, residue,
regression link, authority/privacy/human ceilings and artifact hashes. It
cannot emit a universal score or a release/production claim.

## Claim Ceiling

Passing these planned contracts may prove one local Body War semantic-
navigation prototype under the frozen profile. It cannot prove complete
accessibility, human comprehension, ontology validity, promotion, release,
deployment, production parity, or cross-project calibration.
