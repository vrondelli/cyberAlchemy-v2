# ARE alpha / early-supporter offer

## Storefront copy

The first 100. A year at the workbench.

The first 100 customers receive the downloadable Agent Reasoning Engine alpha,
one year of ongoing ARE updates and upgrades as they are released, and direct
support from the maker for questions, troubleshooting, discussing improvements
and sharing feedback.

## Applied interpretation

- The one-year period starts at purchase, not at a future stable release.
- Both upgrades and direct support are included for that same 12-month period.
- The first-100 offer applies at every accepted price. Existing $200 base and
  hidden $50–$1,000 range are preserved. There are no new software-rights tiers.
- The update promise has no fixed release cadence; there is no guaranteed
  response time, around-the-clock availability, custom implementation service,
  roadmap delivery, or commitment to ship every suggestion.
- There is no automatic renewal or subscription. A later service offer remains
  undefined, and the software license is not changed by the service period.

## Product maturity

The site now clearly says alpha, including in the institutional product overview,
shop badge, release facts, bag, checkout review, and preview-summary export.
It explains that bugs, unfinished parts and breaking changes are possible and
that users should test the release in their environment before relying on it.
It does not invent a release number, production guarantee or stable-release date.

## Customer limit

Only the cap (100 customers) is stated. No sales or remaining-place counter is
fabricated. An item in a bag does not reserve a place. Previewing checkout does
not allocate eligibility, begin a year, or grant updates or support.
Server-side customer eligibility, safe concurrent allocation, verified payment,
confirmed upgrade access and the real support destination are launch blockers.
See CHECKOUT-CONTRACT.md and LAUNCH.md.

## Still to confirm with the maker

The actual support contact channel and delivery process, the first real alpha
archive/version, and post-year-one arrangements remain unpublished. The site
does not silently invent any of them.

## Package status

The files are a local website prototype, not the Agent Reasoning Engine or a
working commerce backend. Product images are artwork, not physical merchandise.
All six legend scenes and the complete legend reader remain intact.
