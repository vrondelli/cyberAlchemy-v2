SYNVORA / AGENT GEAR

FEATURED WORK / 001 · ALPHA

Agent
Reasoning Engine.

A second skin for your agent.

Your way of working, connected.

A.R.E. brings language, context, memory and tools into one coherent working experience. It helps your agent understand what you mean, preserve useful progress, and adapt as the task changes.

It serves both sides of the conversation: explanations and control for you; relevant context, explicit tool contracts and feedback for your agent.

Two cycles, one engine.

The reasoning cycle connects the goal, evidence and next action. The evolution cycle uses observed weaknesses to form hypotheses, compare experiments and recommend improvements. Applying an engine change remains a separate decision.

AN ILLUSTRATIVE MODEL, NOT A LIVE ENGINE DEMO

What is the question, really?

A task arrives with context, constraints, and things that are not yet known. This is where an instrument of reasoning begins: making room for what matters.

What deserves consideration?

Possibilities are not decisions. The question is how to examine alternatives, recognize missing information, and make the next choice more deliberate.

What is the next useful step?

A considered next step might be an action, another question, or a decision to stop. These are the design questions behind the gear—not a claim about an implemented workflow.

Planned downloadable alpha; terms pending.

ARE is planned as a downloadable alpha. No ARE engine release is delivered in this sandbox. Final license terms are under review. Expect bugs and changes while the engine develops; test it with your own setup. This is not access to a hosted engine. The base price is $200 USD, with an editable price selector in the shop.

The first 100 customers

One year of ongoing ARE updates and upgrades as they are released, plus direct support from the maker for that same year. Ask questions, work through issues, share feedback, and discuss improvements. The period starts on purchase, and the benefits are the same at every accepted price within the first 100 customers.

There is no fixed release cadence, response-time guarantee, or commitment to build every requested feature. The actual package, version, system requirements, and support channel still need to be published before sales open. This preview does not process payments or reserve places.

FIELD NOTE / 01 · THE IDEA

What we mean
by agent gear.

The agent is not the whole story.

When we talk about agent gear, we mean the instruments around an agent: the tools and systems that give its work structure, continuity, and a connection to the world.

The phrase is deliberately practical. Gear is something you equip for a purpose. It has a job to do. It can be understood, selected, fitted, maintained, and changed.

Equip the mind. Don’t replace its purpose.

We use the workshop as our organizing idea. A maker does not need to become the adventurer. The maker needs to understand the journey well enough to build equipment that belongs on it.

For Synvora, that means beginning with questions about reasoning, memory, context, and action. These are territories for the work, not a claim that we already offer a finished product in each one.

A category with a clear center.

The Agent Reasoning Engine is the first piece of work we introduce on this site. It belongs to the category because it concerns the equipment of reasoning: the space between a question and a useful next step.

Our name for the category is an invitation to think differently about what gets built. Not only “What can this agent do?” but also “What should this agent have to work with?”

FIELD NOTE / 03 · THE MARK

A mind
at the center.

A core. A surround. The space between them.

Synvora’s mark places a small hexagonal core inside three fitted forms. The surrounding pieces hold together without becoming a single closed mass.

Our interpretation is simple: intelligence at the center, instruments around it.

A signature, not a system diagram.

The mark does not describe the technical architecture of a product. Its pieces are not assigned to three hidden modules. It is a visual idea about equipment, fit, and the relationship between a mind and its tools.

On the site, it is a studio signature: a small indication of where the work comes from.

Made to be carried.

The symbol should make sense on a page, beside a product name, or by itself. It should remain recognizable without extra explanation.

Some marks identify the object.
This one identifies the workshop.
