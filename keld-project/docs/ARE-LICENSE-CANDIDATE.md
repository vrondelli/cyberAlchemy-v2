# ARE reciprocal-source resale license — candidate clauses

**Status:** Private drafting candidate, 2026-09-22. **Not a governing license.**
Do not attach this file to a customer archive, advertise it as current terms,
or use it to authorize a sale. The covered files, copyright owners, third-party
notices, purchaser-verification mechanism and final legal terms remain unverified. The
[owner decision](decisions/ARE-RESALE-SHAREBACK-2026-09-21.md) controls the
intended product rule; qualified legal review is required before adoption.

## Proposed operative language for counsel to revise

1. **Covered Software.** “ARE” means only the owner-controlled Agent Reasoning
   Engine source and object code expressly identified in a future release
   manifest as governed by this license. Separately licensed third-party code,
   the KELD website, artwork, names, and trademarks are not relicensed here.

   A “Verified ARE Purchaser” is a person or entity with a valid paid purchase
   of an ARE copy governed by this license, evidenced by a purchase receipt or
   entitlement that can reasonably be checked. Merely receiving a copy free
   of charge does not make someone a Verified ARE Purchaser. The exact proof
   and privacy-preserving verification process must be settled before release.

2. **General permission.** Subject to these terms, a lawful ARE license holder
   may use, copy, study, modify, and share ARE without charging for the transfer
   of an ARE copy, including while using ARE in commercial work. A person may
   separately charge for hosting, consulting, or support; those services do
   not themselves qualify a software copy for paid resale.

3. **Qualifying paid resale.** A license holder may charge for distributing a
   modified ARE copy only when that version adds substantive, demonstrable new
   functionality or materially improves existing functionality. Merely changing
   presentation, names, packaging, documentation, or fixing bugs does not by
   itself qualify. The reseller must identify the functional changes and retain
   the original notices. An unchanged or nonqualifying ARE copy may not be
   sold under this proposed license.

4. **Source-code share-back.** As a condition of each qualifying paid resale,
   the reseller must offer the complete corresponding source code for the
   qualifying ARE-derived version, including the reseller's functional changes,
   to every Verified ARE Purchaser at no charge. The source must be
   in the preferred form for modification and include the build/configuration
   material the reseller is authorized to distribute and that is needed to
   work with that version. The offer must be available when paid distribution
   begins and use a practical request or download mechanism. The reseller may
   reasonably verify purchaser status but may not make an additional payment
   for that source a condition of access. The source is offered under the same
   ARE software rights and paid-resale restriction, subject to applicable
   third-party terms. A purchaser who receives this source may share it onward
   without charge, and a downstream recipient may likewise share it without
   charge. Such recipients need not prove a purchase to receive a freely
   shared copy; the purchaser check limits only the reseller's direct duty to
   fulfill a no-charge source request. None of this permits paid resale of an
   unchanged or nonqualifying ARE copy.

5. **No service or binary obligation.** Clause 4 concerns source code only.
   It does not require the reseller to provide a compiled binary, installation,
   future upgrades, security maintenance, direct human support, hosting, or
   custom development free of charge. It does not change KELD's separate
   first-100 customer service promises.

6. **Notices and independent rights.** A distributor must preserve applicable
   copyright, license and attribution notices, mark its changes, provide these
   terms to recipients, and comply separately with all third-party licenses.
   This draft grants no trademark rights and cannot restrict rights that a
   recipient already has in independently licensed components.

## Decisions required before this can become a license

- **Purchase verification:** Define acceptable proof, account recovery and a
  privacy-preserving way for a reseller to verify purchases without receiving
  KELD payment credentials. A free-copy recipient is not directly eligible for
  the reseller's direct source offer. The confirmed permission to share the
  received source onward without charge remains in force.
- **Source availability duration:** Select how long the reseller must keep the
  source offer available after it stops selling that version.
- **Legal completion:** Identify copyright owners and covered files; settle
  downstream grants, patent terms, termination/cure, warranty and liability,
  governing law, and how the obligation applies to bundled independent works.
- **Release reconciliation:** The owner approved interim, not-for-sale sandbox
  wording on 2026-09-22: “Sandbox demonstration only. No ARE engine release is
  delivered. Final license terms are under review.” That copy does not adopt
  this draft or grant ARE rights. Before any sale, approve the governing
  license and exact customer bundle, then verify every offer and artifact
  against those operative terms.

This is a custom source-available proposal, not GNU GPL or an OSI-approved
open-source license. The [GNU GPL FAQ](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.en.html)
does not require a seller to give every existing license holder a free copy;
the [Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd) requires unrestricted
resale. [Apache-2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0.html) also
permits distribution with or without modifications.
