The problem · 02 / 08

Intent rarely disappears all at once.

It drifts through a series of reasonable-looking translations.

“What the business needs”
↓
What the requirement says
↓
What the code implements

What the tests happen to check

Meaning first · 03 / 08

Start with definitions that people and tools can both inspect.

Each important definition has three connected forms:

1. Formal voice

The normative statement of the term.

2. Plain-language voice

An explanation for a human reader.

3. Domain-context voice

How the term is used in this business setting.

Definitions connect through a business ontology. An explanation helps with understanding; it does not replace the formal meaning.

An exact claim · 04 / 08

Then state what you are actually claiming.

A FormalClaim binds selected business meaning to an exact proposition, definitions, and assumptions.

Eligible(o) ∧ ValidTotal(o)
∧ means both conditions must hold.
Eligible(o) links to the exact eligibility definition.
ValidTotal(o) links to the exact total definition.
Illustrative example only. Understanding nodes attach to exact clause and expression selectors. They explain the claim; they do not change or prove it.

Human reading order · 05 / 08

Store a graph. Let a person read a story.

The claim keeps its graph identity. The understanding view puts its parts in a deliberate sequence.

1
Show the exact expression.
2
Explain the operator.
3
Explain each predicate and its bound definition.
4
Put the meaning back together.
Provenance stays attached, but it does not have to interrupt every sentence. This understanding projection is a validated candidate contract, not yet compiler-emitted.

CyberAlchemy06
Evidence, with limits · 07 / 08

“Checked” is not one thing.

DomainSpec

For exact graphs, checks applicable structural obligations with generated Lean proof units and selected conformance paths.

TDE

Derives bounded, provider-neutral test obligations from admitted executable projections. It does not invent business meaning.

EVF

Joins current evidence to exact obligations, assumptions, source, and environment.

Important: a bounded pass is not proof of the whole application or approval for production. The general end-to-end TDE/EVF lifecycle is still partial.
