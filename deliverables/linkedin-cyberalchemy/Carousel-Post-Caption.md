I have been building CyberAlchemy mostly alone, and one question keeps pulling me back: when software gets easier to produce, how do we make sure the original intent survives?

This short slide deck shows the path I am exploring: definitions in three forms, a business ontology, exact FormalClaims, human-readable understanding nodes, AlchLang and DomainSpec, then bounded tests and evidence.

Some of it works today. Some of it is still a candidate design. I have tried to make that boundary visible instead of calling the whole thing “verified.”

I am also looking for a team where I can work on formal methods, language tooling, or evidence-driven engineering with other people. If that sounds close to what you do, I would be glad to talk. And if you see a weak point in the chain, I would like to hear it.
