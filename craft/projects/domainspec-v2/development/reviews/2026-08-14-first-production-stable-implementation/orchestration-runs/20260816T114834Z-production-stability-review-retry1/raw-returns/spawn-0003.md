### F-11 — CRITICAL if included — W3 durability contradicts W4 compensation

W3 declares SettlementCreated durable at outbox append and models settlement
write plus outbox append as atomic. W4 separates persist from publish, gives
persist rollback-settlement, and compensates completed steps after later
failure; its tests prove publication failure rolls back persistence. No typed
binding establishes whether persist is the W3 operation or how an already
durable fact/outbox record can be rolled back.

Fix: owner-select a composition rule. The recommended rule is that after the W3
record-plus-outbox commit, publication failure retries from the outbox and
cannot roll back the durable fact; otherwise define and prove atomic retraction
of both state and unseen outbox bytes.

Acceptance: inject failure before/after record write, outbox append, publish
attempt, and acknowledgement; prove state/outbox/fact consistency, no erased
durable fact, idempotent at-least-once retry, and identical TS/Python/Rust
semantics.

Classification: exclude from first slice; semantic/state-owner decision needed
before reopen.
