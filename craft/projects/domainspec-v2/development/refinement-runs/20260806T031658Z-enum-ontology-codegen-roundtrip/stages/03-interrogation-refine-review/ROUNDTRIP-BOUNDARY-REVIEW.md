## Evidence boundary

The existing provider and direct-source adapter are valid inputs to the future
design. They do not prove generation, independent evaluation, composition
observation, or independent replay. The BTE L5 document supplies the desired
proof discipline; the current development proof engine is a precedent with
known self-authorship shortcuts, not the architecture oracle.
