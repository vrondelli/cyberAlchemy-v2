# Research Decision

## Decision

Continue with local repository evidence only. No external research is required
for this refinement run.

## Basis

The Context Builder pack and independent review cover the acceptance-critical
questions:

- the exact admitted TypeScript representation and current provider behavior;
- the adapter and runner's actual observation/evaluation boundaries;
- the canonical-versus-observed Enum property distinction;
- the BTE L0-L8 and one-proof-unit discipline;
- concrete false-green paths in the current development witnesses; and
- the already confirmed evolution pressure from TypeScript to Python and Rust.

The remaining questions are local owner/design choices, not missing external
facts: canonical definition ownership, generated-file policy, precise IR size,
composition sequencing, and target package layout.

## Trigger for later bounded research

External comparison would become relevant only if Design cannot choose a
deterministic source-map/provenance format or compiler isolation mechanism from
the existing Node/TypeScript package conventions. That gap is not currently
acceptance-critical. If it appears, Refine must stop and request exact source
scope confirmation.

## Claim boundary

This decision says the local evidence is sufficient to design and plan the
prototype. It does not claim external best-practice coverage, standards
conformance, production readiness, or technology selection authority beyond
the local target.
