# N14 Design Context

## Objective

Bind the exact `lifecycle` sequencing input without treating it as a behavior
selection, then isolate whether lifecycle responsibility consequences stop at each
direct canonical `owns` fact or include non-authoritative reachability over an
acyclic same-dimension path.

## Evidence Boundary

N13 B requires an independent decision for each admitted D29 dimension. The exact
clarification selects `lifecycle` as the first target. D29 describes lifecycle
ownership as direct responsibility and explicitly withholds inferred transitivity
and closure. D23 recognizes a relationship chain as one possible finite composition
path consequence, which makes derived reachability a genuine owner choice rather
than an invalid candidate.

The denominator contains 15 direct exact sources rather than 14. The increase binds
the new lifecycle clarification while retaining every N04 through N12 selection
receipt. The clarification receipt itself binds the exact N13 closeout hash; the
cumulative closeout is not a direct source because it must change for N14 and would
otherwise self-stale the denominator. Removing inverse or closure receipts would
weaken the proof that N14 does not reopen representation, generated navigation,
partial-view, or cycle-proof behavior.

Excluded are custody, write-responsibility, mixed-dimension paths, new canonical
edges or verbs, derived-result naming and provenance, materialization, representation,
storage, closure proof, partial-view absence verdicts, mandatory ownership, runtime,
numeric, packet, canonical, and implementation work.

no delegation, protected-context transfer, or multi-capability execution graph.

## Ordered Design Rules

1. Bind N13 B and exact `lifecycle` sequencing; preserve N04-N12 selections.
2. Classify only acyclic paths of length at least two whose canonical `owns` edges
   all carry the exact `lifecycle` dimension.
3. A keeps consequences on direct facts only.
4. B permits a non-authoritative derived reachability consequence without authoring
   a new canonical `owns` fact.
5. Do not choose representation, provenance, closure proof, absence verdict, or
   behavior for another dimension.
6. Present A, B, defer, and stop with aligned examples and decision effect none.
7. Stop before N14 selection, Plan, implementation, or canonical mutation.

Authority effect: `none`.
