# N41 Distill validation

Mode: Standard; one proposal track; Proposer and read-only Balancer; two rounds.

## Round 1

Proposer grouped identity, supersession, currentness, schema layout, profile admission, and packet projection into one lifecycle decision.

Balancer objection — responsibility and scope collapse: N40 already keeps currentness external, while schema, profile, and packet owners remain independent. The only immediate collision is multiple sidecars from the same consumer owner for the same boundary, predicate, and exact snapshot.

Reconciliation: retain one identity/lifecycle question and route every other subject separately.

## Round 2

Proposer compared digest-addressed coexistence with an explicit consumer-owner-authored supersession link.

Balancer objection — semantic overreach: supersession could be mistaken for invalidation, present-head currentness, or evaluator authority. Reconciliation keeps B admissible only when it preserves all prior bytes, binds both exact digests, and grants none of those meanings. A remains the recommendation because exact path/digest selection already resolves evaluation identity without adding lifecycle machinery.

## Smallest coherent unit

One collision rule for same-owner, same-boundary, same-predicate, same-snapshot sidecars. It closes because the identity tuple, competing records, owner, and allowed consequence are explicit. It recomposes into N39 placement and N40 snapshot-relative evaluation without changing either decision.

Frame expiry: revisit if a canonical currentness or lifecycle registry is admitted. Premortem: the likely failure is treating a newer filename or supersession link as currentness; guardrail by exact digest binding and explicit non-currentness language.

Historical preselection verdict: `pass-two-admissible-identity-lifecycle-actions-owner-pending`. N41-A was subsequently selected; N42 remains a separate owner gate.
