whether a directed canonical `belongs-to` closed path using at least two exact
admitted D30 kinds is invalid merely because the combined path closes, or whether
its independently kind-bearing direct facts remain allowed.

## Evidence Boundary And Frontier Order

The exact eight-source denominator is closed in `DESIGN-SCOPE-MANIFEST.json`. N22 A
rejects only same-kind longer cycles and explicitly excludes mixed-kind paths. D30
makes `classification` and `home-assignment` pairwise non-overlapping and permits
different parents under distinct kinds. That supports A as the non-collapse
recommendation, but D30 withholds cycle policy, so B remains authority-admissible.

closure is prohibited, no complete absence predicate exists for a closure proof to
evaluate. No source chooses N23.
