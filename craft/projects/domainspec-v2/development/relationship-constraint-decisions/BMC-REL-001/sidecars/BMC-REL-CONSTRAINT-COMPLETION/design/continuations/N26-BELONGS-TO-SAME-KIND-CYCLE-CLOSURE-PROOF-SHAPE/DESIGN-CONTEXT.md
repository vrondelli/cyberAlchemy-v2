# N26 Design Context

## Bounded objective

Select only the evidence obligations that make the N22 same-kind directed-cycle
predicate evaluable for one exact admitted D30 membership kind after N25 A. Do not
select closure metadata, serialization, loader behavior, or any adjacent semantic
facet.

## Frontier ordering and scope resolution

1. N25 A reports `not-evaluated-incomplete-closure` until relevant closure exists.
2. D02 requires declared boundary identity and kind, complete eligible endpoint
   population, placement scope, and relation-directional adjacency evidence.
3. N22 is a directed cycle predicate over one exact D30 kind. Every potential cycle
   edge is discoverable when the eligible Entity population and every outgoing
   canonical `belongs-to` adjacency for that exact kind are complete.
4. Complete incoming adjacency is independent under D02 and may be required as a
   stronger symmetric envelope, but the directed-cycle predicate does not make it
   logically necessary.
5. D30 kind identity is part of the predicate filter. N26 is relation-local, while
   completeness must be asserted for the exact kind under evaluation; a pooled
   kind-free relation claim is inadmissible.
6. N12 is analogy only and supplies no `belongs-to` consent.

No source chooses N26. A is recommended as the minimal sufficient proof. B remains
admissible as a stronger evidence contract with additional acquisition cost.
