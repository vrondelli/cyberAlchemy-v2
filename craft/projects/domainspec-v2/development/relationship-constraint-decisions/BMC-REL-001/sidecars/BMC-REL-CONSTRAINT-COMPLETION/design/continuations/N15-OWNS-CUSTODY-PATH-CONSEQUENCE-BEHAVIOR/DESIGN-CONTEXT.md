# N15 Design Context

## Objective

Bind the exact `custody` sequencing input without treating it as a behavior
selection, then isolate whether custody responsibility consequences stop at each
direct canonical `owns` fact or include non-authoritative reachability over an
acyclic same-dimension path.

## Evidence Boundary

N13 B requires an independent decision for each admitted D29 dimension. N14 A keeps
lifecycle responsibility direct-only and may not be copied into custody. The exact
clarification selects `custody` as the next target. D29 describes custody as direct
responsibility for stewardship, possession, placement, or operational keeping and
explicitly withholds inferred transitivity and closure. D23 recognizes a relationship
chain as one possible finite composition-path consequence, making derived custody
reachability a genuine owner choice rather than an invalid candidate.

The denominator contains seven direct exact sources. This is deliberately smaller
than N14's 15-source denominator: the immutable N14 owner receipt directly preserves
N04 through N13 and binds the unchanged registry, while N15 needs only N13's
per-dimension rule, N14's lifecycle boundary, the custody sequencing receipt, and
the exact D23/D29, registry, canonical `owns`, and packet-row evidence. Repeating
N04-N12 directly would add no N15 claim and would weaken Distill's minimum-unit
boundary. The mutable cumulative closeout is transitively bound by the sequencing
receipt and is not a direct source, avoiding a self-staling denominator cycle.

Excluded are lifecycle reopening, write-responsibility, mixed-dimension paths, new
canonical edges or verbs, derived-result naming and provenance, materialization,
representation, storage, closure proof, mandatory ownership, runtime, numeric,
packet, canonical, and implementation work.

## Ordered Design Rules

1. Bind N13 B, N14 A, and exact `custody` sequencing.
2. Classify only acyclic paths of length at least two whose canonical `owns` edges
   all carry exact dimension `custody`.
3. A keeps custody consequences on direct facts only.
4. B permits a non-authoritative derived custody reachability consequence without
   authoring a new canonical `owns` fact.
5. Do not reopen lifecycle, choose write-responsibility behavior, or select naming,
   provenance, representation, closure proof, or runtime behavior.
6. Present A, B, defer, and stop with aligned examples and decision effect none.
7. Stop before N15 selection, Plan, implementation, or canonical mutation.

Authority effect: `none`.
