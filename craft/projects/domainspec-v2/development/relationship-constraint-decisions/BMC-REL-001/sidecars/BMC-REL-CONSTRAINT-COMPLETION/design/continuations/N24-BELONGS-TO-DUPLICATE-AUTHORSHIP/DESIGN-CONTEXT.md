## Objective

Close N23 A without interpreting independently allowed mixed-kind facts as permission
to repeat one exact kind-bearing proposition. N24 asks what happens when the same
canonical `belongs-to` proposition is authored more than once.

## Exact Duplicate Predicate

An N24 duplicate has the same canonical verb, source Entity concept ID, target
Entity concept ID, exact admitted D30 membership-kind ID, and admitted authority or
feature context. Different exact kinds are distinct facts. The same source and kind
with different targets is a D07 competing-parent conflict, not a duplicate.

## Frontier Order

says observed duplicate violations remain independently enforceable whenever their
evidence is present, while absence verdicts depend on closure. Duplicate authorship
therefore precedes partial-view evaluation. Canonical `belongs-to` already declares
inverse `none`, so no source-backed inverse choice precedes N24. N01, N03, and N08
are stable decomposition analogies only; each forbids a global duplicate policy.
No source chooses N24.
