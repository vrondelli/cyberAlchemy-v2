## Objective

Close N26 A without extending cycle-proof evidence into path behavior. N27 isolates
the prerequisite governance question for future consequences of acyclic, same-kind
canonical `belongs-to` paths of length at least two: will one behavior policy govern
every current and later admitted D30 kind, or will each exact kind require its own
later behavior decision?

## Evidence Boundary

The exact nine-source denominator is closed in `DESIGN-SCOPE-MANIFEST.json`. N26 A
selects only the outgoing-adjacency closure proof for already prohibited same-kind
cycles. D30 makes `classification` and `home-assignment` pairwise non-overlapping and
expressly withholds transitivity. The canonical card already fixes inverse kind to
`none`, so no inverse choice precedes N27. The packet keeps semantic path behavior and
representation residue separate. N13 is analogy only and supplies no `belongs-to`
authority.
