# N30 Design Context

N29 exact A closes the last current same-kind path-consequence decision:
classification consequences remain on direct authored facts. N28 and N28
already do the same for classification and home-assignment. Those three decisions do not settle
paths that traverse more than one D30 membership kind.

N23 exact A permits a directed canonical `belongs-to` path to close across membership kinds,
but expressly does not select transitivity, derived edges, or closure semantics.
D23 recognizes a finite relationship chain as a possible composition-path
consequence. D30 keeps each edge's membership kind pairwise distinct, treats the current
membership kinds as direct membership meanings, and withholds inferred transitivity and
closure.

The smallest coherent unresolved unit is therefore a topology-admitted path of at
least two canonical `belongs-to` facts that uses at least two admitted membership kinds and has
distinct endpoints. N30 asks whether it has only the consequences of its direct
facts or also a non-authoritative reachability result that retains the ordered
per-edge membership-kind trace. The trace is semantic non-collapse, not registry
precedence or a selected storage/provenance shape.

Mixed closed-path self-reachability, same-kind behavior, kind-free
membership consequence, canonical derived edges, naming, provenance, representation,
completeness, mandatory membership, runtime/numeric behavior, and all other
relations remain outside N30.

## Ordered Design Rules

1. Bind N23 A, N27 B, and N28 and N29 A exactly.
2. Classify only topology-admitted directed paths of length at least two that use
   at least two admitted D30 membership kinds and have distinct endpoints.
3. A leaves consequences only on direct kind-bearing facts.
4. B permits non-authoritative reachability while retaining the ordered edge
   membership-kind trace and authoring no new canonical `belongs-to` fact.
5. Do not infer kind-free membership consequence or registry precedence.
6. Leave mixed closed-path self-reachability, same-kind paths, naming,
   provenance, representation, and completeness separate.
7. Present A, B, defer, and stop with aligned examples and decision effect none.
8. Stop before N30 selection, Plan, implementation, or canonical mutation.

Authority effect: `none`.
