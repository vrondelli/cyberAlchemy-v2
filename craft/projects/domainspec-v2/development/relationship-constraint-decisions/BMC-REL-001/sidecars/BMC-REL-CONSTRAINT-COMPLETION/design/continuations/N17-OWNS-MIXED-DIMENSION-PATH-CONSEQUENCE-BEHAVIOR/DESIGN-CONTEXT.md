# N17 Design Context

N16 exact A closes the last current same-dimension path-consequence decision:
write-responsibility consequences remain on direct authored facts. N14 and N15
already do the same for lifecycle and custody. Those three decisions do not settle
paths that traverse more than one D29 dimension.

N07 exact A permits a directed canonical `owns` path to close across dimensions,
but expressly does not select transitivity, derived edges, or closure semantics.
D23 recognizes a finite relationship chain as a possible composition-path
consequence. D29 keeps each edge's dimension pairwise distinct, treats the current
dimensions as direct responsibility axes, and withholds inferred transitivity and
closure.

The smallest coherent unresolved unit is therefore a topology-admitted path of at
least two canonical `owns` facts that uses at least two admitted dimensions and has
distinct endpoints. N17 asks whether it has only the consequences of its direct
facts or also a non-authoritative reachability result that retains the ordered
per-edge dimension trace. The trace is semantic non-collapse, not registry
precedence or a selected storage/provenance shape.

Mixed closed-path self-reachability, same-dimension behavior, dimension-free
responsibility, canonical derived edges, naming, provenance, representation,
completeness, mandatory ownership, runtime/numeric behavior, and all other
relations remain outside N17.

## Ordered Design Rules

1. Bind N07 A, N13 B, and N14 through N16 A exactly.
2. Classify only topology-admitted directed paths of length at least two that use
   at least two admitted D29 dimensions and have distinct endpoints.
3. A leaves consequences only on direct dimension-bearing facts.
4. B permits non-authoritative reachability while retaining the ordered edge
   dimension trace and authoring no new canonical `owns` fact.
5. Do not infer dimension-free responsibility or registry precedence.
6. Leave mixed closed-path self-reachability, same-dimension paths, naming,
   provenance, representation, and completeness separate.
7. Present A, B, defer, and stop with aligned examples and decision effect none.
8. Stop before N17 selection, Plan, implementation, or canonical mutation.

Authority effect: `none`.
