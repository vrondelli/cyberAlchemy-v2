# N12 Design Context

## Objective

Continue from exact N11 selection A. N11 withholds a positive N06 cycle-freedom
verdict when relevant closure evidence is absent. N12 isolates which already-selected
D02 directional closure axes are required to establish the whole-boundary
same-dimension `owns` cycle-freedom claim.

## Exact Predicate

Both action options require a declared boundary identity and kind, complete eligible
Entity population within that boundary, complete placement scope, and complete
canonical outgoing `owns` adjacency for every eligible Entity. Option A stops there.
Option B additionally requires complete incoming canonical `owns` adjacency for every
eligible Entity.

Because `owns` is directed `Entity -> Entity`, complete outgoing adjacency plus a
complete eligible Entity population is the minimal proposed proof: any directed cycle
would be composed of outgoing edges among those Entities. Requiring incoming closure
is a stronger symmetric proof obligation, not a different topology policy.

## Evidence Boundary

D02 selects explicit directional closure and keeps outgoing and incoming adjacency
independent. It requires the relevant endpoint population for whole-boundary
conformance but does not choose the subset needed for N06 cycle-freedom. N11 makes
that subset consequential. N12 does not define closure serialization, infer closure
from `feature_scope`, create per-dimension closure metadata, or reopen D02 evaluation modes.

## Obligations

1. Bind exact N11 A and preserve immediate failure for observed prohibited cycles.
2. Preserve D02's independent directional axes rather than inferring from tokens.
3. Compare the minimal outgoing-plus-population proof with a symmetric incoming-and-outgoing proof.
4. Require boundary identity/kind, eligible Entity population completeness, and placement scope under both actions.
5. Do not introduce per-dimension closure metadata or reopen project-only evaluation.
6. Do not select transitivity, mandatory ownership, representation, runtime, numeric, or other-relation policy.
7. Present A, B, defer, and stop with aligned examples and no decision effect.
8. Stop before N12 selection, Plan, or implementation.
