# N13 Design Context

## Objective

Close N12 exact A without generalizing its cycle-proof evidence, then isolate whether
future same-dimension `owns` path-consequence behavior is governed uniformly across
all admitted D29 dimensions or independently per dimension.

## Evidence Boundary

D29 makes lifecycle, custody, and write-responsibility pairwise non-overlapping and
explicitly withholds transitivity. D23 recognizes finite composition-path consequences
but does not grant general closure. N05 selected uniformity only for cycle rejection;
it cannot silently select path-consequence governance. N13 therefore chooses only the
future policy partition, not whether any path produces a derived result.

Excluded are direct-versus-derived behavior, mixed-dimension paths, new canonical
edges or verbs, result naming, storage, materialization, closure reopening, mandatory
ownership, representation, runtime, numeric, packet, canonical, and implementation work.

## Ordered Design Rules

1. Bind exact N12 A and preserve N04-N12 selections.
2. Classify only acyclic same-dimension canonical `owns` paths of length at least two.
3. Ask only whether later behavior has one shared policy or one per dimension.
4. Do not choose direct-only or derived-reachability behavior at this gate.
5. Do not include mixed-dimension paths or infer uniformity from N05.
6. Present A, B, defer, and stop with aligned examples and decision effect none.
7. Stop before N13 selection, Plan, implementation, or canonical mutation.

Authority effect: `none`.
