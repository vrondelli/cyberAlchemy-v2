# N20 Design Context

## Objective

Close N19 A as a governance-shape choice only, then present the one behavior decision
it makes possible: for every admitted D30 membership kind, reject or allow a direct
canonical `belongs-to_k(e,e)` self-edge. N19 A is not treated as behavioral consent.

## Evidence Boundary

The exact six-source denominator is closed in `DESIGN-SCOPE-MANIFEST.json`. N19 A
requires one future behavior across current and later admitted kinds. D07 preserves
one-parent-per-kind uniqueness and optional membership. D30 keeps kind meanings exact
and explicitly withholds self-edge policy. The canonical card says a source belongs to
“another entity,” which supports recommending rejection, but D30 prevents that wording
from becoming an automatic verdict. The packet leaves `belongs-to.self_relations`
unresolved. Both reject and allow therefore remain owner-gated actions.

5. Exclude longer cycles, transitivity, closure, duplicates, mandatory membership,
