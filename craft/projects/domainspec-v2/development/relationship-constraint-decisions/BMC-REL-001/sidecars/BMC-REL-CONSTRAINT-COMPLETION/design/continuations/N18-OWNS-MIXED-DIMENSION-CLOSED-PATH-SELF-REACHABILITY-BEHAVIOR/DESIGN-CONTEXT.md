# N18 Design Context

N17 exact A keeps consequences on direct dimension-bearing facts for
mixed-dimension paths with distinct endpoints. That answer deliberately excludes
closed paths whose start and end Entity are the same.

N07 exact A permits a mixed-dimension directed canonical `owns` path to close, but
does not select transitivity, derived edges, or closure semantics. N04 exact A
rejects only a direct authored `owns_d(e,e)` fact. N06 rejects same-dimension longer
cycles, so N18 considers only topology-admitted closed paths that use at least two
admitted dimensions. D23 recognizes a finite relationship chain as a possible
composition-path consequence, while D29 preserves pairwise non-overlapping direct
dimensions and withholds inferred transitivity and closure.

The smallest coherent unresolved unit is therefore a topology-admitted canonical
`owns` closed path of length at least two that uses at least two admitted D29
dimensions and returns to its starting Entity. N18 asks whether the path yields no
derived self-reachability consequence or a non-authoritative result that retains
the ordered per-edge dimension trace. Such a B result would be path reachability,
not a direct or canonical self-ownership fact.

Direct self-edges, same-dimension cycles, mixed topology admission,
distinct-endpoint mixed-path consequences, dimension-free responsibility,
canonical derived edges, naming, provenance, materialization, representation,
completeness, storage, mandatory ownership, runtime/numeric behavior, and all other
relations remain outside N18.

## Ordered Design Rules

1. Bind N04 A, N06 A, N07 A, and N17 A exactly.
2. Classify only topology-admitted directed closed paths of length at least two that
   use at least two admitted D29 dimensions and have equal endpoints.
3. A produces no derived self-reachability consequence from that closed path.
4. B permits non-authoritative self-reachability while retaining the ordered edge
   dimension trace and authoring no direct or canonical `owns` self-edge.
5. Do not infer dimension-free responsibility or collapse the trace.
6. Leave topology, direct self-edge, distinct-endpoint behavior, naming, provenance,
   materialization, representation, completeness, and storage separate.
7. Present A, B, defer, and stop with aligned examples and decision effect none.
8. Stop before N18 selection, Plan, implementation, or canonical mutation.

Authority effect: `none`.
