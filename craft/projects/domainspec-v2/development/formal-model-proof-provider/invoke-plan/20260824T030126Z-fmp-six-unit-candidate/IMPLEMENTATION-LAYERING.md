## Active window and dependencies

The current active window is planning only. L0 may become the first execution window only after the exact candidate is accepted and Implementation Readiness selects it. L1 is independently blocked on the factory owner gate. L2 and L3 consume accepted predecessor receipts and may not reinterpret earlier meaning.

The composability path is `WP-FMP-002 -> waves -> tasks -> one SWU per task`. The two corpora remain separate units even though they share a provider, because provenance, denominator, expected outcomes, and the Order Saga known-red residue have different acceptance boundaries.
