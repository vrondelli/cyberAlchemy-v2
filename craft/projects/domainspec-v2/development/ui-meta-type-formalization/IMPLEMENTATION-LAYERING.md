# Implementation Layering: UI Meta-Type Formalization

> Historical layering only. Active UI implementation now follows
> [Required UI Profile Layering](../ui-profile-implementation/IMPLEMENTATION-LAYERING.md),
> where synthetic evidence gates mechanics and empirical breadth is a
> non-blocking confidence dimension.

## Purpose

Sequence UI formation work by decisions unlocked. Layer 0 proves that the
experimental method can preserve one plausible distinction and collapse one
plausibly redundant distinction before the package spends effort on all 11
labels.

## Layer Decision Table

| Layer | Decision question | Minimum working unit | Included scope | Deferred scope | Exit evidence | Promotion decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L0 - method proof | After this layer, we know whether the experiment protocol can produce stable survival and collapse evidence. | Frozen hypothesis matrix, corpus manifest, and E0 runs for Form versus Component and Adapter versus Mapping. | Constructibility, malformed negatives, neighbor separation, deterministic result shape. | All-label fan-out, candidate schemas, authority promotion. | E0 result records are reproducible and adjudication does not change rules after outcomes. | Continue to all-label experiments, repair protocol, or stop. |
| L1 - breadth | After this layer, we know which of the 11 labels have plausible bounded formation laws. | Three disjoint experiment lanes covering all labels. | Constructibility, discrimination, graph coherence, initial portability. | Candidate schema implementation and owner promotion. | All 11 have complete provisional results and named residue. | Select schema-ready, merge, relation, framework, split, or defer outcomes. |
| L2 - hardening | After this layer, we know whether surviving candidates remain credible under schema implementation, instance mutation, second-corpus replication, and adversarial review. | Candidate twins plus fixtures/tests for survivors and replication results. | Candidate schemas/cards, bounded validator rules, round trip, portability, non-collapse review. | Canonical DS-D1 and DS-D8 mutation. | Tests and replication agree; disagreements are adjudicated; private boundary remains intact. | Prepare owner packet, narrow claims, or return candidates to research. |
| L3 - handoff | After this layer, we know whether definitions-governance has enough evidence to make per-type promotion decisions. | One per-type evidence matrix and promotion/no-promotion packet. | Traceability, residue, validation receipts, owner questions. | Actual promotion and relationship mutation. | Packet covers all 11 and distinguishes evidence status from authority status. | Route to definitions-governance, relationship authority, or defer. |

## Value/Cost Boundary

| Layer | Decision value and risk reduced | Main cost drivers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L0 | Prevents a confirmation-biased method that always manufactures new types. | Two contrast-pair fixtures and result normalization. |
| L1 | Establishes the actual candidate set and exposes collapse/framework pressure early. | Corpus preparation and parallel per-type experiments. |
| L2 | Converts plausible prose into runnable evidence and tests generalization. | Schema/card authoring, validator support, replication, adversarial review. |
| L3 | Lets authority owners decide without reopening discovery. | Evidence synthesis and owner review coordination. |

## Non-Regression Guardrails

- Later layers must preserve E0 rules and result schema unless a versioned
  protocol repair invalidates and reruns prior evidence.
- Every candidate remains formation-deferred until a separate owner decision.
- DS-D8 endpoint use remains supporting graph evidence, never node authority.
- Structural validation never imports private derivation rules.
- Synthetic fixtures never replace real-corpus evidence for promotion.
- Rejected, merged, relation, framework, and split outcomes remain visible in
  the residue ledger.
