## Identity Join

The composite receipt must join, without substitution:

- two Rule IDs;
- two edge IDs;
- two obligation IDs;
- two binding IDs;
- two test/execution identities;
- two mutation denominators;
- two typed EVF receipts;
- one identical graph/source/registry digest set.

Any mismatch, omission, semantic merge, or ceiling inflation blocks composite
closure.
