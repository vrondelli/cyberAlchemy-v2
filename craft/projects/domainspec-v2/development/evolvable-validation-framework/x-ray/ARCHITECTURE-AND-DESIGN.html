DomainSpec v2 · architecture x-ray · seed

Can one authored relationship cause a real test?

That is the architecture’s promise. A Rule --enforces--> Operation edge should travel through a graph, become an obligation and a runnable test, bind to an implementation, receive an independent verdict, and leave a replayable receipt.

Why it matters: if any stage invents meaning, hides a skip, or grades itself, the graph may look composable without proving anything about a real system.

Why it matters: learner and navigation views can be generated from the same stable IDs, while source authority remains one semantic graph—not several independently authored truths.

Accepted two-proof topology: the EVF core receipt closes on its own. After the IOLM candidate substrate exists, IOLM consumes that exact handle and emits a fresh independent receipt. A missing consumer receipt blocks only the combined “real application used the proof” claim. The later BTE breadth rerun is a different receipt and cannot be replaced by the narrow one.

08 · reader terms

Reader terms

newcomer contract
Source authority
The YAML or JSON allowed to say what the domain means.
Profile admission
The named rule set deciding which kinds and edges one source may use.
Graph
The admitted, normalized nodes and edges with provenance and digests.
Obligation
A precise claim that the authored graph requires to hold.
TestCase
A runnable assertion derived from an obligation and authored formal input.
Binding
The mapping from a spec target to a real adapter and implementation.
Evaluator
A scoped judge for one claim class, not a universal solver.
Receipt
A replayable evidence record; never automatic release authority.
Typed residue
A machine-readable reason the chain stopped, such as needs_formal.
O_spec / O_gen
The independently expected obligations versus what the engine generated.
