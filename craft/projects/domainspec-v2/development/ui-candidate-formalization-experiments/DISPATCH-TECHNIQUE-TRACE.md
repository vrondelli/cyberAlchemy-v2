## Considered And Skipped

- `fanout`: skipped because the pilot's evidence dependencies are sequential.
- `dialectic`: skipped for plan execution; independent reviewers supply bounded
  disagreement at the evidence stage.
- `pareto_gate`: skipped because hard conjuncts and fixed thresholds are not a
  trade-off optimization.
- `spell_candidate`: skipped until one audited pilot proves reusable behavior.
