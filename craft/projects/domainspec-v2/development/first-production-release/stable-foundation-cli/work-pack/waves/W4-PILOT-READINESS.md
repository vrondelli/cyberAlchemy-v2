# W4 — Pilot and Candidate Readiness

Layer/slice: L3 / SFC-S5. Units: SWU-SFC-019 and SWU-SFC-020.

## Outcome

Have a non-author operator exercise the exact private candidate and a distinct
verifier join the full evidence set into a bounded owner-review handoff.

## Order and gates

`019 → 020` is sequential and requires late-bound identities in `SFC-PR-004`.
The pilot must cover green, red, diagnosis, recovery, rollback/withdrawal
guidance, and support contact without author substitution. The verifier must
resolve all 20 source and closeout receipts and reject stale, changed, missing,
or self-authorizing evidence.

The terminal verdict is only `candidate-ready-for-owner-review` or fail.
Successful closeout has no automatic successor and cannot release, publish,
deploy, or claim the full July product.
