# Constraints And Boundaries

## Execution

- One SWU per Task Session.
- One primary behavior and exact write scope per SWU.
- Pack-first, receipt-before-refresh, refresh-before-next-selection.
- The campaign may auto-select only the sole candidate produced by a passing
  refresh and satisfying `APPROVAL-RECORD.json`.
- Later task labels are not execution authority.

## Owners

Campaign approval does not replace:

- the validation owner for L0 red inputs and six catalogue freezes;
- formation, profile, node-pattern, or relationship admission owners;
- IOLM and Body War consumer-run owners;
- FPR, release, or production owners.

## Forbidden Ambient Authority

- no rewrite of historical receipts or manifests;
- no mutation outside the active SWU allowlist;
- no public Arcanum mutation or private-to-public prose transfer;
- no UI dependency or UI mutation;
- no subagents;
- no commit, push, PR, publication, or parent gitlink movement;
- no admission, release, or production promotion.

## Fallback Exploration

Use extra sources only for a named uncovered current-SWU obligation,
predecessor-receipt resolution, owner-handle resolution, or validator-command
resolution. The final report names the source, gap, and whether it changed the
result.
