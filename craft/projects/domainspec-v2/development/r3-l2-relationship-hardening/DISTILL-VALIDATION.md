## Closure And Recomposition Proof

The plan closes because each active finding maps to a finite action:

- duplicate `orchestrates` identity risk -> `signature_key`;
- prose-heavy `owns` -> `authority_effects`;
- broad `belongs-to` -> `semantic_kinds`;
- thin Saga law -> `saga_obligations`;
- deferred candidates/category laws -> residue ledger and owner route;
- implementation projection risk -> projection sync and validation.

It recomposes because all actions preserve the already-settled root authority boundary and produce evidence
for the broader R3 closeout plan.
