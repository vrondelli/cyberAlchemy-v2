## Round 1 — candidate units and split pressure

### Candidate A — Lean provider lane alone

This is too small. It could define toolchain pinning and theorem outcomes, but it would not say which proposition the proof is evidence for. It fails recomposition because a provider receipt could become detached from the FormalClaim source meaning.

### Candidate B — FormalClaim Lean binding plus provider lane

This is closer, but still too small. Without the EVF evidence boundary and category-model admission, it invites theorem-as-runtime inflation and a global category claim.

### Candidate C — one FormalClaim-to-evidence slice

The slice contains:

1. logic-neutral FormalClaim source authority;
2. native or owner-accepted translated Lean binding;
3. distinct pinned Lean theorem-provider request and receipt;
4. EVF composition with separately owned runtime-conformance evidence; and
5. explicit local category-model admission when categorical language is used.

This is the smallest unit that preserves claim meaning, proof identity, outcome semantics, and authority ceilings end to end.

Split pressure remains between the language bridge, provider lifecycle, EVF composition, runtime conformance, and category-model ownership. Those are implementation layers and owner boundaries inside one coherent Design unit, not reasons to split the source-to-evidence contract into independently acceptable designs.

## Round 2 — pressure test and recomposition

The selected unit survives five removals:

| Removed part | Result | Why it cannot be removed |
|---|---|---|
| source proposition/type identity | collapse | a proof declaration could become co-canonical meaning |
| translated correspondence receipt | collapse | a lossy FOL-to-Lean projection could be presented as exact |
| theorem-provider audit and digests | collapse | stale, unsafe, axiom-expanded, or `sorry`-bearing evidence could pass |
| runtime-conformance separation | collapse | a theorem about a model could be presented as deployed-system evidence |
| category-model admission | collapse | selected category-shaped submodels could inflate into a global DomainSpec category claim |

Recomposition succeeds because the unit plugs into the preserved ontology chain:

`ProofObligation --derived_from--> FormalClaim --formalizes--> BusinessMeaning`

The FormalClaim remains the proposition authority. A Lean binding identifies a representation of that proposition. A Lean receipt is evidence about the exact bound proposition and environment. EVF may compose that evidence with other independently typed receipts. None of these downstream artifacts rewrites source meaning or discharges its own obligation.

## Proposed smallest coherent unit

Admit a logic-neutral FormalClaim source envelope together with exactly one end-to-end Lean evidence slice: source proposition/type identity, binding path, theorem request/receipt, EVF evidence handle, runtime-conformance non-substitution, and optional explicitly admitted category-model profile.

The native path may bind a Lean/DTT proposition directly. The translated path may bind FOL or another source only through a digest-bound semantic mapping whose equivalence or loss class, assumptions, and owner acceptance are explicit. A Lean-native categorical claim does not pass through FOL merely to fit the route.

## Pre-mortem

The most likely failure is that a future implementation treats a successful Lean build as universal proof of the business claim and deployed runtime. The earliest warning is a receipt or UI that exposes only `pass/fail` without source identity, theorem outcome, audit closure, and independent runtime evidence. The Design blocks this by using provider-specific outcomes, exact digests, and explicit non-substitution fixtures.

The second likely failure is category inflation. The earliest warning is language such as “DomainSpec is a category” without declared objects, morphisms, equality, identities, composition, laws, interpretation, owner, ceiling, and counterexamples. The category admission profile and collapse tests are mandatory whenever categorical claims are made.
