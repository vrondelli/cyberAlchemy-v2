# Distill Validate Balancer Criteria

Budget: `Validate`.

The Proposer must retain the smallest coherent design unit that satisfies all four invariants together:

1. stable identity across business meaning, formal claim, proof obligation, and system evidence;
2. non-collapsing owner authority for each node, assertion, lifecycle act, validator, and projection;
3. exact current provenance through `formalizes`, `derived_from`, and the derivation-context manifest;
4. recomposable validation through owner-local checks, typed aggregation, and the twelve-class negative corpus.

A proposed split is rejected if it makes any of these facts unknowable without an unowned mapping, duplicates one canonical proposition, lets a generated view become semantic input, or permits a receipt/validator to grant authority. A proposed merge is rejected if one storage or validation owner becomes the semantic owner of another branch.

The Balancer will report:

- smallest coherent concept unit;
- required parts versus removable elaboration;
- recomposition proof;
- tension ledger;
- split/merge verdict;
- evidence and authority ceiling.
