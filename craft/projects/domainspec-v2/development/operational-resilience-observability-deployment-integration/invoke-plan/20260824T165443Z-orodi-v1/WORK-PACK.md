# Work Pack: ORODI Owner and Profile Admission

Work Pack ID: `WP-ORODI-001`

Designation: **non-executing**

## Outcome

Create the owner decisions and admitted binding artifacts needed for a future Invoke Refresh to decide whether `W-ORODI-001-R2` can become an execution candidate.

This Work Pack deliberately exposes no Task Session route and no mutation-capable SWU. It is a coordination and acceptance plan across distinct owners.

## Preserved contracts

- C01-C16 and M01-M13 remain the accepted evidence-closed denominator.
- C17-C20 and M14-M17 remain proposed until a separate owner decision.
- Domain meanings, profile mechanics, product observations, workflow telemetry, evaluator receipts, protected effects, admission, promotion, and release remain separate.
- The observer is append-only and non-actuating.
- Simulation cannot satisfy actual-deployment control C12.

## Planning waves

### Wave 0 — target and owner decisions

Produce `ORODI-OWNER-BINDING-PACKET-001` with exact subject, environment, stable/candidate artifact classes, topology/configuration ownership, and accountable/contributing/validator/evidence/protected-effect owners. Each owner accepts only their fields.

Gate: every role is named and accepts its scope; unresolved or collapsed ownership blocks all later waves.

### Wave 1 — semantic, ontology, profile and tool admission

Through the relevant lifecycle owners, decide:

- local DomainSpec ontology concepts and generated-projection rules;
- directional provenance-bound CAV2 crosswalks;
- monitoring/observability, load, infrastructure plan, canary analysis, deployment observation and rollback profiles;
- exact tool/runtime/version/configuration bindings for admitted profiles;
- reuse/adapt/supersede/historical dispositions for legacy v1 elements.

Gate: profile admission receipts bind exact source and generated artifacts. No private DomainSpec prose enters public Arcanum.

### Wave 2 — policy, evaluator and receipt-schema decisions

Bind privacy/cardinality rules, health semantics, SLI queries, SLO/alert policies, workload parameters, safety/abort thresholds, infrastructure policy, canary policy, deployment/rollback receipt schemas, evaluators, expiry, invalidation keys, and supersession. Decide whether C17-C20/M14-M17 enter a new closure.

Gate: every numerical or policy field has one owner, evaluator, schema, receipt, expiry and hostile witness.

### Wave 3 — immutable witness binding and dry validation

Assemble an exact `W-ORODI-001-R2` binding manifest. Validate source resolution, owner/profile admission receipts, claim/control graph, fail-closed states, privacy/cardinality, compatibility, plan facts, simulation/live separation, rollback inventory, and freshness. This wave must not run a live workload or protected effect.

Gate: all binding validators pass against exact bytes, or emit typed blockers. A dry validation cannot establish runtime behavior.

### Wave 4 — Invoke Refresh

Run `invoke:refresh` against the exact accepted L0-L3 package. Refresh may author a new execution-candidate Plan only if every live prerequisite is resolved and protected owners explicitly allow planning. Otherwise it refreshes blockers and remains non-executing.

## Acceptance conditions

- Exact owner-binding packet exists and has distinct approvals.
- Admitted profile/tool and ontology/crosswalk receipts exist with source/generation parity.
- Policy/evaluator/receipt bindings are complete and non-vacuous.
- Proposed controls have an explicit admit/defer decision.
- Witness binding is digest-current and validates fail-closed.
- No current artifact claims implementation, live validation, deployment, release, or readiness.

## Stop conditions

Stop on missing owner, ambiguous environment, ambient-latest dependency, unadmitted profile, private/public leak, absent threshold/evaluator/expiry, incomplete rollback inventory, unknown destructive plan fact, missing controller/metrics, stale receipt, or any attempt to expose Task Session before Refresh.

## Next route

First: the decision owner for `ORODI-OWNER-BINDING-PACKET-001`.

Then: lifecycle-owned profile/ontology admissions and policy decisions.

Finally: `invoke:refresh`. There is no current Task Session route.

## Claim ceiling

This Work Pack is a non-executing owner/admission coordination plan. It does not authorize or claim implementation, live tests, infrastructure plan/apply, canary, deployment, rollback, ontology/profile admission, promotion, release, or production readiness.
