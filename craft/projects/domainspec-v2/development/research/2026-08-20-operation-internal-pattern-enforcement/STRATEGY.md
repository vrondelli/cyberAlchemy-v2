auditor-accepted 2026-07-30 result already owns the baseline distinction:
semantic bindings and direct Rule-leaf use edges may be portable, while file,

an accepted premise. It must survive semantic equivalence, asymptotic cost,
allocation, ordering, short-circuit, side-effect, error, concurrency,
streaming, readability, and cross-language checks.

## Blind spots that must be tested

- Query and Operation may not currently encode a typed effect distinction.
- “Database logic” can mean query construction, transaction ownership,
  repository adaptation, consistency policy, or domain decision; these must not
  be collapsed.
- Cyclomatic and cognitive complexity are proxies, not semantic correctness.
- Multiple loops may be asymptotically equivalent, intentionally staged,
  streaming-safe, allocation-reducing, or clearer.
- A reduce/fold may hide mutation, block early exit, alter ordering or error
  behavior, allocate intermediate structures, or be non-idiomatic.
- Static tools may not resolve dynamic imports, reflection, macros, generated
  code, monkey patching, feature flags, SQL builders, or ORM behavior.
- Cross-language normalization may erase real TypeScript, Python, and Rust
  differences.
- A passing diagnostic receipt does not confer authority or runtime proof.
