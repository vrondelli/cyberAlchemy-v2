# Local Research Glossary

Status: `local-only-not-promoted`

These entries explain this tower. They are not canonical Arcanum or DomainSpec
definitions and require a separate definitions-governance decision before reuse
outside this research folder.

| Term | Source kind | Local meaning | Arcanum reading | Promotion status | Misuse warning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| software factory | `operator-reading` | An intent-to-evidence production system whose work, execution, evidence, acceptance, and promotion remain distinguishable. | A composition of owned capabilities, not one mandatory product or controller. | local-only | Do not call every coding agent a factory. |
| coding assistance | `market-source` | Interactive completion, edit, chat, or bounded-task help. | Possible input aid, not governed execution. | local-only | Assistance adoption is not autonomous-factory adoption. |
| human-supervised repository agent | `primary-source` + `local-inference` | A task/session that produces a reviewable repository change while protected actions and merge remain supervised. | Execution precedent below semantic and promotion owners. | local-only | “Autonomous” overstates the defensible operating model. |
| Kiro-documented specification workflow precedent | `primary-source` | Requirements, design, tasks, dependency waves, and implementation progression documented by Kiro. | A possible projection precedent. | local-only | A vendor spec is not a Work Pack and no lossless mapping ran. |
| vendor-managed agent orchestration control plane | `primary-source` + `local-inference` | Provider-managed fleets of sessions, repositories, policies, environments, and telemetry. | Adapter precedent, not DomainSpec authority. | local-only | No shared cross-provider factory contract is established. |
| programmable agent-runtime harness | `primary-source` + `local-inference` | A replaceable agent execution primitive or SDK. | Candidate execution substrate under Arcanum ownership. | local-only | A harness is not a turnkey factory or semantic owner. |
| typed DomainSpec kernel | `local-current-state` | The bounded typed graph, edge validation, realization, and same-host replay evidence already owned in v2. | The semantic/intention side of the composition. | existing owner; no new promotion | Do not expand bounded proof into cross-host or production readiness. |
| field-preserving runtime adapter | `operator-reading` | A projection that either preserves every required Task Session/handoff field or fails closed. | Arcanum-owned experiment route. | future experiment | Omission, rewriting, inference, and silent defaults are failure. |
| exported runtime lifecycle evidence | `primary-source` + `operator-reading` | Provider events, analytics, audit logs, task states, or structured outputs considered as distinct inputs. | Possible observability inputs. | future experiment | None is a governed receipt until an exact field mapping runs. |
| recovery equivalence | `operator-reading` | Equality of resumed outcome across bytes, lineage, authority, and residue. | A falsifiable execution/continuation experiment. | future work | Checkpoint, retry, resume, archive, cancel, and takeover are not synonyms. |
| product facade | `local-inference` | A later private, profile-bounded surface exposing existing kernels and receipts unchanged. | Non-authoritative composition/UI. | planned and blocked | It must not own semantics, workflow, evidence, freshness, approval, or promotion. |
| integrated autonomous v2 factory | `operator-reading` | A single v2 controller absorbing scheduling, runtime, evidence, continuation, and authority responsibilities. | Rejected architecture. | `BLOCK` / `KILL` | Vendor precedent does not supply a local owner or end-to-end witness. |
| governed receipt | `local-current-state` | Owned evidence binding inputs, execution, validation, and unresolved state under a declared schema. | Separate evidence artifact. | existing owner | Runtime success, analytics, tests, and PR state are not automatic substitutes. |
| promotion | `local-current-state` | A separately authorized change in canonical or release status. | Outside this research tower. | not authorized | Research recommendation and audit acceptance do not promote anything. |
