## Source-Kind Rules

- `primary-source`: stated by a product or project owner.
- `market-source`: stated by a named survey, dataset, or analyst source.
- `local-current-state`: evidenced by current repository bytes.
- `local-inference`: inferred from source-backed material.
- `analogy`: useful mapping, not source or schema equivalence.
- `operator-reading`: practical design interpretation.
- `open-residue`: unresolved question or future task.
