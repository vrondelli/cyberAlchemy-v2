# Tower Model

Status: `closed-local-research`

Authority effect: `none`

## Working Hypothesis

A DomainSpec v2 software factory should be an intent-to-evidence production
system: it turns typed intent into a bounded build graph, projects admitted work
into replaceable execution substrates, and returns evidence without allowing
runtime success to impersonate semantic correctness or authority.

This is a hypothesis to test, not the answer the research must preserve.

## Competing Shapes

| Branch | Shape | Main attraction | Main failure risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | v2 owns an integrated agent runner and factory control plane | single product surface | collapses DomainSpec intent ownership into execution mechanics and duplicates Arcanum/runtime owners |
| B | v2 is the typed contract/compiler and evidence evaluator above replaceable runtimes | portable semantic core | may remain too abstract or operationally incomplete for users |
| C | a product facade composes v2, Arcanum, and one or more execution adapters | end-to-end factory experience with separated owners | integration contracts and user-visible state may fragment |
| D | v2 remains specification-only | smallest scope | fails the user's desired intent-to-working-system outcome |

The result must compare all four branches. It may recommend a hybrid only if it
names every retained owner, interface, and evidence boundary.

## Audited Outcome and Branch Normalization

The initial table above is preserved as hypothesis evidence. During synthesis,
the final report normalized the letters around the retained components:

| Initial hypothesis | Final findings label | Audited disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A — integrated v2 runner/control plane | D — integrated autonomous v2 factory | `BLOCK`; `KILL-no-witness`; `KILL-collapses-split-owners` |
| B — typed contract/compiler above runtimes | A — typed kernel plus B — runtime projection | recommended owner-preserving composition |
| C — composing product facade | C — private Validation Toolkit facade | later; planned and blocked |
| D — specification-only | no retained final branch | not selected because it fails the requested intent-to-working-system outcome |

The recommended target is therefore the typed DomainSpec kernel plus a
replaceable Arcanum-owned execution projection, with a later non-authoritative
private facade. This is a research conclusion at the stated evidence ceiling,
not an implementation or promotion decision.

## Levels

### L0 — corpus

Freeze a representative market cohort, independent market signals, current
DomainSpec v2 evidence, and the prior Factory.ai handoff/review. Record dates,
status labels, source kinds, exclusions, and open-world limits.

### L1 — residue and tensions

Test category boundaries, market maturity, buyer problems, runtime architecture,
product workflow, governance, evidence, and ownership. Preserve disagreement
between external product reality, execution architecture, and internal v2 fit.

### L2 — closure

Produce an exact-bound market map, capability matrix, v2 owner map, reuse
portfolio, competing target operating models, and smallest follow-up experiments.
Close through independent precedent, non-vacuity, and definitional-soundness
reviews plus a final audit.

## Claim Classes

Every load-bearing statement must be labeled as one of:

- `primary-source` — stated by the product or project owner;
- `market-source` — stated by a named survey, dataset, or analyst source;
- `local-current-state` — supported by current repository bytes;
- `local-inference` — inferred from source-backed local material;
- `analogy` — a useful comparison, not equivalence;
- `operator-reading` — a design interpretation for DomainSpec v2;
- `open-residue` — unresolved or awaiting evidence.

## Required Final Views

1. Market segmentation and maturity snapshot.
2. Product capability and operating-model matrix.
3. Table-stakes, differentiator, and unproven-claim map.
4. DomainSpec v2 current-owner and current-evidence map.
5. Reuse matrix with `borrow-carefully`, `adapter`, `build-from-owned`,
   `analogy-only`, `future-work`, or `block` verdicts.
6. Branch A/B/C/D comparison and a bounded recommendation.
7. Smallest experiments and evidence contracts for unresolved candidates.
8. Claim ceiling and owner handoff.
