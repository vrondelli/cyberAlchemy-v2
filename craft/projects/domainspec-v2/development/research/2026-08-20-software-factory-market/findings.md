# Goal

Establish the market state of agentic software-development and software-factory products as of **2026-08-20**, then determine what DomainSpec v2 can reuse without collapsing typed intent, execution, evidence, continuation, acceptance, and promotion into one controller.

# TL;DR

The market is commercially active but epistemically immature. **[independently observed]** AI assistance is mainstream in the cited developer samples; **[documented]** human-supervised repository agents, isolated work units, delegation, lifecycle events, recovery controls, and enterprise policy surfaces are now common product mechanics. But **[unknown]** there is no independently established market-wide rate of autonomous-factory adoption, and no evidence here that any vendor mechanism is semantically equivalent to a DomainSpec Work Pack, governed receipt, or promotion decision.

DomainSpec v2 should **reuse its current typed kernel**, **experiment with complete-field-preserving Arcanum runtime adapters**, and **repair owner-bound drift invalidation**. It should defer parallel execution, recovery equivalence, exported runtime lifecycle evidence, Kiro projection, planned conformance/formal mechanisms, and the private Validation Toolkit CLI until their named witnesses and owner gates exist. It should **block Branch D, an integrated autonomous v2 factory**: vendor precedent exists, but the proposal has no local end-to-end witness and collapses intentionally split owners.

# Context

The investigation was needed because “software factory” spans unlike products and proof claims. A coding assistant, a repository agent, a vendor-managed orchestration plane, an app builder, and a programmable runtime harness may share visible mechanics while owning different work units and guarantees. The prior Factory handoff also remained under a `FIX` review: aggregate analytics had been overstated as receipt evidence, runtime projection did not guarantee complete field preservation, and current authority/status wording was too strong. Three explorers therefore separated market evidence, official product mechanics, and current DomainSpec v2 owner evidence; a synthesizer reconciled them; precedent, non-vacuity, and definitional reviewers attacked the result; and one feedback round closed selected provider selectors. ## Table stakes, differentiators, and claims to refuse

**[inferred] Table stakes:** reviewable diffs and human merge/approval; sandbox/worktree isolation; repository-scoped instructions/extensibility; visible tests and failures; interrupt/takeover/retry; repository and issue/PR integrations; usage/budget visibility; enterprise identity/audit/privacy/model/repository/network controls; precise preview/beta/current-limit labels.

**[proposed] DomainSpec-relevant differentiators:** typed acceptance-bound intent preserved through execution; explicit separation of execution, tests, semantic conformance, owner acceptance, and promotion; replaceable adapters with complete field preservation; falsifiable dependency/join/cancellation behavior; exact drift invalidation; reproducible realization; buyer-visible recovery lineage instead of activity-only telemetry.

**[blocked/unproven] Reject as claims:** broad AI adoption proves factory adoption; more parallel agents improve delivery; customer quotations establish representative outcomes; a successful session, green test, applied task, merged PR, analytic metric, or structured output is a governed receipt or proves semantic conformance; vendor usage implies market share; app-builder polish implies enterprise controls; “software factory” names a stable shared contract.

## The two Factory handoff repairs carried forward

1. **Telemetry versus receipt fields.** **[documented]** Factory exposes typed runtime events, JSON/JSON-RPC/structured output, analytics, OpenTelemetry, and audit attribution. **[proposed]** DomainSpec/Arcanum receipts require an exact mandatory-field inventory. The former does not prove the latter. Every adapter experiment must map each event, analytic, audit record, task state, and structured output separately, then reject any missing mandatory field. Aggregate analytics must never be described as per-work-pack evidence or per-Mission exportability without a witnessed mapping.
2. **Complete runtime-envelope preservation.** A runtime adapter passes only when every Task Session/handoff field is preserved losslessly, including fields a provider cannot represent. Unsupported fields must cause fail-closed rejection—never omission, rewriting, inference, or silent defaults. Current wording must remain exact: transient-output bytes are unaccepted; nine App Server traces are specified but unexecuted; `cyberAlchemy-v2` authority material is candidate/staging where recorded; the private CLI is planned and blocked; no integrated factory is delivered.
