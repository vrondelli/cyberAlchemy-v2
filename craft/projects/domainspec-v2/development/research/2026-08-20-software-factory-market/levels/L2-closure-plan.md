## Required Findings Structure

- direct answer and claim ceiling;
- market shape and maturity, with denominators and caveats;
- representative product matrix with exact source bindings;
- buyer problems and competitive convergence;
- current DomainSpec v2 factory-relevant capability map;
- Branch A/B/C/D target-operating-model comparison;
- reuse portfolio and owner map;
- candidate sequence: now / next / later / reject;
- smallest experiment per unresolved high-value candidate;
- blind spots, volatility, and open residue;
- required verdict matrix: candidate, owner, witnessed, sound, verdict.

## Reuse Decision Test

A pattern may be recommended only if the report identifies:

1. the user or operator problem it solves;
2. at least one exact observed market implementation;
3. the current local owner and current evidence state;
4. what is genuinely reusable rather than merely analogous;
5. the required interface and protected authority boundary;
6. the smallest positive and negative witness;
7. implementation and adoption risks;
8. a verdict of `borrow-carefully`, `adapter`, `build-from-owned`,
   `analogy-only`, `future-work`, or `block`.

## Empirical Handoff

If a promising claim requires measured execution, live vendor access, repository
upload, credentials, paid usage, or comparative benchmarking, the tower stops and
hands that question to `research-evidence-harness` or a separately authorized
experiment. This research does not silently convert documentary evidence into a
runtime proof.

## Done Definition

The tower is done only when all agents are joined and closed, every load-bearing
claim is source-bound or explicitly open, the writer's artifact passes the final
audit, the reuse matrix preserves owner boundaries, and remaining implementation
or experiment questions have named next routes. Research closure alone authorizes
no v2 mutation.
