# L1 — Residue and Research Lanes

Status: `reconciled-with-future-work`

## Central Tension

The market increasingly bundles planning, coding, execution, review, security,
and administration into one product. DomainSpec v2 deliberately separates typed
intent, execution method, evidence, and authority. The research must determine
how to deliver a factory experience without erasing the distinctions that make
the local system defensible.

## Lane M — Market Reality

- What product strata are real and durable on the cutoff date?
- Where is adoption broad, and where is it still supervised experimentation?
- Which buying criteria now dominate: model quality, workflow coverage, security,
  governance, integration, price, support, or measurable delivery outcomes?
- Which claims have independent evidence and which are vendor self-report?

## Lane X — Factory Mechanics

- What is the operational work unit: prompt, issue, Mission, spec, task graph,
  session, pull request, app, or recurring automation?
- How do products isolate work, preserve context, delegate, validate, recover,
  expose progress, and hand control back to humans?
- Which interfaces are portable patterns and which are vendor-specific mechanics?

## Lane V — DomainSpec v2 Fit

- Which factory functions are already evidenced by v2, Arcanum, Craft, or an
  adjacent owner?
- Which are planned or candidate-local but not implemented or authorized?
- Does the reusable pattern belong in v2's typed contract/evidence core, in an
  Arcanum execution adapter, in a product facade, or nowhere?
- What observable outcome would prove that each reuse candidate earns its cost?

## Category-Error Risks

1. Calling any coding agent a software factory.
2. Treating a vendor's work unit as schema-equivalent to a Work Pack or SWU.
3. Treating operational controls as semantic or promotion authority.
4. Treating a polished UI as proof of a reliable execution contract.
5. Treating generated tests or green CI as evidence of correct business intent.
6. Treating a local research candidate as a delivered v2 capability.
7. Rebuilding Arcanum runtime or existing v2 factory machinery under new names.
8. Assuming market popularity implies strategic fit or defensibility.

## Pre-Execution Blockers

- the research dispatch has not received explicit human confirmation;
- the external source cohort is candidate-only and not yet frozen by explorers;
- the v2 worktree is concurrently dirty, so current-state citations need digest
  binding and must not be silently refreshed mid-synthesis;
- the prior Factory.ai handoff carries a `FIX` review and cannot be adopted as a
  clean baseline without those findings being accounted for.
