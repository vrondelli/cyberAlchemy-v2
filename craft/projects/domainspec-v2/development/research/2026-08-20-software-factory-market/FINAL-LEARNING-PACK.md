## Plain-Language Answer

The market is real, busy, and still immature. AI help is normal in the cited
developer samples, and products now routinely document repository agents,
isolated environments, delegation, background work, recovery controls,
lifecycle events, and enterprise administration. The hard part is no longer
only “can an agent write code?” It is “can a team control, inspect, recover, and
trust the work without confusing activity with correctness?”

DomainSpec v2 should not copy a vendor software factory wholesale. Its reusable
advantage is the typed intent and validation kernel it already owns. The practical
factory shape is to keep that kernel, project admitted work through
field-preserving Arcanum runtime adapters, keep receipts and authority decisions
separate, and add the planned private Validation Toolkit facade only after its
existing gates close.

The all-in-one alternative is rejected. Factory and Devin show that managed
orchestration products exist, but there is no local end-to-end witness for a
single autonomous v2 controller, and such a controller would duplicate or absorb
owners that are intentionally separate.

## Market Reading

| Question | Bounded answer |
| --- | --- |
| Is AI-assisted development established? | Yes, within the cited survey samples; this is broader than autonomous factories. |
| Are repository agents commercial? | Yes, with human supervision and review as the defensible norm. |
| Are software-factory control planes a stable shared category? | No. Vendor-managed orchestration exists, but contracts and work units differ. |
| Are parallelism and recovery solved generically? | No. Providers expose different joins, cancellation, checkpoints, archives, interrupts, and takeover mechanics. |
| Do lifecycle events prove delivery or correctness? | No. They are useful inputs, not automatic receipts, conformance, acceptance, or promotion. |
| Is there evidence for market-wide autonomous-factory adoption or causal improvement? | Not in this corpus. |

## Reuse Decisions

### Reuse now through existing owners

- typed graph and edge-law validation;
- bounded realization and same-host reproducibility evidence;
- governance separation between execution, receipts, acceptance, and promotion;
- exact drift defect witnesses as the basis for a bounded repair experiment.

### Run bounded experiments next

- complete-field-preserving runtime substitution, including unsupported-field
  rejection;
- provider lifecycle class to governed receipt-field mapping;
- deterministic parallel admission, collision, cancellation, join, and residue;
- recovery equivalence across bytes, lineage, authority, and residue;
- exact drift invalidation and non-dependent preservation;
- cross-host reproducibility and language-profile fixtures.

### Defer

- Kiro requirements/design/tasks projection until a lossless round trip exists;
- exported runtime lifecycle evidence until an exact mapping passes;
- the private Validation Toolkit CLI until profile, denominator, fused-unit,
  selection, and validation gates close;
- planned conformance and formal mechanisms until fixtures, solver runs, and
  implementation traces exist.

### Reject

- a second controller, scheduler, work graph, continuation ledger, evidence
  ledger, approval owner, freshness truth, or promotion owner inside v2;
- any claim that a successful session, green test, applied task, merged pull
  request, analytic metric, structured output, or bounded model proves semantic
  conformance or promotion;
- hosted, public, autonomous, deployed, released, or production-stable
  DomainSpec-factory claims from this evidence.

## Open Residue and Next Route

Documentary research is complete, but empirical evidence is not. The next route
for runtime-envelope, lifecycle-field, parallelism, recovery, drift, or
cross-host claims is `research-evidence-harness`, scoped to one positive and one
forced-negative fixture under the named existing owners. Vendor access,
credentials, paid use, repository transport, selection, implementation,
procurement, publication, deployment, and promotion require separate explicit
authorization.

The remaining source gaps are listed in [open-residue.md](./residue/open-residue.md).
Provider architecture remains `partial-selector-closure`; no vendor product was
executed and volatile facts must be rebound before procurement use.
