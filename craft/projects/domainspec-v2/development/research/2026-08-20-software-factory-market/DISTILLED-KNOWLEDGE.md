# Distilled Knowledge

Status: `audited-local-research`

## One-Sentence Model

DomainSpec v2 should remain the typed semantic kernel of a software-factory
experience, project admitted work through replaceable Arcanum-owned runtimes,
consume runtime evidence without confusing it with truth or authority, and add a
private facade only after its existing blockers close.

## Compositional Spine

```text
typed DomainSpec intent
  -> owned validation and work graph
  -> field-preserving Arcanum execution adapter
  -> distinct runtime events / tests / audit / structured outputs
  -> governed receipts and semantic checks
  -> separate continuation, acceptance, and promotion owners
  -> later private product facade
```

## What the Market Supports

- AI assistance is mainstream within the cited samples.
- Human-supervised repository agents and isolated work units are commercial.
- Delegation, lifecycle streams, recovery controls, enterprise policy, and
  multi-surface operation are common documented mechanics, but their contracts
  are non-equivalent.
- Independent trust and supervision evidence is materially weaker than adoption
  evidence.
- No evidence in this tower establishes market-wide autonomous-factory adoption,
  causal delivery improvement, or a shared software-factory standard.

## What to Borrow

- explicit plan/admission before mutation;
- isolated work units and bounded permissions;
- interrupt/resume/takeover with attribution;
- lifecycle event streams and structured outputs as distinct evidence inputs;
- parent/child identity, dependency, cancellation, and join concepts;
- enterprise policy separated from semantic authority.

Each item remains either an existing owned capability or a bounded experiment,
not blanket product adoption.

## What to Keep Analogy-Only

- vendor Missions, sessions, threads, tasks, specs, checkpoints, and pull
  requests as substitutes for Work Packs or receipts;
- provider “completion” as semantic conformance;
- app-builder polish as reliability or governance proof;
- market usage as strategic fit.

## What to Block

- an integrated autonomous controller inside v2;
- duplicate schedulers, work graphs, continuation ledgers, evidence ledgers,
  approval owners, freshness truths, or promotion owners;
- present-tense conformance/formal-proof claims from plans or bounded models;
- deployment, release, procurement, publication, or production claims from this
  documentary research.

## Honest Cutoff

The evidence cutoff is 2026-08-20. Provider architecture is
`partial-selector-closure`; the product cohort and source freeze are not
complete, no vendor was executed, and volatile product facts require rebinding.
