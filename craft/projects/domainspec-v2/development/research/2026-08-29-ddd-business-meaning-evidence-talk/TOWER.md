# Research Tower Contract

## Target

Produce a defensible CFP approach and a reusable learning pack for a DDD Brasil
talk about the path from business meaning to bounded formal and executable
evidence.

## Claim Under Study

A typed evidence loop can help a DDD team preserve and challenge selected domain
meaning across model, implementation, and verification boundaries.

This is a method hypothesis supported by a narrow internal experiment. It is not
yet an adoption result, production result, or validated product outcome.

## Levels

| Level | Question | Exit condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L0 | What do DDD, related research, the CFP, and the repository actually say? | Source corpus records provenance and claim limits. |
| L1 | Where does the proposed story align, diverge, or overclaim? | Claim map separates observed evidence, inference, analogy, and residue. |
| L2 | What talk approach best fits this audience and evidence? | One recommended thesis, arc, abstract seed, and public-boundary plan. |

## Research Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Parent researcher | Source selection, repository evidence, synthesis, and final claim ceiling. |
| Independent CFP-fit reviewer | Read-only challenge of DDD fit, audience fit, overclaims, and public/private risk. |

## Source-Kind Rules

- `primary-source`: author, project, paper, or event source that directly states
  the claim.
- `related-source`: adjacent work used for comparison, not as authority over this
  repository.
- `local-inference`: conclusion derived from repository evidence.
- `analogy`: a similarity useful for explanation but not identity.
- `operator-reading`: a judgment about presentation or audience fit.
- `open-residue`: a question not closed by available evidence.

## Authority Ceiling

The tower may recommend a submission direction. It does not submit a CFP, approve
public disclosure, promote vocabulary, validate the CyberAlchemy product thesis,
or extend the current EVF evidence ceiling.
