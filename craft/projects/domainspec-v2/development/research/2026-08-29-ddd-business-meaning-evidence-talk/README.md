# DDD Business Meaning To Evidence Talk Research

Status: research candidate
Visibility: private
Authority effect: none
Research date: 2026-08-29

## Research Question

How should a 30–40 minute DDD Brasil submission present the CyberAlchemy product
thesis and the DomainSpec v2 EVF experiment without becoming a product pitch or
claiming more formal assurance than the evidence supports?

## Current Answer

Lead with a familiar DDD problem: business meaning is explored in a shared
language, but its path into code, tests, and later decisions becomes difficult to
inspect. Present one experiment that keeps a domain rule connected to a typed
model, a formal obligation, an implementation binding, positive and negative
checks, and a bounded evidence receipt.

CyberAlchemy is the product context for making that loop understandable and
resumable. DomainSpec v2 owns the typed meaning. EVF owns the evidence chain.
Neither the product thesis nor the current EVF evidence is a production or market
validation claim.

## Recommended Talk Position

**Da linguagem ubíqua à evidência executável**
*Um experimento para transformar regras de negócio em obrigações verificáveis sem perder o domínio*

The talk is about formal evidence as feedback for domain modeling. It is not a
general claim that DDD should become formal methods, and it is not a claim that a
formal proof establishes that business experts meant the right thing.
