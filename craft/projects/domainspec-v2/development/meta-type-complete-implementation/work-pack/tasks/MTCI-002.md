Owner route: `definitions-governance`. Status: ineligible until MTCI-001 passes.

## Outcome

Produce discriminating structural and, where necessary, exercised relationship evidence; obtain an explicit human-owner decision: admit, reject, revise, or defer.

## Inputs

MTCI-001 packet, canonical DS-D1 and DS-D8 sources, current formation fixtures, and named nearest rivals.

## Write scope

The instance review folder plus only definitions-governance review/decision surfaces selected by that skill. No active registry/card/schema mutation occurs in this SWU.

## Required evidence

- positive formation witness and at least two one-cause negatives;
- pairwise non-collapse table against the nearest rivals;
- proof that declared relationship pressure resolves through active signatures, or an owner-reviewed relationship delta;
- stable-ID/tombstone decision;
- authored-candidate versus independent-owner separation;
- signed/recorded owner verdict.

## Acceptance

Only `admit` with a complete independent decision receipt opens MTCI-003. `revise` returns to MTCI-001; `reject` and `defer` close without canonical mutation. A candidate cannot issue its own admission receipt.
