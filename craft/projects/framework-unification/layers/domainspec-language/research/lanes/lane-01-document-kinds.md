# Lane 01 — Document Kinds and Canonical Top-Level Field Model

## Summary

The DomainSpec evidence already separates three semantic layers cleanly: a
**contract** layer (obligations, methods, invariants, authority, lifecycle, gates —
seen in `CRAFT-INTERFACE.md`, `CRAFT-INTERACTION-CONTRACT.md`, and `handoff.md`), a
**schema** layer (fields, types, enums, refs, required keys, validation rules — seen
in `dispatch.schema.yml`, `CRAFT-LEDGER-SCHEMA.yml`, and the two Craft ledger
schemas), and an **iteration/change** layer (versioned status, supersession, residue,
decisions, validation deltas — visible across `status`/`schema_version`/`superseded`
fields and the recompose/apply_receipt flows). I therefore propose **five minimal
document kinds** — `contract`, `schema`, `iteration`, `example`, and `use_case` —
plus one borderline kind (`handoff`) that is best modeled as a contract subtype, and
two candidates (`decision`, `validation`) that the evidence shows are **records inside
iteration / instances of schema**, not separate top-level kinds. Every DomainSpec
document shares a small canonical envelope of ~9 fields (`domainspec`, `id`, `kind`,
`version`, `status`, `title`, `purpose`, `authority`, `references`), each of which is
demonstrated by at least two evidence sources below.

## 1. Proposed Minimal Document Kinds

### Recommended kinds (5)

| Kind        | What it carries                                                                                                                                                                                                | Primary evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Justification                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `contract`  | Semantic agreement: purpose, authority, ownership, invariants, methods/operations, lifecycle, gates, boundaries, evidence requirements, downstream obligations.                                                | `CRAFT-INTERFACE.md` (Purpose, Method Contract, Invariants, Validation Boundary); `CRAFT-INTERACTION-CONTRACT.md` (Capability Contracts, Boundary, Closure Rule); `handoff.md` (Purpose, Output Contract, Mode Gates)                                                                                     | The handoff doc requires it not be collapsed into schema. All three contract docs express obligations/invariants/boundaries that no schema field captures (e.g. "raw blockers cannot be resolved directly" in `CRAFT-INTERFACE.md`; "Craft records the native verdict but does not rewrite it" in `CRAFT-INTERACTION-CONTRACT.md`). |
| `schema`    | Structural shape: `row_families`/`properties`, `type`, `enums`, `required`/`required_fields`, `optional_fields`, `pattern`, references, `validation_rules`, `extends`.                                         | `dispatch.schema.yml` (JSON-Schema `properties`/`required`/`enum`/`$defs`); `CRAFT-LEDGER-SCHEMA.yml` (`enums`, `row_families`, `validation_rules`); `CRAFT-INTERFACE-LEDGER-SCHEMA.yml`; `CRAFT-INTERACTION-LEDGER-SCHEMA.yml`                                                                           | Two independent schema dialects already exist (JSON-Schema vs. Craft `row_families`). The language must be able to express both as one kind with a machine-checkable shape; this is the validation-authority surface.                                                                                                               |
| `iteration` | Governed change record: changed artifact, reason, prev/next state, changed obligations/fields, examples added, validations run, decisions opened/closed, blockers created/resolved, residue, promotion status. | `DOMAINSPEC-LANGUAGE-HANDOFF.md` "Iteration Layer"; `CRAFT-INTERACTION-CONTRACT.md` `apply_receipt`/`recompose`/`open_residue`; `CRAFT-INTERACTION-LEDGER-SCHEMA.yml` `route_events` + `route_event_type` enum; schema `status`/`schema_version`/`superseded` fields across all `.yml` files              | Change is explicitly called out as a needed layer in the handoff. The interaction ledger's ordered `route_events` (classify→handoff→receipt→apply→residue→recompose) is a concrete iteration-record shape; `superseded` enum members in every schema prove supersession is first-class.                                             |
| `example`   | Instance/fixture binding that validates against a `schema` and is referenced as authoritative test evidence.                                                                                                   | `CONTEXT-PACK.md` (CRAFT-INTERFACE-EXAMPLE.yml / CRAFT-INTERACTION-EXAMPLE.yml as fixtures); `CRAFT-INTERFACE-VALIDATION.md` "Example Review" table citing `CTX-CRAFT-TEST`, `BLK-STORAGE-SOURCE-001`, etc.; `handoff.md` "Output Shape" Invoke Validation Fixture Result                                 | Examples are used as validation fixtures with their own pass/flag/block verdicts, not as prose. The handoff lists "these examples are fixtures" and "these validations are authoritative" as distinct language statements. Keeping `example` a kind lets a fixture declare which `schema` it binds and which `contract` it proves.  |
| `use_case`  | Concrete binding from a generic `contract`/`schema` to a project, sigil, route, or workflow (with vocabulary boundaries and which obligations it must satisfy).                                                | `CONTEXT-PACK.md` "Derived Language Requirements" defines `use_case`; `DOMAINSPEC-LANGUAGE-HANDOFF.md` "Required Use Cases" (Craft, dispatch-spec, invoke, task-session, refine, decision-gate); `CRAFT-INTERACTION-CONTRACT.md` Capability Contracts (refine/invoke/task-session/dispatch-spec bindings) | The handoff's central non-goal is "do not make Craft canonical." A `use_case` kind is the mechanism that keeps the core language generic while letting Craft, dispatch, and invoke each bind it without renaming core fields.                                                                                                       |

### Borderline / merge recommendation

| Kind      | Recommendation                                                                                                      | Evidence basis                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `handoff` | **Merge into `contract` as a subtype** (`kind: contract`, `contract_type: handoff`), not a separate top-level kind. | `handoff.md` is structurally a contract: it has Purpose, Activation Gate, Inputs, Output Contract, Mode Gates, Boundaries ("must not mutate the target sigil"). It already overlaps `route_handoffs` in `CRAFT-INTERACTION-LEDGER-SCHEMA.yml` (a _schema_ row family) and the dispatch `boundary` kind `capability_handoff` in `dispatch.schema.yml`. A handoff is one obligation-bearing contract whose instances are validated by a schema, so it does not need a distinct top-level kind — a `contract_type`/profile discriminator suffices. |

### Candidates the evidence shows should NOT be separate kinds

| Candidate         | Where it actually lives                                                                                                    | Evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `decision`        | A **row family inside a schema** and a **record inside `iteration`**, not a document kind.                                 | `decisions` row family in `CRAFT-LEDGER-SCHEMA.yml`; `open_decision`/`decide` methods in `CRAFT-INTERFACE.md`; `decision` appears as a step input/output `kind` and a gate `kind` in `dispatch.schema.yml`. Decisions are sub-records governed by contracts/schemas, never standalone top-level documents.                                                                                    |
| `validation`      | An **output of a schema's `validation_rules`** plus a **report referenced by `iteration`/`example`**, not a distinct kind. | `validation_rules` blocks in all schema `.yml` files; `CRAFT-INTERFACE-VALIDATION.md` is a _report about_ a contract+schema+example, with verdict `pass`. Modeling it as a top-level kind would duplicate `example` (fixture) and `iteration` (validations run). Recommend: validation reports are `iteration` records or `example` verdicts that point at the validated `contract`/`schema`. |
| `blocker` / `gap` | Sub-records inside contracts/iterations.                                                                                   | `typed_items` (`blocker`) and `gaps` row families in `CRAFT-LEDGER-SCHEMA.yml` / `CRAFT-INTERFACE-LEDGER-SCHEMA.yml`; `gaps and blockers` listed as Output Contract sections in `handoff.md`. First-class as fields, not as documents.                                                                                                                                                        |

**Net minimal set: `contract`, `schema`, `iteration`, `example`, `use_case`** (with `handoff` as a `contract` profile). This satisfies the handoff's required separation of contract / schema / iteration while adding only the two binding kinds (`example`, `use_case`) the evidence repeatedly relies on.

## 2. Canonical Top-Level Field Model

The small envelope every DomainSpec document carries. Each row cites at least two evidence sources demonstrating the field (sometimes under different names — the canonical name is the proposal).

| Canonical field | Meaning                                                                                                    | Evidence (existing analogues)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `domainspec`    | Language/dialect marker + spec version (the `$schema` analogue).                                           | `dispatch.schema.yml` `$schema:` + `$id:`; all Craft schemas open with `schema_id:` (e.g. `craft.interface_ledger`, `craft.recursive_ledger`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `id`            | Stable unique identity of THIS document.                                                                   | `dispatch.schema.yml` `dispatch_id` (required); `CRAFT-LEDGER-SCHEMA.yml` `schema_id`; `handoff.md` "Canonical ID: invoke" + "identity and source session reference" required section.                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `kind`          | One of the minimal kinds (`contract`/`schema`/`iteration`/`example`/`use_case`).                           | Implicit but consistent: `title:` "Arcanum Dispatch Spec" classifies a schema in `dispatch.schema.yml`; `handoff.md` "Mode: handoff" + "Scope: library"; `CONTEXT-PACK.md` "Derived Language Requirements" enumerates the kinds.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `version`       | Semantic version of this document's content.                                                               | `CRAFT-LEDGER-SCHEMA.yml` / `CRAFT-INTERFACE-LEDGER-SCHEMA.yml` / `CRAFT-INTERACTION-LEDGER-SCHEMA.yml` all carry `schema_version: 0.1.0`; `handoff.md` "L2 companion contract".                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `status`        | Lifecycle state (draft, refined, candidate, gated, implemented, validated, promoted, superseded, blocked). | `CRAFT-INTERFACE.md`/`CRAFT-INTERACTION-CONTRACT.md` `Status: candidate-local`; `CRAFT-LEDGER-SCHEMA.yml` `status: candidate`; `handoff.md` `Status: implemented (...)`; lifecycle enum demanded by `CONTEXT-PACK.md` "lifecycle state".                                                                                                                                                            |
| `title`         | Human-readable name.                                                                                       | `dispatch.schema.yml` `title:`; `CRAFT-INTERFACE.md` H1 "Craft Interface Contract"; `handoff.md` H1.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `purpose`       | One-paragraph intent / objective.                                                                          | `CRAFT-INTERFACE.md` "## Purpose"; `CRAFT-INTERACTION-CONTRACT.md` "## Purpose"; `handoff.md` "## Purpose"; `dispatch.schema.yml` `description:` + `intent.objective`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `authority`     | Source/validation/lifecycle/promotion ownership map (who owns what).                                       | `dispatch.schema.yml` `$defs/authority_map` (`lifecycle`/`execution`/`validation`/`evidence`/`memory`/`promotion`); `CRAFT-LEDGER-SCHEMA.yml` `owner_context_id` + `source_of_truth_policy`; `CRAFT-INTERFACE-LEDGER-SCHEMA.yml` `owner_surface` + `source_of_truth_policy`; `CRAFT-INTERACTION-CONTRACT.md` "Craft owns route memory ... the called capability owns its native artifact contract". |
| `references`    | Typed links to other DomainSpec docs (source artifacts, extended schemas, contracts implemented, parent).  | `dispatch.schema.yml` `parent_dispatch_id` + step `inputs/outputs.ref`; all Craft schemas `extends:` + `source_artifacts:`; references `references`/`references_any` field type throughout `CRAFT-LEDGER-SCHEMA.yml`; `handoff.md` "source session reference" + "selected source session references".                                                                                               |

Two fields are strongly attested but proposed as **near-canonical optional** rather than required, because not every kind needs them:

| Near-canonical field | Meaning                                                       | Evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---- | ------- |
| `evidence`           | Provenance / fixture / receipt pointers backing the document. | `evidence` field appears in nearly every Craft row family (`definitions`, `gaps`, `relations`, `decisions`, `typed_items`, `route_events`); `dispatch.schema.yml` `evidence_artifact` + `boundary_evidence`; `CRAFT-INTERFACE-VALIDATION.md` "Evidence Reviewed". |
| `validation`         | Declared rules or a verdict (`pass`/`flag`/`block`).          | `validation_rules` in all schemas; `gate_status`/`receipt_status` enums `pass`/`flag`/`block`; `handoff.md` "Phase status: pass                                                                                                                                   | flag | block". |

## 4. Open Questions / Risks (Lane-specific)

1. **`handoff` as profile vs. kind is a recorded decision, not a settled fact.** Evidence supports merge (it has contract structure and appears as a schema row family `route_handoffs` and a dispatch `boundary.kind: capability_handoff`), but `handoff.md` is authored by `invoke` as a first-class artifact. Risk: under-modeling loses invoke's distinct activation/output gates. Needs a decision-gate (cited as required next step in `DOMAINSPEC-LANGUAGE-HANDOFF.md` route step 3).

2. **`example` vs `iteration` overlap for validation reports.** `CRAFT-INTERFACE-VALIDATION.md` is simultaneously a fixture verdict and a change record. If both `example.verdict` and `iteration.validations_run` can hold verdicts, validation authority could fork. Need a rule for where the authoritative verdict lives.

3. **Two schema dialects, one kind.** `dispatch.schema.yml` is JSON-Schema 2020-12; Craft schemas use a bespoke `row_families`/`enum_ref`/`references_any` dialect with `extends`. The `schema` kind must abstract over both or pick one canonical surface — this collides with the still-open YAML-vs-DSL-vs-dual decision (`CONTEXT-PACK.md` "Recommended First Refinement"). Risk of premature syntax lock-in (handoff non-goal).

4. **`authority` field shape is uneven.** `dispatch.schema.yml` has a structured `authority_map`; Craft uses `owner_surface` + `owner_context_id` + `source_of_truth_policy`. Canonicalizing `authority` requires choosing a single substructure that covers lifecycle/validation/promotion/evidence ownership without overfitting to either.

5. **`id` uniqueness and namespacing across kinds.** Craft ids are prefix-typed (`CTX-`, `DEF-`, `HND-`, `RCT-`) and dispatch ids are free strings. The canonical `id` field needs a cross-kind uniqueness + reference-resolution rule, since `references`/`references_any` must resolve across documents and kinds.

6. **Lifecycle (`status`) enum is not yet unified.** Members are scattered: `candidate`/`candidate-local`/`active-local`/`superseded`/`promoted-by-owner` (Craft definitions), `planned`/`active`/`completed`/`blocked`/`superseded` (handoffs), `implemented` (invoke handoff doc), plus the handoff's target list (draft/refined/gated/implemented/validated/promoted/blocked). One canonical lifecycle enum must be reconciled — this is its own lane but blocks the required `status` field here.
