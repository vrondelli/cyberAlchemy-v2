## Objective And Central Decision

The result must select one authority-correct topology or return a typed owner
blocker. At minimum it compares:

1. one cross-project canonical registry;
2. two independent canonical registries with a derived crosswalk; and
3. one shared record contract with owner-specific canonical catalogs and

without letting CyberAlchemy v2 own DomainSpec semantics or letting DomainSpec
redefine CyberAlchemy authority. It is a hypothesis for attack, not a decision.

The result must keep these objects non-collapsed: proof claim, obligation,
predicate, binding, fixture, evaluator, evidence, execution receipt, discharge
status, negative control, attestation, canonical source, derived projection,
authority kind, and promotion decision.

## Claim And Authority Boundary

The current EVF candidate demonstrates useful record shapes and exact lineage,
but untracked schema-shaped artifacts are not canonical, and planned runtime
sources are not executed proof. CyberAlchemy v2 can govern authority kinds,
promotion, and claim ceilings without owning DomainSpec domain meaning.
Hashes can bind bytes; receipts can report execution; attestations can protect
integrity or identity at their demonstrated tier. None of those facts alone
establish semantic truth or promotion.

No research agent may edit DomainSpec v2, CyberAlchemy v2, public Arcanum,
Inventory, telemetry, Craft, Git, or implementation sources. Only the writer
may create `findings.md` after evidence closure.
