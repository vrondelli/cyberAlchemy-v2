### Ordinary-language contribution boundary

The paper may propose an architecture in which:

1. definitions state admitted intent;
2. a rule emits an obligation;
3. a generator may create a check artifact from that obligation;
4. a runner may execute that check;
5. the observed result records what happened for those exact inputs.

These are distinct states. An emitted obligation is not an executed check. A generated check is not a passing result. A passing result is not runtime correctness.

The only bounded C2-style observation supported by the admitted execution evidence is:
