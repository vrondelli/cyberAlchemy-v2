# DomainSpec v2 Paper Evidence Refresh Strategy

This is an unregistered, non-authority proposal. It does not authorize research
execution, edit or deploy the public paper, disclose private source material,
promote DomainSpec v2, or establish release, production, openness, or proof.

## P1 Trigger Decision

Governed multi-agent dispatch is required. All four P1 triggers fire:

- synthesis: the result must reconcile semantic authority, executed behavior,
  historical evidence, related work, and publishable claims;
- context protection: the source census and verbatim returns would swamp the
  parent context;
- context isolation: authority, implementation, public-boundary, skeptical,
  writing, and audit roles must not collapse into one voice;
- parallelism: the three evidence lanes and three independent attacks are
  separable and materially useful in parallel.

## Inventory Preflight

The root machine Inventory passed lookup readiness with zero failures and 146

canonical meaning, observed execution, historical evidence, and public-safe
claims. Live owner sources must refresh counts, status, receipts, negative
controls, citations, and source identities before any prose change.

## Lanes And Subagents

- Turing, Alan verifies validators, engines, fixtures, commands, receipts,

### Synthesis

### Skeptical Review

- Popper, Karl applies the falsifiable non-vacuity and negative-control gate.
- Quine, Willard Van Orman applies the definitional soundness and authority

Each reviewer owns one independent attack record and typed repair requests.

### Findings And Final Audit

- Senge, Peter may write only `findings.md`, and only after the research owner
  returns exact-binding evidence closure.
- Brandenburg, Martin is the sole final approver. The auditor reads only the
  completed findings package and may accept it, request one bounded writer
  repair, or stop the dispatch.

## Dependency Flow

`explorers -> synthesizer <-> reviewers -> writer -> auditor`

The three explorers fan out and join. The synthesizer/reviewer zig-zag is capped
at one repair loop. Missing source bytes, provenance, selectors, anchors, or
exact pointers route from synthesis back to the eligible explorer, also capped
at one loop; they never route to the writer. The auditor has one bounded
feedback edge to the writer. All joins require every declared return.

## Outputs And Handoff

The dispatch preserves verbatim explorer evidence in `research.md`, produces an
exact-binding closure package, then produces auditor-reviewed `findings.md` and
receipts under this working folder. The research writer does not edit the paper.
Only after final audit may the parent apply the accepted revision contract to
`arcanum/docs/paper.html` and run source, HTML, browser, link, and deployment-
configuration validation. Deployment or publication remains a separate effect.

## Public And Claim Boundary

Private evidence may inform the private research package, but private paths,
prose, test-derivation rules, and implementation details cannot enter public
Arcanum. DomainSpec v2 and Saturn must not be described as shipped. Plans,
candidate models, Inventory pages, green aggregate commands, and validated
receipts do not by themselves prove implementation, promotion, publication,
release, deployment, production readiness, generality, or formal correctness.
