## Net verdict

Adopt-conservative, none of the fifteen candidates clears the bar to **ADOPT-NOW**: every
rejected tool trades domainspec-v2's load-bearing **byte-stable determinism** or a
**non-collapse rule** for capability the repo has explicitly scoped out, and two whole areas
(SMT and DSL) were already frozen-rejected internally on 2026-07-02. The single **PILOT** is
narrow and consensual — fast-check as a pinned-seed, dev-time parser round-trip that never
touches the emit path — which both finders independently endorse. The one honest *later*
gap is the cross-graph / cross-rule layer (edge cycles, reachability a node-local check
provably can't state); note the adjudication that **no advocate tool actually fills it** —
the advocate pitched SMT for the formal cell, but the only admitted solver is **Datalog/Cedar,
cross-graph-only, quarantined, not SMT, and not yet wired by design** (`findings.md:36,42-43,73-74`),
so that remains a future owned-pattern task-session rather than an OSS adoption this lane can bank today.
