# Research

Status: active boundary
Owner: CyberAlchemy v2 research route

Research turns source cohorts into candidate understanding. It does not mutate
definitions, constitutions, specs, validators, runtime contracts, or
implementation without a promotion gate.

Research towers should cite [../SOURCE-MAP.md](../SOURCE-MAP.md), keep source
paths visible, and record borrow, analogy, block, and promotion-candidate
decisions.

## Canonical Research Records

The [Research Document Type](RESEARCH-DOCUMENT-TYPE.md) defines the private

and points to separate findings; it does not make its synthesis authoritative.
