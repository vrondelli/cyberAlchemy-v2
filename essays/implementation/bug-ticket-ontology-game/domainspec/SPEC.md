## Capability slice

The player freely revisits Incident Control, the Policy Archive, and the
Transfer Annex, operates three subsystem machines to assemble a Replay Address,
and confirms an event-word rain across a browser reload before transforming
discovered artifacts into a bounded construction chain. The UI must make unsupported
shortcuts playable but visibly unsafe: a ticket is not a rule, a graph is not
an owner decision, and an unresolved policy question must remain residue.

The interaction contract includes point-and-click inspection, keyboard
activation, freely revisitable places with gated internal machinery, cross-place
inventory use, masked machine witnesses, typed Replay Address entry, seeded
word-rain capture, browser-reload confirmation, drag-and-drop artifact use,
environmental puzzles, progressive hints, local autosave, and a final
evidence-chain lock. Inspectable objects are discoverable
through the illustrated environment rather than an always-visible station menu.

## Authority behavior

The browser may simulate an owner decision as a narrative event. Neither its
score, graph, validator, nor generated artifacts can perform the real decision
to adopt this Product Capability or any CyberAlchemy definition.
